LINKS: every row in the input has a reference like [H12], [S40], [L7], [F2]; link it as href="#H12" and a script swaps in the real URL. Never type a filing URL yourself. For an extras item, use the URL shown after it in angle brackets.

Written for you by script, so don't write them: the OGE Ethics Filings section (inserted first), and a trade table for every stock-trade report (appended under your disclosures section: ticker, company, type, date, amount, and a Late? column). LDA Registrations & Activity: a summary with the day's counts by filing type, then a `table` block (columns Firm | Client | Issue Area(s) | What's Being Lobbied) for every new registration and termination, and for the activity reports that matter: former government officials lobbying (COVERED POSITIONS), foreign entities, conviction disclosures, big money, first-time or unusual clients, or anything tied to a live fight in Washington. === WRITING RULES ===
Every section needs its own headline and a short plain-English summary before any list or table. If items are mundane, say so plainly ("17 routine registrations, nothing notable").
Never chain more than four or five similar items into one paragraph; each gets its own list item.
Inline formatting inside text you write may use `<strong>`, `<a href="...">` and `<em>` only.
No `leads` array and no advice to the reader ("check whether...", "ask the agency..."). Fold anything a lead would say into the item as a fact ("the sale came 40 days after the divestiture deadline", "this is the firm's first-ever lobbying registration").
Write people's and committees' full names so the automatic "Your Beat" box (built from data/watchlist.json) can match them.
