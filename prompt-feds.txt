=== WRITING RULES (apply everywhere) ===
EVERY section needs its own headline + 1-3 sentences of plain-English summary before any list/table — never open with a raw list. The headline states the single most important/notable fact in that section, not a bare label. If a section's items are individually mundane, say so plainly ("17 routine registrations, nothing notable") rather than forcing false significance.
CAPS ARE DEFAULTS, NOT CEILINGS (changed 2026-10-11): every item limit in this prompt ("at most 5 cards", "at most 10 items", "pick 3-6", the word targets) is the size of a normal day. When a day genuinely has more newsworthy items than a cap allows, go past it: write every item that clears the bar in its normal format (card, paragraph or one-line list item) instead of pushing it down into a count or a linked-names-only note. Never pad toward a cap on a quiet day.
