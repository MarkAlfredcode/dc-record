Top Story
Two Texas Republicans still have no 2025 annual financial disclosure on file, 60 days past the final extension
Rep. Michael T. McCaul and Rep. Dan Crenshaw both filed extensions but have posted no CY2025 annual report.

The Deadline Watch check of every sitting member against the Clerk of the House index and Senate eFD found two members with no calendar-year 2025 annual financial disclosure on file: Rep. Michael T. McCaul (R-TX-10) and Rep. Dan Crenshaw (R-TX-2). McCaul filed an extension on 4/29/2026 and Crenshaw on 6/4/2026. The final extension date for both was 8/13/2026, so each is now 60 days past it.

The check excludes members sworn in after Nov. 1 of the report year. The tracker also notes that a filing under an unexpected spelling of a name can read as missing, so the finding rests on the index search.

Separately, the Ethics Agreement Tracker lists seven Senate-confirmed officials with no posted certification of ethics-agreement compliance more than 30 days past deadline. The longest is Michelle White Bowman, Federal Reserve Governor and Vice Chairman for Supervision, confirmed 6/4/2025 with certification due 9/2/2025, 405 days ago. No new OGE filings were identified in this window, and there are no new FARA items or financial disclosure filings.
