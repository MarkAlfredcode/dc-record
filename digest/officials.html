Rep. Dan Crenshaw still has no 2025 annual financial disclosure on file, 68 days past his final extension
The House member's last extension expired August 13, and no report appears in the Clerk of the House index or Senate eFD.

Rep. Dan Crenshaw (R-TX-2) filed an extension on June 4, 2026. The final extension date was August 13, 2026, so as of today the calendar-year 2025 annual report is 68 days overdue.
