Top Story
Former White House cabinet-secretary official Matthew Flynn is listed on two new Steptoe manufacturing registrations
Nothing bigger came in today: no FARA filings, no presidential trades and no Senate filings were available.

Steptoe LLP registered to lobby for two new clients, Deterrence Defense, Inc. and Machina Labs, Inc., on "issues relating to domestic manufacturing." Both registrations list Matthew Flynn as a covered former official. His listed positions include member of the Homeland Security Advisory Council, deputy assistant secretary of defense, and deputy assistant to the president and acting White House cabinet secretary. The filing text is cut off after that, so the full list is not shown here.

Neither filing logs a contact with the President or a cabinet member. They are registrations that put a former White House official on the lobbying team for two manufacturing clients.

The rest of the day is routine. The OGE filing portal could not be reached, so no OGE filings were reviewed. One House periodic transaction report (Rep. Ann Wagner) was filed.
