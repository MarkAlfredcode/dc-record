OGE Ethics Filings
Four Presidential Nominees File Ethics Agreements with Divestitures and Employment Resignations

Four nominees for positions at federal agencies submitted ethics agreements committing to divest financial holdings or resign from prior employment to address potential conflicts of interest. Additionally, eight routine filings were submitted by nominees across State Department, Treasury, Justice, and Education positions.

  • Francis J. Browne Jr., Under Secretary for Domestic Finance at the Department of the Treasury, filed an Ethics Agreement committing to divest his interests in SPDR Gold MiniShares Trust (GLDM), iShares Bitcoin Trust ETF (IBIT), iShares Silver Trust (SLV), iShares Gold Trust (IAU), and SPDR Gold Shares (GLD) within 90 days of confirmation.
  • Dino LaVerghetta, nominee for Ambassador to the State of Kuwait at the Department of State, filed an Ethics Agreement committing to resign from his position with Sidley Austin LLP and to refrain from participating in matters involving the firm or his former clients for one year after resignation.
  • Scott E. Krueger, nominee for U.S. Marshal for the Northern District of Indiana at the Department of Justice, filed an Ethics Agreement committing to resign from his positions with the Indiana State Police and Alliance Security, Inc., with one-year post-resignation recusal periods.
  • Mark Priebe, nominee for Inspector General at the Department of Education, filed an Ethics Agreement committing to resign from positions with five sports officials associations, including the Rappahannock Valley Football Officials Association and Fredericksburg Volleyball Officials Association.
