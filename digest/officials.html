Top Story
Former Trump White House liaison chief registers to lobby for a company tied to Greece's finance ministry
Capitol Counsel LLC's new registration for PPC North America Corporation lists two Greek government-linked foreign entities and a former Trump White House official.

Capitol Counsel LLC registered to lobby for PPC North America Corporation, an energy company, on U.S. energy security, critical infrastructure, digital infrastructure investment and global competitiveness. The registration lists two foreign entities: the Hellenic Corporation of Assets and Participations S.A. and the Hellenic Republic (Minister of Economy and Finance). Its issue areas are Energy/Nuclear, Foreign Relations and Utilities, and the stated lobbying covers the Executive Branch and Congress. Registration.

The filing's covered positions include George Sifakis, listed as Assistant to the President of the United States and Director of the Office of Public Liaison (President Trump, 2017), and Martin Gold, listed as Floor Advisor and Counsel to Senate Majority Leader Bill Frist.

The other contact-related filing today is a quarterly LDA report from the National Small Business Association naming two cabinet-level officials (see the Contacts section). Nothing else in the input shows a direct communication with the President, the Vice President or a cabinet member. The Office of Government Ethics published no new filings, and no new FARA documents or House or Senate disclosures came in.
