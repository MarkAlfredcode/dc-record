Top Story
FBI Director Kash Patel amends his stock report to add a six-figure MicroStrategy purchase he left off
The amendment, delivered to Mark Alfred's OGE inbox on Nov. 5, discloses a purchase about 186 days after it happened, against the STOCK Act's 45-day limit.

A Justice Department cover letter dated May 28, 2026 forwards a May 26 request from Kashyap P. Patel, Director of the Federal Bureau of Investigation, to amend his periodic transaction report (278-T). The original was signed December 4, 2025 and certified by the U.S. Office of Government Ethics on December 30, 2025. The amendment adds a purchase of MicroStrategy, Inc (MSTR) on November 21, 2025, in the $100,001-$250,000 range. Patel says it was inadvertently omitted because of a miscommunication.

The original report lists only one sale: ON Semiconductor Corporation (ON) on October 6, 2025, for $50,001-$100,000. The MicroStrategy purchase was not reported until the May 26, 2026 amendment, about 186 days after the trade. That is a late disclosure. The amendment came roughly six months after OGE certified the original. The report also notes that Patel received a 30-day filing extension.

The document arrived Nov. 5 at oge@markalfred.news in answer to an automatically filed OGE Form 201 request. Also delivered the same day was a certificate of divestiture for Todd Blanche (file OGE-2026-183-CDIV); the readable text did not give his position.
