  • Rep. Robert E. Latta (R-OH-5) filed 6 periodic transaction reports in the 12 months from 7/25/2025 to 7/25/2026, and none in the 91 days since the last, on 7/24/2026

House members who filed 6 or more periodic transaction reports (STOCK Act trade disclosures) in the year before the last 90 days and none since, per the House Clerk's disclosure index. Silence doesn't say why: the member may have sold off holdings, moved assets into a blind trust or index fund, been covered by a new law, or just not traded -- and trades are reportable up to 45 days late, so the latest ones may not be filed yet. House members only; the Senate's e-filing search isn't covered.
