Former Vice President adviser registers to lobby for a company with a foreign parent entity
Continental Strategy's new registration for Future Pipe Industries lists a former Special Advisor to the Vice President as a covered position.

Continental Strategy, LLC registered to lobby for Future Pipe Industries LLC, a fiberglass pipe manufacturer, on U.S. composite pipe manufacturing and energy infrastructure. The issue areas are budget and appropriations, clean air and water, energy and nuclear, fuel, gas and oil, manufacturing, and trade. Registration.

The filing lists Wessam Hassanein, whose covered position is "Special Advisor to the Vice President," and names Future Group Holdings SA SPF as a foreign entity. The input gives no dates for his government service. Continental Strategy filed a second registration the same day, for Treaty Oak Clean Energy LLC, which lists no covered positions or foreign entities. Registration.
