Sen. Jim Justice Reports Partial Sale of Greenbrier Hotel Stock Valued Over $50 Million
A Senate transaction report lists a single August 14 sale in the form's highest value bracket.

Sen. James Conley Justice II filed a Periodic Transaction Report on September 28, 2026 listing one transaction: a partial sale on August 14, 2026 of non-public stock in Greenbrier Hotel Corporation and Affiliates, with the owner marked "Self" and the amount "Over $50,000,000."

The filing gives no buyer, price or explanation, and the report says only that the sale was partial. The Senate form's amount ranges are open-ended at the top, so the actual value cannot be determined from the filing.

Personal Financial Disclosures & Stock Trades
Sen. Jim Justice Reports Partial Sale of Greenbrier Hotel Stock; Sen. Richard Blumenthal Files 33 Spousal Partnership Trades

Two Senate Periodic Transaction Reports and four House filings surfaced since the last edition. Both Senate reports were read in full; the House filings are three new candidate reports and one amendment, none of them trade reports. The 30-filing read cap was not reached, and no House Periodic Transaction Reports appeared in the window.

Senate Periodic Transaction Reports

  • Sen. James Conley Justice II filed a Periodic Transaction Report on September 28, 2026 with a single line: a partial sale on August 14, 2026 of non-public stock in Greenbrier Hotel Corporation and Affiliates (White Sulphur Springs, West Virginia), owner listed as "Self," in the form's top bracket of over $50,000,000. No ticker applies, so there is no trade table.
  • Sen. Richard Blumenthal filed a Periodic Transaction Report with 33 line items, all listing the owner as spouse. They are purchases and partial sales between August 19 and September 11, 2026 in Not Fade Away LLC, Days Between LLC and MH Built to Last LLC, described as a hedge fund and partnerships and categorized "Other"; each line names an underlying family LLC such as Peter L. Malkin Family 2000 LLC. The largest lines are $250,001–$500,000 (purchases on August 27 and September 11, and a partial sale on August 19). None involves a publicly traded ticker, so no trade table.
