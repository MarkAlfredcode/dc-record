Top Story
Lobbying firm reports pushing White House and Justice Department on clemency for a client in a securities dispute
A third-quarter Lobbying Disclosure Act report from Rulon & White Governance Strategies lists clemency work for Veriteaseum alongside Securities and Exchange Commission negotiations.

A 3rd Quarter report filed by Rulon & White Governance Strategies for its client Veritaseum Inc. describes the issue as: "Securities Exchange Commission Emmet and Murphy Negotiations U.S Senate Tim Scott Department of Justice Department of Clemency White House Counsel Alice Johnson Clemency for Reginald Middleton." The lobbyist listed is Todd White.

The text is quoted as filed. It names the White House Counsel, the Department of Justice and Alice Johnson in connection with clemency for Reginald Middleton, and it puts that work in the same entry as the securities negotiations. The report does not say whether any contact took place or what the outcome was.

The Pardon Lobbying section below carries the filing link. No other filing in today's window showed a communication with the President, the Vice President or a cabinet member. No new House financial disclosures turned up, and the Senate disclosure check failed, so Senate coverage is incomplete.
