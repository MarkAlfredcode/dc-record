Top Story
New FARA registrant Starboard LLC signs on to represent a group acting for the State of Israel
Olympic Media LLC, doing business as Starboard LLC, registered Oct. 28 with LDRS Group LLC on behalf of the State of Israel as its foreign principal.

Olympic Media LLC, doing business as Starboard LLC (registration #7788), registered under the Foreign Agents Registration Act on October 28, 2026. Its foreign principal is LDRS Group LLC, listed as acting on behalf of the State of Israel (registration record).

In the Exhibit AB, the registrant describes the foreign principal's business as advertising and checks that it is a foreign or domestic organization, not a foreign government. It answers "No" to every question on whether the principal is supervised, owned, directed, controlled, financed or subsidized by a foreign government or party (Exhibit AB). The OCR text available does not show contract dates or fees.

The registration statement lists Ryan Patrick Coyne as chief executive and 100 percent owner. The company was organized in Richmond, Virginia, on January 20, 2017, and has an Alexandria, Virginia office. Will Ambruzs is listed as chief operating officer and Michael Reid as chief financial officer. Coyne also filed a short-form registration as an individual agent (registration statement, short-form).

No filing in this window shows a communication with the President, Vice President or a cabinet official, and no financial disclosures were available to weigh against it.

  • DGA Group Government Relations LLC (#7321) added a new foreign principal, the Ministerio de la Presidencia de la Republica Dominicana (Dominican Republic), under a contract signed October 16, 2026 (amendment). The Exhibit AB says the firm will research and advocate for human rights best practices and communications strategies, support the ministry's outreach to U.S. government officials, thought leaders and the media, and advise on combating human trafficking and smuggling. The firm may also work with other Dominican ministries, including the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, and the activities may include lobbying and public relations (Exhibit AB). The OCR text shown does not give fees.
