Top Story
Rep. Darrell Issa reports buying and selling the same Treasury note within six days, each leg in the top disclosure range
No cabinet-level contact or foreign-agent news came in today; the largest single disclosure is a House periodic transaction report.

Rep. Darrell Issa (R-CA) filed a periodic transaction report on October 22 showing two transactions in a U.S. Treasury 3.375% note due 09/15/28: a purchase on October 8 and a sale on October 14. Each is reported at $5,000,001 to $25,000,000, the highest range on the form. Both were disclosed within the 45-day STOCK Act window, so neither is a late disclosure. The filing parses as two lines and shows no other trades.

Also in today's House filings, Rep. Cleo Fields (D-LA) reported four purchases of $1,001 to $15,000 each: three of NVIDIA Corporation on October 1 and one of Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Company on October 9. The filing was made within the deadline.

The Office of Government Ethics reported nothing notable. No FARA filings, presidential or vice-presidential 278-Ts, or pardon-lobbying filings were new in this window.
