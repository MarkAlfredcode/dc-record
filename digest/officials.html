Top Story
Mercury Public Affairs posts seven second-quarter lobbying terminations in a single batch
The terminations all list a June 30, 2026 end date, about four months before they appeared in the LDA database.

Mercury Public Affairs, LLC had seven 2nd Quarter terminations posted since November 1, each with a termination date of June 30, 2026. They cover Better Schools for a Better Future ($70,000), CADA Energy, Inc ($40,000), Elanco US Inc. ($60,000), Grup Immobiliari Cierco ($100,000), Jeffer Mangels Butler & Mitchell LLP on behalf of Dateline Resources Limited ($40,000), Keystone Strategic Advisors on behalf of Batric Radunovic ($120,000) and SEMI Global ($30,000). Together they report $460,000 in income.

Quarterly lobbying reports are due 20 days after the quarter ends, so these filings appeared well after the deadline for a June 30 termination. The input does not show original filing dates, so the dates when they were first submitted are not confirmed here. The filings include former Sen. David Vitter as a covered position on the education client and former Rep. Patrick Costello (a congressman from 2005 to 2008, per the filing) on the rare earth elements client.
