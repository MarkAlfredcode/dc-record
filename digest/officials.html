Top Story
GRV Strategies amends its Qatar filing to add activity it left out of its last supplemental statement
The Washington firm told the Justice Department it did additional work for Qatar's International Media Office that was not reported for the period ending March 31, 2026.

GRV Strategies LLC (registration #7458) filed an amendment on October 27, 2026, stamped received by the National Security Division's FARA Registration Unit at 12:36 p.m. The amendment says it reports "additional activities on behalf of the International Media office of State of Qatar that was previously not reported on Supplemental Statement ending 3/31/2026 in Item 12." Item 12 is the section on political activity for a foreign principal.

The appendix amends the Item 12 response for the six-month period ending March 31, 2026. It describes the work as media communications "to promote positive relations with the State of Qatar." The amendment was signed October 27 by Garrett Ventry, almost seven months after that period closed. The OCR text available to this edition cuts off before the itemized activities, so the specific outreach is not described here. GRV Strategies also filed a separate supplemental statement the same day, linked below.

No contact between GRV Strategies and the President, Vice President or a cabinet official turned up in today's search of FARA filings.
