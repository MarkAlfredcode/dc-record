Top Story

White House publishes twin actions tightening H-1B visa oversight and restricting entry of nonimmigrant workers

Two presidential documents landed in the Federal Register on the same day, but neither carries a public abstract — only the titles and the underlying text are on the record so far.

The Executive Office of the President published two documents in the Federal Register on September 23, 2026: "Enhancing Program Integrity and Interagency Coordination in the Administration of the H-1B Nonimmigrant Visa Program" and "Restriction on Entry of Certain Nonimmigrant Workers." Both are classified by the Federal Register as presidential documents rather than agency rules, meaning they originate directly from the White House rather than a department or independent agency.

Neither filing includes an abstract in the Federal Register's metadata, so this newsletter cannot characterize their substantive requirements beyond what the titles state: one is framed around coordinating federal agencies' administration and integrity checks on the H-1B specialty-occupation visa program, and the other around restricting entry for an unspecified category of nonimmigrant workers. Readers who need the operative text — effective dates, which visa categories or countries are covered, and any exceptions — should consult the full documents at the links below.

Published together on the same day from the same source, the pairing suggests a coordinated policy push touching a visa category widely used by federal contractors and technology firms, though that inference rests on the titles alone and not on confirmed text.

Elsewhere, it was a quiet day for this newsletter's other beats: no new OMB apportionments or flagged OGE ethics filings this week, no new $2 million-plus contract awards surfaced in USAspending's trailing window, and SAM.gov's opportunity search API was throttled for the full reporting window (details below).

The Federal Register

White House publishes two H-1B-related presidential documents; FTC moves to eliminate its post-employment 'revolving door' clearance rule

Two same-day presidential documents on H-1B visas and nonimmigrant worker entry topped a Federal Register window that otherwise consisted mostly of routine agency rules — sanctions general-license publications, Coast Guard safety zones, and technical corrections. The Federal Trade Commission also finalized changes to its own rules of practice, including eliminating a rule that required post-employment ethics clearance for departing staff.

Presidential documents

"Enhancing Program Integrity and Interagency Coordination in the Administration of the H-1B Nonimmigrant Visa Program" was published September 23, 2026, credited to the Executive Office of the President. The Federal Register listing carries no abstract, so only the title is confirmed; it points toward new coordination requirements among federal agencies overseeing the H-1B program. Read the document.

A companion filing, "Restriction on Entry of Certain Nonimmigrant Workers," was published the same day, also without an abstract. The title alone indicates new entry restrictions for a nonimmigrant worker category; which visa classes or countries are affected is not established by the metadata available here. Read the document.
