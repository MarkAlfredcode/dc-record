Presidential Actions Widen the Canada Tariff Dispute

Five separate Executive Office of the President documents published September 14 escalate an ongoing tariff fight with Canada across three product categories. Two “modify the scope” of existing additional duties (on motor vehicles and on alcoholic beverages), while three others go further and exclude certain Canadian products from importation into the United States entirely (on motor vehicles, dairy, and alcoholic beverages again) — all framed as a response to “continued Canadian discrimination against the commerce of the United States.” None of the five carry a Federal Register abstract, so the specific tariff rates, HS codes, or exempted categories are only in the full text at each link below, not summarized in the docket entry.

  • Motor vehicles — Modifying the Scope of Products of Canada Subject to the Additional Duties.
  • Alcoholic beverages — Modifying the Scope of Products of Canada Subject to the Additional Duties.
  • Motor vehicles (exclusion) — Excluding Certain Canadian Products From Importation.
  • Dairy (exclusion) — Excluding Certain Canadian Products From Importation.
  • Alcoholic beverages (exclusion) — Excluding Certain Canadian Products From Importation.

Separately, the Executive Office of the President also published a proclamation for Patriot Day 2026, marking the 25th anniversary of the September 11 terrorist attacks. Read the proclamation.

OPM's Federal Workforce Rules

The Office of Personnel Management published two workforce-related actions the same day. One is a substantive correction: an amendatory instruction in OPM's August 3 reduction-in-force overhaul “inadvertently resulted in the removal of two paragraphs from the definition of ‘displaced’” in the Career Transition Assistance Plan regulations, which this document restores. The other is an interim rule reshaping how agencies share hiring certificates — letting one appointing authority share a competitive or excepted-service hiring certificate with other agencies, and enabling OPM-led, multi-agency hiring actions for occupations common across government.

  • Reduction in Force; Correction — restores language dropped from the definition of "displaced" in OPM's reduction-in-force rule. Federal Register.
  • Shared Certificates and Pooled Hiring Actions — interim rule letting agencies share competitive and excepted-service hiring certificates and use OPM-led hiring actions across agencies. Federal Register.
