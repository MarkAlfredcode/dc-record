Top Story
Former Federal Maritime Commission chairman registers to lobby on Venezuela property repatriation
Thorn Run Partners' new client wants seized American property in Venezuela returned, and one of the two lobbyists assigned to the account just left the federal panel that regulates ocean shipping to and from the country.

Thorn Run Partners registered on September 11 to lobby for Vientos del Caribe, described in the filing as a hotel and resort company, on "repatriation of seized American property in Venezuela." The registration lists two lobbyists: Louis Sola and Andres Kardonski.

Sola's disclosed covered position is "Chairman / Commissioner, Federal Maritime Commission" — the independent federal agency that regulates U.S. international ocean shipping, including trade routes touching Venezuela. Kardonski's covered positions include communications adviser to Sen. Eric Schmitt and communications director/press secretary to Rep. Juan Ciscomani, plus earlier stints as a press assistant/intern for Rep. Carlos Gimenez and an internship at the Federal Maritime Commission itself.

The filing does not specify which seized properties are at issue or provide further detail on Vientos del Caribe's ownership or business activities beyond the "Hotel and Resort" description in the client field. No lobbying activity reports or termination notices for this account have posted yet.
