A quiet stretch: no new lobbying registrations, no FARA activity
The one disclosure of note was a K Street lobbyist's mid-year report of personal campaign contributions to three members of Congress.

Nothing significant hit the disclosure pipeline in this window. The Senate Office of Public Records logged zero new Lobbying Disclosure Act registrations (Form LD-1), and DOJ's FARA e-file system recorded zero new or terminated foreign-agent registrants — a lull, not necessarily a trend, given normal day-to-day swings in filing volume.

Only three other Lobbying Disclosure Act filings posted in the window: two second-quarter activity reports and one registration termination, all routine (details below).

The most notable filing was a Lobbying Disclosure Act mid-year campaign-contribution report (LD-203) from Triangle Associates, Inc. lobbyist Philip Andrew Blalock, disclosing $6,500 in personal contributions made across the first half of 2026 to three sitting members of Congress: Rep. Josh Riley, Rep. David Rouzer, and Rep. Eugene Vindman.

No new registrations; a lobbyist's contributions are the day's disclosure of note

No new lobbying registrations (Form LD-1) were filed in this window. Three other Lobbying Disclosure Act filings posted — two routine second-quarter activity reports and one termination — none tied to legislation or members otherwise in today's newsletter.

Separately, three LD-203 lobbyist campaign-contribution reports posted; two disclosed no contributions. The third, from Triangle Associates, Inc., itemized five personal contributions from lobbyist Philip Andrew Blalock to three House members.

  • First Amendment Partners LLC, on behalf of client American Kratom Association — second-quarter activity report covering kratom regulation and consumer-safety issues (Consumer Issues/Safety/Products; Accounting). View filing.
  • First Amendment Partners LLC, on behalf of client Meltsner Strategies, LLC on behalf of Institute for Responsive Government Action — second-quarter activity report covering advocacy for federal election-infrastructure funding under Title I, Section 101 of the Help America Vote Act (Budget/Appropriations; Government Issues). View filing.
  • Melony Edwards, on behalf of client American Meat Producers Association — registration termination; prior activity covered the Save Our Bacon Act (H.R. 4673) and the Food Security and Farm Protection Act (S. 1326), plus general agricultural-practices issues (Agriculture). View filing.
