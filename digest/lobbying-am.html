Top Story
Former GOP Rep. Barbara Comstock's lobbyist contributions flow mostly to Democrats in 2026 races
A Baker Donelson lobbyist and former Virginia congresswoman disclosed $9,550 in personal campaign contributions to a dozen 2026 Senate, House, and governor candidates, most of them Democrats.

Barbara Comstock, a lobbyist at Baker Donelson Bearman Caldwell & Berkowitz and a former Republican member of Congress from Virginia, disclosed $9,550 in personal campaign contributions covering January through June 2026 in an LD-203 mid-year report posted to LDA.gov on September 22.

The recipients lean heavily Democratic and span competitive 2026 contests: $1,000 to Roy Cooper for North Carolina Senate, $500 to Seth Moulton for Massachusetts Senate, $2,500 across two contributions to Elaine Luria for Congress in Virginia, $200 to James Talarico for Senate in Texas, $500 to Women Count (backing Jocelyn Benson's Michigan governor bid), $500 to Ghazala Hashmi for Lt. Governor in Virginia, $500 to Friends of Janelle Stelson in Pennsylvania, $250 across three contributions to Chris Pappas for Senate in New Hampshire, $100 to Josh Shapiro for Governor, and $1,000 to a committee called George Conway for Congress.

She also gave to two Republicans in the same period: $2,000 across two contributions to Kelly Ayotte for Governor in New Hampshire and $500 to Kyle for Alabama Senate, backing Kyle Sweetser.

K Street
Cozen O'Connor logs eight new health care clients in a single day

Twenty new federal lobbying registrations posted overnight. Cozen O'Connor Public Strategies alone accounted for eight of them, all health care- and Medicaid-focused clients ranging from children's hospitals to a behavioral health crisis provider. Other new business includes Ocean Aero, an autonomous underwater vehicle maker retaining McKinnon Group, LLC, and Boehringer Ingelheim USA Corporation, a pharmaceutical company retaining Manatt, Phelps, and Phillips.

Four registrants disclosed actual lobbyist campaign contributions for the period (most LD-203 filers report none). Beyond Baker Donelson's Barbara Comstock (see Top Story), Birch, Horton, Bittner & Cherot lobbyist Jon DeVore reported six contributions to Alaska and Wisconsin federal candidates; Chamber of Commerce of the U.S.A. lobbyist John Drake reported two small contributions; and Ericsson Inc. lobbyist Matthew Rhoades reported one $250 contribution.
