Top Story
Eight Space and Defense-Tech Firms Swap K Street Shops in a Single Day
Velocity Government Relations dropped 11 clients over the summer; Strategic Marketing Innovations picked up eight of the exact same companies, with both sets of filings posted to the public record the same day.

Velocity Government Relations, LLC filed third-quarter termination notices with the Senate Office of Public Records for 11 clients — effective July 31 or August 31, 2026 — all posted to the public database on September 16, 2026. The same day, Strategic Marketing Innovations, a separate Washington, D.C. lobbying firm, filed brand-new registrations for eight of those exact same companies, all space and defense-technology firms: Decision Sciences Incorporated (DSI), Earth Observant Inc., Innovative Rocket Technologies, Inc. (iRocket), Exquadrum, NextGen Aeronautics, SAVIT Corporation, TRL11 and Umbra Lab, Inc.

The new lobbying-activity descriptions track closely with what Velocity had been doing for these clients: propulsion, solid rocket motors and munitions production for Exquadrum and iRocket; national-security space and ISR work for TRL11 and Umbra Lab; defense-industrial-base and supply-chain issues for DSI and SAVIT. Three other companies Velocity dropped the same day — Ad Astra Rocket Company, L3Harris Technologies, Inc. and Science Applications International Corporation — do not appear among Strategic Marketing Innovations' new filings or any other registrant's filings posted in this window, so it isn't clear from the public record whether they moved to another firm, brought the work in-house, or simply lapsed.

Neither filing set states a reason for the switch — LDA registrations don't require one. But the pattern is unusual enough to flag: same day, same eight-client roster, closely matching subject matter, in a lobbying niche (space and defense-industrial-base policy) that has been drawing growing K Street attention.

K Street
28 New Lobbying Registrations, Led by Apple, AstraZeneca and a Defense-Tech Bloc

The Senate Office of Public Records posted 28 new lobbying registrations in this window, plus 46 additional quarterly reports, terminations and amendments. Eight of the new registrations are the defense/space-technology clients covered in today's Top Story, above; the rest span finance, health care, technology, energy and food policy.

Notable new business: Apple Inc. hired Bellwether Government Affairs LLC for tax, trade and technology policy; AstraZeneca Pharmaceuticals LP hired Tholos Government Relations to work pharmaceutical, intellectual-property and trade issues; and Block, Inc., the payments company behind Cash App, hired BGR Government Affairs on financial-services and mobile-payments issues. Sargento Foods Inc. registered BGR Government Affairs to work the Codifying Useful Regulatory Definitions (CURD) Act, H.R. 1394/S. 184. Accountable for Health (A4H) registered Healthe Consulting, LLC to lobby on H.R. 8129, which would create a full-risk accountable care organization track and a complex-care payment track in Medicare, along with MIPS reporting exemptions.

Lobbyist campaign-contribution (LD-203) reports were sparse this period: of eight filings posted, seven reported no contributions. The exception was National Association of Wheat Growers, whose political action committee, Wheat PAC, gave $1,000 to Adam Gray for Congress — the campaign committee for Rep. Adam Gray — on June 30, 2026.
