Senate Lobbying Database Blocked Access All Day; FARA Logs One New Registrant
A persistent access block at the Senate Office of Public Records' lda.gov meant no lobbying registrations, activity reports, or LD-203 contribution filings could be verified for this edition.

Every attempt to query the Senate Office of Public Records' Lobbying Disclosure Act database at lda.gov today returned an "Access Denied" response from the site's edge network, rather than filing data. This affected all three lobbying-disclosure queries this newsletter normally runs: new lobbying registrations, broader quarterly activity reports and terminations, and LD-203 lobbyist campaign-contribution filings. The block was consistent across repeated attempts, different tools, and different request headers, so nothing from lda.gov could be confirmed as accurate for today's coverage window and no K Street section is being published in this edition as a result.

DOJ's FARA e-file system was reachable and returned data normally. In the window covered by this edition, one new foreign-agent registrant filed with the Department of Justice and no registrants terminated their registrations. Details are below.

 
FARA Registrants
One new foreign-agent registration, no terminations

The Department of Justice's FARA e-file system recorded one new foreign-agent registrant in the period covered by this edition and no terminated registrations. Registration filings themselves (the actual document list for each registrant) load via a client-side request DC Record does not fetch, so the link below goes to the registrant's public filings page for direct review rather than to a document already read.

  • Pearson Unlimited, LLC registered with the Department of Justice under the Foreign Agents Registration Act on September 10, 2026 (registration #7768). No termination date is on file. See the registrant's filings page for the underlying documents, and the New Registrants report for the source listing.
  • No registrants appear on the Terminated Registrants report for this window.
 

Coverage window: September 10, 2026 (12:00am Pacific) through the morning of September 11, 2026

Sources: Senate Office of Public Records LDA API; DOJ FARA e-file system.
