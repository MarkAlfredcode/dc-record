Top Story

National Association for the Advancement of Colored People sues Department of Homeland Security

The civil rights organization filed suit in D.D.C. today; with the complaint not yet available via PACER/RECAP, the specific claims could not be confirmed.

The National Association for the Advancement of Colored People filed suit today, September 18, 2026, against the U.S. Department of Homeland Security in the U.S. District Court for the District of Columbia, docketed as National Association for the Advancement of Colored People v. U.S. Department of Homeland Security, No. 1:26-cv-03263.

The case is in its earliest procedural stage: as of this writing, no judge has been assigned, and the docket does not yet carry a populated nature-of-suit code or statutory cause of action, so the specific legal theory behind the suit could not be identified from the docket alone.

The complaint itself, filed as docket entry No. 1, is marked not available in CourtListener's RECAP archive, meaning it sits behind the PACER paywall and could not be read for this report. That leaves the underlying policy or agency action being challenged unconfirmed; this item reports only what the docket caption shows.

A second filing, entered the same day and described on the docket as a "Notice of Related Case," suggests the suit may be connected to other litigation already pending before the court, though that connection could not be confirmed since the related filing is likewise not available on RECAP.

A national civil rights organization suing a cabinet department is notable on its own terms regardless of the still-unknown specifics, and this docket is worth tracking as filings become available.
