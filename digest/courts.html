Top Story

No new cabinet-agency or D.C. government litigation filed in this window

Every new D.D.C. civil filing in the past three days fell outside this edition's scope.

The U.S. District Court for the District of Columbia logged 23 new civil filings between September 11 and September 14, 2026, but none named a cabinet-level federal agency or official, or the District of Columbia municipal government, as a party.

The batch included a pair of Foreign Sovereign Immunities Act wrongful-death suits against the Islamic Republic of Iran brought by relatives of U.S. service members, a federal-question removal case between private insurance parties, two diversity-jurisdiction contract and insurance disputes, an unrelated case between two individuals with no case detail available, and one criminal matter — all of which are outside this edition's scope under the exclusion rules (foreign-sovereign suits, purely private diversity suits, and criminal dockets are not covered here).

Because none of today's filings involved a cabinet department, a cabinet official sued in an official capacity, or the D.C. government, there is no new litigation to report in either the general or FOIA categories today.
