Top Story

House Democrats file West Bank settlement sanctions bill during pro forma session

Neither chamber held a vote Thursday, but Representative Joaquin Castro introduced H.R. 10642 with dozens of cosponsors.

The House met for three minutes on Thursday, October 1, 2026, and the Senate for 35 seconds, both in pro forma sessions with no recorded votes. Even so, 98 measures were introduced in the House, and the most consequential is H.R. 10642, from Rep. Joaquin Castro (D-TX-20).

The bill would impose sanctions with respect to settlements in the West Bank and Gaza. It was referred to the Committee on Foreign Affairs, and in addition to the Committee on the Judiciary, the Committee on Financial Services and the Committee on Ways and Means. The Congressional Record lists dozens of House cosponsors.

Two other bills drew wide notice: H.J.Res. 218 from Rep. Steve Cohen (D-TN-9), a proposed constitutional amendment to abolish the electoral college, and H.Res. 1603 from Rep. Jamie Raskin (D-MD-8) on book banning. Introduction does not signal floor action; the House and Senate each next meet in pro forma session Monday, October 5, 2026.
