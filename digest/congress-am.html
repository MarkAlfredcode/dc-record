Top Story

A discharge petition on immigration status sits one signature short of 218

Both chambers met only briefly in pro forma sessions on Thursday, so the petition tally is the most consequential item this week.

Discharge Petition No. 25, which would force consideration of H.R. 1589 (a bill to authorize the cancellation of removal and adjustment of status of certain aliens), stands at 217 of 218 signatures, one short of the number needed. It was filed through H.Res. 1414, sponsored by Rep. Sylvia R. Garcia. No open petition gained signatures between October 27 and today.

Discharge Petition No. 27, sponsored by Rep. Thomas Massie for H.R. 185, has 216 of 218 signatures. Discharge Petition No. 3 (H.R. 185, sponsored by Rep. Brendan F. Boyle) and Discharge Petition No. 24 (H.R. 7007, sponsored by Rep. Jamie Raskin) each have 212.

The floor itself was quiet. The Senate met for under a minute in a pro forma session, and the House met for about two minutes with no recorded votes. Both chambers next meet in pro forma sessions on Monday, November 2.

Pro forma sessions in both chambers, 13 committee reports and 34 new House bills

The Senate met at 2:00:01 p.m. in a pro forma session and adjourned at 2:00:39 p.m. until 4:30 p.m. on Monday, November 2. No Senate committee meetings were held.
