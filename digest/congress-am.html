Top Story

A discharge petition on immigration status sits one signature short of 218 as Congress holds pro forma sessions

Discharge Petition No. 25 stands at 217 of 218 signatures while neither chamber held a vote on Thursday.

Discharge Petition No. 25, sponsored by Rep. Sylvia R. Garcia (H.Res. 1414), would force consideration of H.R. 1589, a bill to authorize the cancellation of removal and adjustment of status of certain aliens. It has 217 of the 218 signatures needed, with one to go. No petition gained signatures, was filed, or reached 218 since 10/20/2026.

Discharge Petition No. 27, sponsored by Rep. Thomas Massie (H.Res. 1430) on H.R. 185, has 216 signatures, two short of 218.

The floor was quiet. The House met at 3 p.m. and adjourned at 3:02 p.m. with no Yea and Nay votes, no recorded votes and no quorum calls. The Senate met in pro forma session for about 30 seconds. Both chambers are scheduled for pro forma sessions again on Monday, October 26.

No new Congressional Record issue has been published since this one (first covered 2026-10-23); the summary below is carried over.

On the Record

Two-minute House session, 30-second Senate session, and 59 new measures introduced

The Senate met at 9:00:03 a.m. in pro forma session, adjourned at 9:00:33 a.m. and next meets at 5:30 p.m. Monday, October 26. No Senate committee meetings were held. The House met at 3 p.m. and adjourned at 3:02 p.m., with Rep. Lloyd Smucker serving as Speaker pro tempore. There were no recorded votes and no House hearings.

The House received 53 public bills (H.R. 10855-10907), one joint resolution and five simple resolutions, and filed one committee report, on H.R. 1382. Most of the measures are routine. Among the more notable are a bill to lower the Medicare eligibility age to 50, a bill to bar waivers on Russian energy imports, a proposed constitutional amendment on a national referendum process, and an elections-interference bill with a long list of Democratic cosponsors.
