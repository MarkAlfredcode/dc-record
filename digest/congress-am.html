A one-signature gap on an immigration discharge petition as Congress stays out

The House met for two minutes on Monday, October 26, 2026 (12:30 p.m. to 12:32 p.m.), with no votes and no quorum calls. The Senate met in a pro forma session from 5:30 p.m. to 5:30 p.m. and adjourned until 2 p.m. on Thursday, October 29, 2026. On the Record

Pro forma sessions in both chambers; 13 measures introduced in the House

The House met briefly on Monday with no recorded votes and no reports filed, and the Senate held a pro forma session. The measures below were all introduced in the House on October 26, 2026; none were reported by a committee. They are mostly routine, covering agriculture, wildfire, food safety, health care and three commemorative resolutions. The one with a foreign-policy angle is H.R. 10912 on defense articles for Ukraine.

  • H.R. 10908, sponsored by Rep. Michael Cloud (R-TX-27) — would implement Universal Savings Accounts and expand transparency, competition and patient rights in health care; referred to the Committee on Energy and Commerce and, in addition, the Committee on Ways and Means.
  • H.R. 10909, sponsored by Rep. Steve Cohen (D-TN-9) — would amend the Death in Custody Reporting Act of 2013 to require reports be submitted to the Director of the Bureau of Justice Statistics; referred to the Committee on the Judiciary and, in addition, the Committees on Homeland Security and Energy and Commerce.
  • H.R. 10910, sponsored by Rep. James Gallagher (R-CA-1) — would amend the Animal Welfare Act to protect companion animals held or cared for at certain facilities; referred to the Committee on Agriculture.
  • H.R. 10911, sponsored by Rep. Marcy Kaptur (D-OH-9) — would create a voluntary U.S. Swine health improvement plan under the Animal Health Protection Act, run by the Secretary of Agriculture with the States and the swine industry; referred to the Committee on Agriculture.
  • H.R. 10912, sponsored by Rep. William Keating (D-MA-9) — would amend the Arms Export Control Act to address third party transfers of certain defense articles for Ukraine; referred to the Committee on Foreign Affairs.
  • H.R. 10913, sponsored by Rep. Nancy Mace (R-SC-1) — would amend the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act regarding adulterated food containing certain preservatives; referred to the Committee on Energy and Commerce.
  • H.R. 10914, sponsored by Rep. Jennifer McClellan (D-VA-4) — would improve assistance for socially disadvantaged farmers and ranchers and veteran farmers and ranchers; referred to the Committee on Agriculture.
  • H.R. 10915, sponsored by Rep. Richard McCormick (R-GA-7) — would direct the Director of the National Institute of Standards and Technology to develop standards, methodologies and recommendations for the cybersecurity of cloud-based infrastructure; referred to the Committee on Science, Space, and Technology and, in addition, the Committee on Oversight and Government Reform.
  • H.R. 10916, sponsored by Rep. Joe Neguse (D-CO-2) — would require the Secretary of Agriculture and the Secretary of the Interior to jointly develop a strategy for using aquatic restoration and conservation in wildfire risk mitigation and post-fire recovery; referred to the Committee on Natural Resources and, in addition, the Committee on Agriculture.
  • H.R. 10917, sponsored by Rep. Jimmy Panetta (D-CA-19) — would direct the Secretary of Agriculture to select and implement landscape-scale forest restoration projects to help communities build resilience to wildfire; referred to the Committee on Agriculture and, in addition, the Committees on Natural Resources, Energy and Commerce, and Education and Workforce.
  • H. Res. 1639, sponsored by Rep. Wesley Bell (D-MO-1) — supports designating October 26, 2026, as the "Day of the Deployed"; referred to the Committee on Armed Services.
  • H. Res. 1640, sponsored by Rep. Young Kim (R-CA-40) — expresses support for designating October 2026 as "National Financial Planning Month"; referred to the Committee on Financial Services.
  • H. Res. 1641, sponsored by Rep. Raja Krishnamoorthi (D-IL-8) — honors the contributions of Indian Americans and supports designating July as "Indian American Heritage Month"; referred to the Committee on Oversight and Government Reform.
