Top Story

Khanna and Massie file a war powers resolution on Yemen

A bipartisan pair wants the House to order U.S. forces out of unauthorized hostilities against the Houthis.

On Friday, October 9, Rep. Ro Khanna (D-CA-17) introduced H.Con.Res. 124, which would direct the President under section 5(c) of the War Powers Resolution to remove United States Armed Forces from hostilities against the Houthis in Yemen that Congress has not authorized. It was referred to the Committee on Foreign Affairs.

Rep. Thomas Massie is listed in the Congressional Record as a cosponsor. The resolution was one of 31 bills and resolutions introduced during a House session that lasted about two minutes, and the Congressional Record shows no debate or vote on it.

Brief sessions in both chambers, 31 measures introduced in the House
