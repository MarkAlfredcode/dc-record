Top Story

Immigration and Customs Enforcement sole-sources a three-year Hootsuite social media monitoring platform, value redacted

The agency's justification says only Hootsuite can supply the platform, including an artificial intelligence tool trained on twelve years of social media data.

The U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement Office of Acquisition Management, part of the Department of Homeland Security, plans to buy Hootsuite Inc. Enterprise licenses and the Talkwalker platform for its Office of Public Affairs. The order goes through Carahsoft Technology Corp., the reseller on a Department of Homeland Security-wide agreement. The justification (J&A-26-0292) is filed as "items peculiar to one manufacturer," and the dollar amount is redacted in the posted copy. The planned period of performance runs September 30, 2026 through September 29, 2029, so it begins before the notice was posted on October 12.

The document says Hootsuite is the only vendor able to provide "proprietary AI capability unavailable from other sources," naming a tool called Wisdom "which has been trained on twelve years of historical social media data." It describes the platform as covering publishing, monitoring, listening, alerting, archiving and workflow approval across multiple social media accounts. A separate prior justification for Hootsuite Enterprise and the Talkwalker visual recognition tool at the same agency is also on file in this pipeline's vendor history.

Hootsuite is a commercial software company with federal contracts on record since 2011. The unusual part is the scope: a multi-year, single-vendor monitoring platform for the agency's public affairs office, justified by the vendor's own proprietary AI.
