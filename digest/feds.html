Top Story

Education Department orders a $237,405 print-fleet transition from AlphaSix without competition, saying its old contract ended one day before the new one had to start

The justification was posted October 23, more than three weeks after the October 1 start date it gives for the order.

The Education Department's Federal Student Aid office posted a justification on October 23 for a sole-source delivery order to AlphaSix for managed print services. The order runs through the NASA SEWP V contract vehicle (contract number NNG15SD28B) and covers networked multifunction devices, installation, maintenance, supplies, secure print functions and transition support. The estimated value in the description of the action is $237,405.22, although the form's header lists $209,360.64. The base year is October 1, 2026 through September 30, 2027, with one option year. The agency cites urgent need under the exception to fair opportunity, meaning it skipped the competition among existing contract holders.

The document says the office's current devices come off the department-wide contract effective September 30, 2026, and the new order must be effective by October 1. It says this leaves no gap in which to remove, replace and configure a different fleet. It also says the timing is "substantially shorter than the transition period contemplated by the Performance Work Statement, which allows up to 60 calendar days for transition-in," and calls it "an unforeseen shift." The document adds that the existing fleet is tied to HP lease and service agreements "held through the current contractor," and that the order is meant to keep printing running "while the Government completes a competitive follow-on acquisition." It does not name the current contractor.
