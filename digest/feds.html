Top Story

Air Force seeks design-build teams for F-47 fighter campus at Nellis Air Force Base using fast-track agreements

The presolicitation, updated today, uses Other Transaction Agreements rather than standard federal contracts.

The Air Force's 772d Enterprise Sourcing Squadron, working with the Air Force Civil Engineer Center, is seeking design-build teams to deliver rapid construction projects supporting the F-47 fighter mission at Nellis Air Force Base, Nevada. The work would use a progressive design-build model, in which designer and builder work together from the start.

Any resulting agreement would be an Other Transaction Agreement under 10 U.S.C. § 2808a, a flexible deal type that sits outside the usual federal acquisition rules. The notice was first listed October 31 and updated today to add Attachment F, covering proposed construction markups and commercial pricing parameters. The three attached files have not yet been read and are queued for a later edition.

Army Corps of Engineers plans a $40 million to $70 million Veterans Affairs medical center build in Canandaigua, New York

Two new presolicitations were posted and 3 repeats were left out. The Nellis Air Force Base F-47 campus notice is today's Top Story.

Army Corps of Engineers plans design-build project at the Canandaigua Veterans Affairs medical center, valued at $40 million to $70 million
Presolicitation  ·  $40M to $70M estimated  ·  U.S. Army Corps of Engineers, Buffalo District
The U.S. Army Corps of Engineers, Buffalo District, intends to award a firm-fixed-price design-build contract for work at the Veterans Affairs medical center in Canandaigua, New York. It will use two-phase selection, with Phase I narrowing the field to up to five firms for Phase II. No proposals are being accepted yet.

U.S. Transportation Command seeks commercial carriers for recurring Pacific passenger flights carrying government weapons and ammunition
Sources Sought  ·  Two round trips per month on each route  ·  U.S. Transportation Command
U.S. Transportation Command is looking for approved commercial carriers (FAA Part 121 or 135) to fly passengers, baggage, government-furnished weapons and small-arms ammunition (Hazard Class 1.4S) from Honolulu to Wake Island Airfield (up to 39 passengers) and to Kwajalein Atoll (up to 12 passengers), each twice a month.
