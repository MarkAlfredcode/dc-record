Drug Enforcement Administration sole-sources a spot in Detroit's Thanksgiving parade to promote its Fentanyl Free America Initiative

The agency says only one organization, the parade's own foundation, can let its agents march in the parade.

The Drug Enforcement Administration plans to buy participation in the 2026 America's Thanksgiving Parade in Detroit from Michigan's Thanksgiving Parade Foundation without competition. The justification says participation will support the Fentanyl Free America Initiative by raising public awareness of illicit fentanyl. DEA members will walk in the parade carrying the American flag and a banner for the agency and the initiative. The Detroit Field Division is the requiring office, and performance starts November 26, 2026.

The agency's reason for skipping competition is that the foundation, founded in 1982, exists to stage and perpetuate the parade, so it is the only organization able to provide participation. The authority cited is the "only one responsible source" rule (FAR 6.103-1). The estimated amount is blank in the posted copy, so no value is available. USAspending shows no federal contract on record under the foundation's name. The notice was posted as a Special Notice, and the justification file is what identified it as a sole-source action.
