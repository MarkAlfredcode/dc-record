Top Story

Navy opens bidding to dismantle and dispose of the decommissioned nuclear cruiser ex-USS Long Beach

A request for proposals now covers transport, dismantlement and disposal of the ship, including its defueled reactor plants.

The Department of the Navy, through Naval Sea Systems Command, posted a request for proposals to transport, dismantle, de-militarize and dispose of ex-Long Beach (CGN9), including the defueled reactor plants, according to the notice description. The notice names PAE Maritime in connection with the request.

The notice ties the request to an earlier Request for Information (N00024-26-I-CGN9), which carried information from an industry day, ship tours and data library access instructions. An earlier edition listed this solicitation on October 7. The new posting is the formal request for proposals, which is why it counts as news again.

No value or deadline appears in the input, and the attached files have not been read in this edition.

USAspending Contracts

Immigration and Customs Enforcement adds $44.8 million to Ardent Group's pharmacy benefit contract for its Health Service Corps

From this morning's edition: USAspending.gov is pulled once a day.

8 new awards of $2 million or more (none from the Defense Department backlog), 3 large modifications and 1 cut. 4 are written up here, and the 8 routine ones are listed, each linked, in "USAspending: Every Other New Award" at the end of the edition.

Ardent Group, LLC received a $44,769,832 modification (P00006) from the U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement on October 22, exercising Option Period 1 of its contract. The contract provides the agency's Health Service Corps with a pharmacy benefit manager to handle the drug formulary, claims processing, pharmacy network administration and benefit plan design. Award.

Accenture Federal Services LLC received a $150,807,170 modification (P00004) from the Department of Veterans Affairs on October 22. It is described as a funding-only action for option year 1: incremental funding and a ceiling reallocation on the electronic health record modernization system integration contract. The description cites Executive Order 14398. Award.

Vivsoft Technologies LLC won a $10,473,395 award signed October 20 from the General Services Administration's Federal Acquisition Service, described as "Operational Enterprise Services OES III" for engineering and technical support, classified under computer systems design services. The description does not say which agency the work serves. Award.

Battle's Transportation, Inc. received a $2,715,912 task order from the Department of Veterans Affairs, signed October 22, for wheelchair van transportation at the Washington, D.C., VA Medical Center. Award.
