Air Force plans $1.5 million in "Outward Mindset" leadership training from the Arbinger Institute with no competition

The justification says only the Arbinger Institute can supply its proprietary courses for about 900 leaders at the Ogden Air Logistics Complex and two outposts.

The Air Force's Ogden Air Logistics Complex at Hill Air Force Base, Utah, intends to set up a single-award blanket purchase agreement with The Arbinger Institute (also called The Arbinger Company) for leadership training. The justification's cover page gives an estimated contract cost, including options, of $1,500,000, while its narrative describes a five-year ordering period, from about February 15, 2027 to February 14, 2032, with "an estimated total ceiling of $3M". The justification invokes the only-one-responsible-source authority and describes the courses as proprietary. It was posted as a Special Notice rather than under the Justification category.

The requirement covers about 900 leaders and key personnel at the complex and at two outposts, the Aerospace Maintenance and Regeneration Group at Davis-Monthan Air Force Base, Arizona, and the 575th Aircraft Maintenance Squadron at Randolph Air Force Base, Texas. The courses are Developing and Implementing an Outward Mindset, Outward Leadership and Outward Performance, plus train-the-trainer certifications. The document calls the certification pathway "a key component" meant to build a self-sustaining internal training capacity.

The document also says the complex "previously attempted to remove barriers to competition by having internal personnel facilitate courses modeled on the Arbinger concept." USAspending shows a federal contract under Arbinger's name going back to December 2018, so this is not its first government work.
