Customs and Border Protection awards $144 million contract for a Jacksonville facility

The largest new federal contract action in the window is a September 30 construction order for a Florida border-agency building.

Southwest Valley Constructors Co was awarded $144 million on September 30, 2026 to build a U.S. Customs and Border Protection facility in Jacksonville, Florida, under the Department of Homeland Security. USAspending describes it as construction of administrative facilities and service buildings.

Separately, the Department of the Navy plans to rent a Northern Virginia community college concert hall for 12 U.S. Marine Band concerts without competition, and the Department of Health and Human Services is adding $298 million to a sole-source Ebola therapeutic contract with Regeneron Pharmaceuticals Inc.

Marine Band wants to rent a community college concert hall for 12 concerts and 6 recording days, with no competition
Virginia Community College System  ·  no value given  ·  only one company can do it  ·  Department of the Navy (Marine Corps)
The Department of the Navy intends to award a sole-source fixed-price contract to the Virginia Community College System for 18 days of rental at the Rachel M. Schlesinger Concert Hall and Arts Center in Alexandria, Virginia, between January and August 2027, for 12 U.S. Marine Band concerts and 6 recording days. The notice was posted as a Special Notice. According to the justification, concerts are planned at the start of the fiscal year and publicized at least six months ahead, so competing the venue "would negatively impact marketing efforts and projected attendance." No federal contract is on record under this vendor's name in USAspending, and no dollar value is stated.

Regeneron gets a sole-source $298 million add-on to supply Ebola treatment doses through 2031
Regeneron Pharmaceuticals Inc.  ·  $297,715,850 estimated  ·  only one company can do it  ·  Department of Health and Human Services
The Administration for Strategic Preparedness and Response at the Department of Health and Human Services is modifying its Project BioShield contract with Regeneron Pharmaceuticals Inc. to buy up to 26,500 more doses of the Ebola therapeutic Inmazeb for the Strategic National Stockpile through 2031. The document brings the contract total to $990,772,776.82 and says Inmazeb is the only FDA-approved Ebola therapeutic available. It also says moving production to another manufacturer would cost over $30 million and take at least three years.

Air Force signals it may sole-source crash-test dummy parts and calibration to Humanetics

Air Force tests the market for crash-test dummy parts, saying it may sole-source to the manufacturer
Sources Sought  ·  Department of the Air Force
The Air Force Test Center at Arnold Engineering Development Complex is seeking sources for replacement parts and factory calibration of anthropomorphic test devices (crash-test manikins). The notice says the government believes the requirement may be a sole-source buy to the original manufacturer, Humanetics Innovative Solutions, Inc., and asks whether other suppliers hold the necessary design rights and data.

OMB Apportionments

Department of Labor Apportions $4.5 Billion for Workforce Programs With Grantmaking Oversight Conditions

The Department of Labor approved apportionments totaling over $4.5 billion in fiscal year 2027 funds for workforce development, employment training, and apprenticeship programs under Public Law 119-75. Eight specific grant programs totaling approximately $651 million are subject to an oversight-hold condition that delays obligation until the department submits and OMB approves supporting spend plans. All apportioned amounts must comply with Executive Order 14332, 'Improving Oversight of Federal Grantmaking.'

Grant Programs Pending Spend Plan Approval

The following eight grant programs are being held pending submission and approval of Funding Opportunity Announcement (FOA) documentation. Funds are only available for obligation 10 business days after OMB receives the final Secretary-approved version of each FOA. The apportionment footnote explains the hold: 'An agency spend plan or other documentation is necessary to better understand how the agency intends to obligate some or all of the apportioned funds.' Apportionments Governed by Executive Order 14332

Two Department of Labor total budgetary resources apportionments, totaling $3.625 billion, include an explicit footnote conditioning spending: 'As permissible by law, amounts apportioned shall be spent in a manner consistent with the directives provided in Executive Order 14332, "Improving Oversight of Federal Grantmaking."'
