Top Story

Navy extends emergency construction contract at Bahrain's Fifth Fleet headquarters, citing troop reassignments to "Operation Epic Fury"

A no-cost bridge action keeps four incumbent contractors on the job at Naval Support Activity Bahrain while a stalled follow-on competition catches up.

The Naval Facilities Engineering Systems Command Europe Africa Central has extended, without raising the contract ceiling, the ordering period on its Mini Multiple Award Construction Contract (Mini MACC) covering Naval Support Activity Bahrain, the forward headquarters of the U.S. Fifth Fleet, for an additional 12 months through September 1, 2027. The extension draws on roughly $44 million of remaining capacity within the contract's existing $95 million ceiling and keeps four incumbent contractors -- Liberal Construction, LLC; Consorzio Continental Socconsortile A R; RocKs GMBH; Kooheji Contractors; and Macro Vantage Levant DMCC -- eligible for task orders.

The Navy's justification invokes "unusual and compelling urgency" under 10 U.S.C. § 3204(a)(2), warning that a lapse in construction services at the base would leave it unable to perform emergency repairs and would "degrade U.S. national security during a period of heightened operational activity in the region." It attributes the delay in awarding a competitive follow-on contract in part to the reassignment of key acquisition personnel to support "Operation Epic Fury," an operation name that does not otherwise appear anywhere else in today's contracting data.

The posting frames the bridge action as necessary to buy time for a full and open competition on the next long-term construction vehicle, saying a new contract award right now would cause a multi-month delay because of the security, legal, and logistical requirements of operating in Bahrain.
