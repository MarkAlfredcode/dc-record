Drug Enforcement Administration buys its way into Detroit's Thanksgiving parade without competition

The agency says only one organization can stage the parade, and the walk-in supports its Fentanyl Free America Initiative.

The Drug Enforcement Administration has posted a justification for skipping competition to take part in the 2026 America's Thanksgiving Parade in Detroit, Michigan, with Michigan's Thanksgiving Parade Foundation. The requirement comes from the agency's Detroit Field Division, and the period of performance starts 11/26/2026. According to the justification, participation 'will support the Fentanl Free America Initiative by increasing public awareness of the risks associated with illicity fentanyl' (typos as in the document), and 'DEA members will walk in the Parade with the American Flag and a DEA and Fentanyl Free America banner.'

The agency's stated reason is that the foundation 'was established with the sole purpose of staging and presenting the parade' and is the only organization able to do it. It cites the only-one-responsible-source authority (41 U.S.C. 3304(a)(1), FAR 6.103-1). The estimated amount line in the document is blank, so no value is given. USAspending shows no federal contract on record under the foundation's name. The justification was posted to SAM.gov as a Special Notice rather than in the Justification category.
