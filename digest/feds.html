Fujifilm Sonosite wins a $957.7 million Defense Logistics Agency and Veterans Affairs imaging contract

Two award notices posted today name the ultrasound maker on a joint radiology and imaging program contract.

Fujifilm Sonosite Inc. of Bothell, Washington, was awarded an indefinite-delivery, indefinite-quantity contract on October 29, 2026 under the joint Defense Logistics Agency and Department of Veterans Affairs Radiology and Imaging Systems Program (GEN 3), according to the award notice. The notice cites Veterans Affairs solicitation 36H797-25-R-0003 and Defense Logistics Agency solicitation SPE2D1-26-R-0002. It posts an amount of $957,714,000 (contract SPE2D1-27-D-0001).

A second notice from the Defense Logistics Agency's Troop Support office repeats the same $957,714,000 figure for Fujifilm Sonosite under contract SPE2D127D0001 (capital equipment notice). The notice describes the vehicle as a multiple-award contract, but the two postings name only Fujifilm Sonosite and do not say whether the amount is a ceiling shared with other holders. Neither notice's attachments were available to read.
