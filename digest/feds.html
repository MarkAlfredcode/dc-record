Top Story

Federal prison in Mississippi plans to hire FlixBus, the intercity bus company, without competition

The Bureau of Prisons says FlixBus North America is the only provider that can run scheduled bus service for its Yazoo City complex.

The Federal Bureau of Prisons, through the Federal Correctional Complex Yazoo City in Mississippi, posted a notice of intent on November 3 to award a firm-fixed-price sole-source contract for fiscal year 2027 "commercial long-distance bus transportation services" to FlixBus North America. The notice describes scheduled passenger bus routing to and from the prison complex for "critical operational transportation needs, official passenger movement, and regional transport connectivity." No dollar value is given. It was posted as a Special Notice rather than as a Justification.

The stated basis is that only one responsible source can meet the need. According to the notice, FlixBus is "the sole provider capable of meeting the government's specific requirement due to its proprietary intercity network and established commercial routes passing directly through the immediate geographic footprint of Yazoo City." It adds that any other vendor would require "a custom, non-commercial route approach." USAspending has no federal contract on record under the name FlixBus North America since fiscal year 2008, which makes a consumer bus brand an unusual sole-source vendor for a federal prison.

The notice invites other firms to submit capability statements by November 18, 2026, at 4:00 p.m. (the document gives both Eastern and Central time), and says the government alone decides whether to compete the work.
