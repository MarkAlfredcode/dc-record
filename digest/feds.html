Top Story

Health and Human Services books three Anthropic awards worth $9 million in one day for an 'enterprise wide chat bridge'

Three records dated October 27 from the department's Office of the Assistant Secretary for Financial Resources name Anthropic, PBC as the recipient.

The Department of Health and Human Services (Office of the Assistant Secretary for Financial Resources) recorded three awards to Anthropic, PBC on October 27: $4,842,000, $3,573,000 and $585,000, which add up to exactly $9,000,000. All three carry the same description, 'ANTHROPIC SI ENTERPRISE WIDE CHAT BRIDGE', and are coded as software as a service for business applications under computer systems design services.

The amounts are what has been obligated so far on each base award. The records in today's data don't say whether the awards were competed, what 'SI' or 'bridge' refers to, or how many department staff will use the service. The two larger awards are in the $2 million-and-up tier and the $585,000 award is in the smaller tier.

NASA buys nine lunar neutron spectrometers from Lockheed Martin without competition, citing the race with China
Lockheed Martin Advanced Technology Center  ·  Value redacted (award notice lists $4,663,726)  ·  Urgent need, no time to compete  ·  National Aeronautics and Space Administration (Ames Research Center)

Between October 24 and 29, five apportionment files were approved by the Office of Management and Budget without raising any review flags. All were loan-account apportionments: Department of Energy's Title 17 innovation lending, Department of Veterans Affairs housing and rehabilitation lending, and Department of Defense's health program.
