Marshals Service gives Coinbase an $8.1 million no-bid cryptocurrency custody contract after a reported $46 million theft at its previous contractor

The U.S. Marshals Service says it faces an urgent risk to seized crypto and that Coinbase is the only company that already holds its required security authorization.

The U.S. Marshals Service (Department of Justice) plans to award Coinbase Inc. an 18-month contract for custody, management and disposal of seized cryptocurrency, with no competition. The justification puts the estimated value at $8,128,125. It invokes unusual and compelling urgency, citing "an emergent and urgent situation that requires an immediate solution in order to avoid further possible loss of seized cryptocurrency" (notice, justification).

The justification says Coinbase "is the only company with a current Authorization to Use (ATU) designation, required by the USMS who could perform work without substantial interruption." It also says the earlier Class 2-4 award went to Command Services and Support Inc. and "was unsuccessfully protested by an unsuccessful offeror at the Agency level and the GAO." Separately, the document describes a stop-work notice issued January 23, 2026 after an employee of that incumbent allegedly stole seized cryptocurrency, a loss it puts at at least $46 million. That passage was not independently matched word for word against the file, so it is reported here as the document's own account.

USAspending shows a Coinbase federal contract on record since May 2020, so this is not its first government work. The resulting vehicle is a commercial firm-fixed-price indefinite-delivery contract.

NASA buys Artemis rocket avionics from Space Vector without competition, citing urgency and the race with China
Space Vector LLC  ·  value redacted  ·  urgent need, no time to compete  ·  National Aeronautics and Space Administration
NASA's Marshall Space Flight Center is buying Secondary Payload Avionics Units and engineering support, including a spare, for the Artemis IV and V missions of the Space Launch System.
