Top Story

Air Force picks an individual airshow announcer, with no federal contract on record, as the sole source for Dover's 2027 air boss job

The Dover Air Force Base open house would pay a named person, not a company, to run its air boss, narration and sound operations with no competition.

The Department of the Air Force plans to award a contract without competition to William David Schultz for air boss, narration, sound and ground-support services at the 2027 First State Airshow at Dover Air Force Base in Delaware. The notice, titled “Intent to Award Sole Source - Air Boss,” was posted as a Special Notice on October 15 rather than in the Justification category. No value is stated.

The draft performance work statement sets the show for June 12-13, 2027. The air boss would direct performers, liaise with the Federal Aviation Administration and air traffic control, manage taxiing aircraft and vehicle traffic, and run the mandatory pilot safety briefing. A narrator would prepare introductions and record announcements for financial sponsors. The audio requirement calls for speakers along a 5,000-foot crowd line, sound projection to crowds of at least 50,000, and a control platform of at least 320 square feet.

The notice’s stated reason for skipping competition is that he is the only qualified source. As extracted from the notice, it cites more than 200 hours of warbird airshow experience, operations alongside the Thunderbirds, Blue Angels and Snowbirds, and the specialized FAA and military requirements. USAspending shows no federal contract on record under this name since fiscal 2008, so this would be the first on record. The performance work statement attached to the notice is marked as a draft.
