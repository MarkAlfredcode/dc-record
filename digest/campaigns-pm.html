Top Story
House Tables Third-Party Impeachment Push Against Trump, 232-147
A resolution from Rep. Al Green accusing President Trump of high crimes and misdemeanors was killed on the floor Tuesday, with 47 members voting present rather than yes or no.

On Tuesday, September 15, the House voted 232-147, with 47 members voting "present," to table — and thereby kill without a vote on the merits — H.Res. 1486, a resolution introduced by Rep. Al Green (D-TX-9) on August 24 that would have impeached President Donald John Trump for "high crimes and misdemeanors." The motion to table was offered by Rep. Russell Fry (R-SC-7) and adopted on a yea-and-nay vote, Roll No. 298. The House separately agreed without objection to lay a motion to reconsider that vote on the table, foreclosing another attempt.

Because the resolution was tabled rather than voted up or down, the House never took a direct vote on the underlying impeachment articles themselves.

The same legislative day also saw the House pass, 220-204, H.Con.Res. 93, directing the President under the War Powers Resolution to remove U.S. Armed Forces from hostilities with Iran, and pass H.J.Res. 210 disapproving an EPA rule on California nonroad-engine and ocean-going-vessel pollution standards, 216-11. Full detail on both, plus every bill introduced that day, is in "On the Record" below.

FEC Sends RFAIs to Rep. Nick LaLota and Sen. Steve Daines

Two sitting members of Congress received Requests for Additional Information from the FEC this week, both dated September 15. Notably, LaLota's campaign and Paul LePage's open-seat Maine campaign (see Requests for Additional Information, below) share the same treasurer, Thomas Datwyler.

Webb's View of M64. A composite of Hubble and Webb images reveals the "Black Eye Galaxy" (M64) in new detail: Webb's infrared instrument shows the dust band that gives the galaxy its name, glowing red as it absorbs and re-emits light from newborn stars. The galaxy's inner and outer gas regions rotate in opposite directions, evidence of a past merger with a smaller galaxy.
