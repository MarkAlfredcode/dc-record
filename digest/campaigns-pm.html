Top Story
Union PAC returned $20,863.51 it says was deposited in error, and FEC wants details
FEC's October 7 letter to a sprinkler fitters' local union political committee questions a refund to the union itself.

The FEC's Reports Analysis Division sent a request for additional information to the political action committee tied to Road Sprinkler Fitters Local Union No. 669, U.A., AFL-CIO over its June monthly report. Schedule B shows $20,863.51 in disbursements to the union itself, described as a "Refund of contribution (deposit made in error)."

The letter notes that federal law bars unions from contributing to influence federal elections, apart from paying a connected committee's solicitation and administrative costs. It asks the committee to explain the transaction, give the date of the original deposit, and report the original receipt on Schedule A of an amended report.

Separately, Vote Rev PAC was asked about a reimbursement for "administrative expenses" to "Vote Rev," an apparently unregistered organization, which FEC says looks like an in-kind contribution.
