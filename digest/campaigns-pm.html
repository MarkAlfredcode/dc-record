Top Story
Massa for Congress says it is holding cash for legal fees after alleged forgery and theft of House pay records
The committee told the Federal Election Commission on Thursday that a former employee stole tens of thousands of dollars.

Massa for Congress, the committee of Eric Massa (D-NY-29), filed a Form 99 on October 2 responding to a Federal Election Commission letter dated September 2, 2026. The letter explained why the committee has kept cash on hand after ceasing operations. The committee said it is retaining the money as a contingency fund for legal fees tied to pending legal matters, and that it will dispose of excess funds and terminate once those matters are resolved.

The filing says the committee has informed the Justice Department, the House Office of Employment Counsel and the House Ethics Committee of outstanding legal issues. It describes them as "documented proof of forgery of House pay records that resulted in the theft of tens of thousands of dollars by a former employee," unearthed during a "multi year investigation of debunked allegations against Congressman Massa." The committee says the documents have been presented to those bodies and the matter remains unresolved. These are the committee's own unverified assertions; the filing names no employee, and I found no independent confirmation.

The Federal Election Commission's candidate record still tags Massa as an "Incumbent." The filing itself describes a committee that has ceased operations.

Sitting Lawmakers
Three House Democrats answer the Federal Election Commission, two of them over general-election money

Three incumbent House committees filed Form 99 correspondence on October 2. Two involve contributions for a general election in which the member is not on the ballot or is not participating.

  • Rep. Frederica S. Wilson (D-FL-24), via Frederica S. Wilson for Congress: reported that general-election contributions were refunded in full on September 30, 2026, and that the refunds will appear on the committee's October filing. Filing.
  • Rep. David Scott (D-GA-13), via David Scott for Congress: in a letter from Treasurer Alfredia Aaron Scott about the July Quarterly Report (04/01–06/30/2026), acknowledged receiving contributions for a general election in which the committee is not participating. It said refunds are being issued and will be in the fourth-quarter report. Filing.
  • Rep. Rohit Khanna (D-CA-17), via Ro for Congress Inc: replied to a Request for Additional Information dated 08/30/2026 about the July Quarterly Report. Treasurer Melissa Nissen said all excessive contributions have been refunded, redesignated or reattributed with notices to donors. On the question about two donors with foreign addresses, she said the committee obtained current passport copies and confirmed U.S. citizenship. Filing.

The Massa for Congress filing, also tagged incumbent in Federal Election Commission records, is covered in the Top Story.

Challengers, PACs and a presidential candidate: mostly short notes

Five non-incumbent Form 99 submissions followed the Federal Election Commission's templates: a no-activity notice, a status form and a treasurer change, plus one reply to an information request. Nothing here is unusual.

  • Steven Fuller for Congress (Independent American Party, CO-5 challenger, candidate committee), October 2: said in response to its quarterly filing that the committee has not raised or spent $5,000. Filing.
  • Fight On PAC (PAC), October 2: replied to a Request for Additional Information dated October 1 about its August Monthly Report (07/01–07/31/2026). Treasurer Cabell Hobbs said the PAC will file an amended Statement of Organization naming Team Hinson as a joint fundraising representative. Filing.
  • Alaine Elizabeth Giuliani (presidential committee), received October 1: a one-line notice, "No monies to report for October Quarterly." Filing.
  • Deliver Energize Succeed PAC (PAC), received October 1: a paper Notification of Multicandidate Status, shipped by Campaign Compliance Group Inc. on September 25. The scan is largely illegible, so I could not read the qualifying details. Filing.
  • American Commercial Barge Line LLC PAC (PAC), received September 30 and surfacing in the API only now: a paper cover letter dated September 8, resent by FedEx after certified mail failed, enclosing an amended Form 1 that names Rachel Carlton as new treasurer and custodian of records. Filing.
