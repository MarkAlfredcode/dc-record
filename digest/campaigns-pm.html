Top Story
Sen. Amy Klobuchar’s committee defends $42,695 asset sale to her state-level committee
Filed Thursday in response to a Federal Election Commission inquiry on the April Quarterly Report.

Klobuchar for Minnesota, the campaign committee of Sen. Amy Klobuchar (D-MN), told the Federal Election Commission on October 1 that $42,695.14 it received on March 31, 2026 from Minnesotans for Klobuchar was payment for transferred assets, not a fundraising sale.

The letter, signed by treasurer Samuel Clark, answers an August 27 inquiry. It says the assets — mailing lists, photographs, video footage and access to a website domain — were built for the committee’s own use, had an ascertainable market value, and were sold at the usual and normal charge in an arm’s-length transaction, citing Advisory Opinions 2002-14 and 2003-19.

The committee says it will amend the April Quarterly Report to clarify the nature of the assets and that they were sold at fair market value. Read the letter.
