Top Story
Two committees, one embezzling ex-treasurer: FEC ties Rep. Don Beyer's campaign and BackPAC to the same restitution debt
BackPAC and Friends of Don Beyer share an office, a current treasurer, and now an identical FEC inquiry into money owed them by a former treasurer who has pleaded guilty to embezzlement.

On September 22, both BackPAC and Friends of Don Beyer — the campaign committee of Rep. Don Beyer (D-Va.) — filed nearly identical responses to Requests for Additional Information from the Federal Election Commission. Both letters, dated August 18, questioned a debt each committee had disclosed on Schedule D of its 2026 July Quarterly Report: a "court-ordered restitution payment owed to the Committee" that the FEC said "appears to be related to an unauthorized use of committee funds."

Both committees gave the same answer. The debt reflects restitution owed to them by their former treasurer, Katherine Buchanan, who has pleaded guilty to embezzlement and been ordered by a federal court to pay restitution to her victims — including both committees. Both filings say the matter has already been referred to the FEC's Office of General Counsel.

The two committees share more than a former treasurer: both list the same Arlington, Virginia mailing address (2503-D N. Harrison St., Box #310) and the same current treasurer, Allison Murray, who signed both responses. Neither filing discloses the dollar amount Buchanan was ordered to repay.

A New Lunar Crater: McGetchin. A comet or asteroid roughly the size of a humpback whale struck the Moon sometime between April and May of 2024, and NASA's Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter caught before-and-after images of the resulting crater — named McGetchin, about the size of two soccer fields. Craters this size are only expected once every 132 years.
