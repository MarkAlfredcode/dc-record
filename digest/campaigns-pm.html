Top Story
FEC flags four committees over apparent unauthorized spending, including $13,064.50 in “misappropriations” recoveries at a Seafarers PAC
Letters dated October 6 and 8 say the Commission may take further legal action over what it calls apparent improper use of committee funds.

The Federal Election Commission asked Seafarers Political Activity Donation - Seafarers International Union of North America about a $13,064.50 receipt on its September Monthly report labeled “Insurance recovery for additional misappropriations found See memo.” The letter, filed October 8, calls the item an apparent unauthorized use of committee funds and says the Commission “may take further legal action regarding this apparent improper use of Committee funds.” Seafarers letter.

The same day, the Commission sent EchoStar Corporation and DISH Network Corporation PAC a letter about a $4,767.41 disbursement labeled “DISPUTED ACH DEBIT” with the same warning, and Bish for Congress (Republican challenger Christine S. Bish) one about disbursements described as fraud charges ($483.27 and $1,000.00, noted as reported to Wells Fargo) plus Wells Fargo provisional credits of $5,001.21 and $6,192.30 that it must tie to specific expenditures. EchoStar letter; Bish letter.

Earlier, in a letter filed October 6, James Osyf for Congress (Democratic challenger James Osyf in Virginia) was asked about a $2,058.01 disbursement labeled “Unauthorized transaction” on its July Quarterly report; the Commission noted the committee’s explanation but also said it may take further legal action. Osyf letter.
