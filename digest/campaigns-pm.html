Top Story
Jeff Flake's dormant Senate committee: he "has not yet decided" on a future campaign
A one-page FEC response filed this week is the clearest signal yet that the former Arizona senator hasn't ruled out running for federal office again.

Jeff Flake for US Senate Inc., the campaign committee of former Republican Senator Jeff Flake of Arizona, told the FEC on September 12 that Flake "has not yet decided whether to become a candidate for federal office in a future election cycle," and that the committee "accordingly intends to retain most of or all of the cash on hand pending a decision by Senator Flake with respect to his future plans." The one-page Form 99 letter was filed in response to an RFAI the FEC sent the committee on September 2, 2026.

Flake represented Arizona in the Senate from 2013 to 2019, chose not to seek re-election in 2018 amid friction with President Trump, and later served as U.S. Ambassador to Turkey. His campaign committee has stayed administratively open ever since — the FEC's inquiry appears to have been prompted by why it is still sitting on cash years after his last campaign, and the committee's answer is that Flake himself hasn't foreclosed a future run.

The letter was signed by treasurer Ashley Ragan, filing from a Phoenix, Arizona address. The same day, the same treasurer filed an unrelated Form 99 on behalf of Fiscal Leadership and Knowing Economics PAC from the identical address — see the Form 99 Correspondence section below.

On the Record
A three-minute session marks 9/11's 25th anniversary; 35 measures introduced

The most recent Congressional Record available is from Thursday, September 10 — Congress has not been in session since. The House convened at 3:30 p.m., adopted H. Res. 1520 expressing the House's sense on the 25th anniversary of the September 11, 2001 attacks, and adjourned three minutes later "as a further mark of respect to the memory of the victims." There were no recorded votes, no yea-and-nay votes, and no quorum calls. The Senate met in a 30-second pro forma session and adjourned until 3 p.m. Monday, September 14.
