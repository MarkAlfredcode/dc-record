Top Story
House Budget Committee takes its fiscal-commission pitch to Dallas, with Panetta, Portman and Manchin as witnesses
A field hearing Monday afternoon puts three former top officials from both parties in front of the committee to make the case for a bipartisan fiscal commission.

The House Committee on the Budget holds a field hearing Monday, September 21 at Old Parkland in Dallas, Texas, titled "America's Fiscal Future: Examining the Need for a Fiscal Commission." The hearing is scheduled for 2:00 p.m. Eastern (11:00 a.m. Pacific).

The witness list is notable for its bipartisan, cross-branch pedigree: Leon Panetta, a former Democratic member of Congress from California who also served as White House Chief of Staff, Secretary of Defense and, earlier in his career, chairman of the House Budget Committee, now heads the Panetta Institute for Public Policy; Rob Portman, a former Republican senator and representative from Ohio who also directed the Office of Management and Budget, now runs the Portman Center for Policy Solutions; and Joe Manchin, a former Democratic senator and governor of West Virginia known for his work on bipartisan fiscal deals.

All three witnesses have submitted written statements ahead of the hearing, linked below alongside the committee's field hearing notice.
