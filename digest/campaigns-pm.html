Top Story
PAC Tells FEC a Federal Court Ordered Its Former Treasurer to Repay $29,413.77
Forward Together PAC answered an FEC inquiry on Tuesday about restitution it lists as a debt owed to the committee.

Forward Together PAC of Williamsburg, Virginia filed a Form 99 on October 6 responding to a September 1 Request for Additional Information on its July 2026 Quarterly Report. The FEC's letter flagged a Schedule D debt described as "Court-ordered restitution payment owed to the Committee," and said it appeared to be related to an unauthorized use of committee funds.

In the response, the committee says a federal court has ordered its former treasurer, Katherine Buchanan, to pay restitution totaling $29,413.77, which it therefore reports as money owed to the committee. It cites FEC advisory opinions 1991-38 (DeConcini) and 2005-04 (Boehner) and says it explained the matter in an earlier Form 99 filed July 20, 2026 and has made other filings giving context.

The filing does not say how much was taken, over what period, or how much has been repaid; the committee says only that it "continues to work with the Commission on its resolution." Separately, two other committees drew FEC questions this week over transactions the agency labeled apparent unauthorized disbursements: James Osyf for Congress ($2,058.01) and Friends of Chris Bennett ($112.67), both listed under Requests for Additional Information below.
