Top Story
Nebraska House hopeful Kishla Askins says a $270,000 ad-buy report was wrong and two in-kind gifts went unreported
Her Sept. 30 Form 99 answers an FEC request after her campaign had already filed its termination report.

Kishla Askins for Congress, the committee of Democrat Kishla Askins (Nebraska’s 2nd District, open seat), filed a Form 99 on 09/30 responding to a Request for Additional Information. The visible response starts at item 2; the FEC’s letter itself isn’t in this filing.

Askins says she made two in-kind contributions that never appeared on a 48-hour report: $32,525.94 on 05/01/2026 and $3,525 on 05/02/2026, both for texting services from Scale to Win. She also says the 48-hour report filed 05/05/2026 for a $270,000 in-kind contribution was wrong; the correct figure, $263,006, is in her Termination Report. That amount went to MLZ/Canal Media for TV ads.

The filing also itemizes smaller personal-funds spending, including $450 to Mary Broughton for canvassing support (dated 04/28/2025), $850 to Dan Woodward as an event speaker, and several LinkedIn advertising charges ($25, $101.58 and $150.93). It does not say whether the FEC considers the matter resolved.

Requests for Additional Information
Fifteen new RFAIs, led by a late-24-hour-report question on $32,834 in mailers

The FEC sent 15 new RFAIs to non-incumbent committees on 09/29 and 09/30. Most are routine math and disclosure fixes: opening balances that don’t match, year-to-date totals that don’t add up, and committee-name rules. The most substantive is A Fight Worth Having, flagged for apparently late 24-hour independent-expenditure reports.

  • A Fight Worth Having — RFAI (09/30) on the July Quarterly Report: the FEC says the committee may have missed required 24-hour reports on last-minute independent expenditures. An attachment lists payments dated 05/26/2026 (received by the FEC 05/28) for the California primary, split evenly across Scott Wiener, Saikat Chakrabarti and Connie Chan: $30,783.58 total to “UP2U Print Inc” (about $10,261 per candidate) and $2,050.00 total to “Feld, Peter J” (about $683 per candidate). Response due 11/04. Filing.

Response due 11/04. Filing.

Twenty-five Form 99 filings: RFAI responses, debt forgiveness and a debt-settlement acknowledgment

Two filings (Unity & Justice Fund, Association of State Democratic Committees) contain no text. Gene Douglass for Congress and Reviving American Jobs Again PAC each filed more than once.
