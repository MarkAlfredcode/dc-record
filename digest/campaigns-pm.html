Top Story
House Debates Pulling Troops From Iran Hostilities as Rep. Al Green Forces an Impeachment Vote
A Sept. 14 floor session paired a stalled war-powers resolution with a Texas Democrat's move to compel a vote on removing President Trump.

On Monday, September 14, the House of Representatives considered H. Con. Res. 93, sponsored by Rep. Seth Moulton (D-MA-6), which would direct President Trump, under Section 5(c) of the War Powers Resolution, to remove U.S. armed forces from hostilities with Iran. After debate, the chair announced on a voice vote that the resolution's noes had prevailed, but Rep. Moulton demanded a recorded vote, and the chair postponed further proceedings — meaning the measure remains alive, pending a yeas-and-nays vote at a time not yet announced.

Separately that same day, Rep. Al Green (D-TX-9) formally notified the House of his intent to offer a privileged resolution forcing floor action on H. Res. 1486, which he introduced back on August 24 to impeach President Trump for "high crimes and misdemeanors." Because privileged resolutions can bypass committee referral and force a vote within days, Green's notice sets up a near-term floor fight even though no vote date has been set.

Both developments came during an otherwise heavy legislative day: the House also passed the National Wildlife Refuge System Invasive Species Strike Team Act (371-33) and a military-family leave bill (345-60), and introduced 26 new bills — including one from Rep. Joe Courtney (D-CT-2) that would bar transferring the VC-25B "Bridge Aircraft" (the Qatari-donated jet being retrofitted as a future Air Force One) outside the Department of the Air Force.

Eleven RFAIs Land, Led by a $176,978.93 Question for End Citizens United

The FEC issued eleven Requests for Additional Information in this window; all eleven were read in full. Most are routine compliance nudges — signature omissions, cash-balance mismatches, missing Schedule F totals — but two stand out: a six-figure contribution question for the national Democratic PAC End Citizens United, and a notice warning Sholdon Daniels for the People that it may not have filed its July Quarterly report at all.

Filing.

House Passes Wildlife and Military-Family Bills, Introduces 34 New Measures

The House convened at noon on Monday, September 14 and adjourned at 9:40 p.m. after a full floor day. Beyond the Iran war-powers debate and impeachment notice covered above, members passed the National Wildlife Refuge System Invasive Species Strike Team Act by a 371-33 vote and a military-family leave bill, the Making It Likely for Families of the Military to Live with Leave Access Act, by 345-60.
