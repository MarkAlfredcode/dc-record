Top Story
Seafarers union PAC reports $13,064.50 insurance recovery for "additional misappropriations"
The Federal Election Commission flagged the receipt, filed October 8, as apparent unauthorized use of committee funds.

The Federal Election Commission wrote to Seafarers Political Activity Donation, the political action committee of the Seafarers International Union of North America, on October 8 about its September Monthly report. The report lists a $13,064.50 receipt labeled "Insurance recovery for additional misappropriations found See memo." The Commission said the receipt "appears to be related to an apparent unauthorized use of Committee funds" and that it may take further legal action, while saying any clarification will be considered.

The committee's own label refers to "additional" misappropriations, which implies earlier ones were already found. The letter does not say how much was taken, who took it, or when.

Three other letters in the same batch raise apparent unauthorized spending: Bish for Congress reported debit-card fraud charges of $483.27 and $1,000.00 and Wells Fargo Bank provisional credits of $5,001.21 and $6,192.30; EchoStar Corporation and DISH Network Corporation PAC reported a "DISPUTED ACH DEBIT" of $4,767.41; and James Osyf for Congress reported an "Unauthorized transaction" of $2,058.01.
