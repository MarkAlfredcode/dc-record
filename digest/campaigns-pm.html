Seven large negative transactions surface, including $51,652.89 charged back to Emmer Majority Builders
No new RFAIs or Form 99 filings appeared in this edition's window, so voided and negative transactions are the only new FEC items.

The largest is a $51,652.89 negative Schedule A entry for Emmer Majority Builders tied to Edward E. Martin Jr., dated 07/07/2026 and described as a 2026 aggregate earmarked by WinRed that was charged back. A large negative amount is FEC's convention for a voided, reversed or refunded transaction, and it is often mundane.

The Texas Democratic Party shows three negative entries labeled excessive contribution transfers: $27,500 for Stacy Schusterman, $27,500 for Lynn Schusterman and $15,000 for Susan C. Young, all dated 09/02 to 09/03/2026. Trump 47 Committee, Inc. shows two $22,500 negative earmarked contributions for Lewis S. Pemberton and Tricia Pemberton on 09/25/2026.
