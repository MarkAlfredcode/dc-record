Education Department plans $352 million sole-source follow-on for Accenture's student-loan platforms

The justification cites eight years of incumbent knowledge and about $23 million in switching costs.

Federal Student Aid plans to issue a logical follow-on task order to Accenture Federal Services LLC for the continued operation, maintenance and enhancement of its student, borrower and partner platforms, at an estimated $352,153,142 over up to 27 months, according to a justification posted on SAM.gov.

The document says Accenture has run the systems for more than eight years, holds undocumented knowledge of interfaces and legacy code, and that switching vendors would cost about $23 million in transition costs. The order goes through a National Institutes of Health government-wide contract.

Separately, the General Services Administration's White House Service Center posted two sole-source justifications for urgent repairs at a Joint Base Anacostia-Bolling building it describes as operating under top-secret protocols; the values are redacted.

General Services Administration's White House Service Center picks sole-source contractors for urgent repairs at a Joint Base Anacostia-Bolling building tied to the White House complex
BSA Contractors, LLC; M&M Welding & Fabricators, Inc.  ·  value redacted  ·  urgent need, no time to compete  ·  General Services Administration (White House Service Center)
Two justifications dated October 5 cover masonry restoration and power washing, and heating and cooling repairs, at Building 410 at Joint Base Anacostia-Bolling, which the documents describe as operating under top-secret security protocols. Both say the work must begin on an October date that "cannot be disclosed because of security concerns," and the masonry document says BSA Contractors previously did work at the White House complex, the Blair House and the President's Guest House. The masonry justification itself states the agency plans to market ideas early next fiscal year "to proactively avoid the excessive number of sole source acquisitions that have resulted from urgency."

Air Force extends sole-source U-2 forecasting dashboard with Credence Management Solutions after the prior order lapsed on September 29
Credence Management Solutions, LLC  ·  $3,880,000 estimated  ·  limited-source order (follow-on to a federal supply schedule order)  ·  Department of the Air Force
The follow-on covers development and maintenance of a cloud-based analytics dashboard for U-2 logistics and fleet readiness. The justification says the current order expired September 29, 2026 and that a transition would leave the program without its forecasting tool; it was signed September 14, two weeks earlier, and cites the incumbent's 12 months of proprietary knowledge and a $2.5 million-plus switching cost.

Defense Threat Reduction Agency bridges its nuclear stockpile database contract to General Dynamics Information Technology because the competitive follow-on isn't ready
General Dynamics Information Technology  ·  value redacted  ·  only one company can do it  ·  Defense Threat Reduction Agency
The six-month sole-source bridge, with options up to twelve months, supports the Defense Stockpile Management Systems that maintain the national nuclear weapons stockpile database.
