Top Story

Prison bureau signed four contracts with GEO Reentry in the weeks before Pamela Bondi's successor was confirmed; the company gave $4.4 million to MAGA Inc.

Federal Election Commission records tie the company to large giving to a Trump-world committee.

The Federal Prison System (Bureau of Prisons) awarded four residential reentry and home-confinement contracts, worth about $7.4 million combined, to Geo Reentry Inc between July 22 and August 7, 2026. Todd Blanche was confirmed as the successor to Pamela Bondi at the Department of Justice on August 8, 2026.

The largest is $2,671,092 for a Bronx, New York center, signed August 7, one day before the confirmation. The others are $2,178,408 (Las Vegas, signed August 6), $1,488,823 (Kansas City, Kansas area, option year seven, signed July 22) and $1,070,863 (Brownsville, Texas, option year two, signed July 22).

Federal Election Commission records show the company itself gave $4,413,000 to MAGA Inc. That shows the company's giving, not who owns it. Two of the four awards are option-year exercises on existing contracts.
