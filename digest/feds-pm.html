Top Story

Marine Band wants to rent an Alexandria community college concert hall without competition

The notice of intent covers 12 concerts and 6 recording days from January to August 2027.

The U.S. Marine Band, through the Department of the Navy, intends to award a sole-source contract to Virginia Community College System for the Rachel M. Schlesinger Concert Hall and Arts Center in Alexandria, Virginia.

The agency says only one source is available because outreach events require advance scheduling and venue availability can't be guaranteed otherwise. No value is stated. USAspending shows no federal contract on record under this vendor name.

The U.S. Marine Band intends to sole-source 18 days of rental at the Rachel M.
