Border Patrol builds: seven Customs and Border Protection construction awards total $777,754,316

New stations and checkpoint facilities across Texas, New Mexico and Arizona dominate the late-September contract awards.

U.S. Customs and Border Protection booked seven design-build awards between 2026-09-28 and 2026-09-29, led by $158,000,000 to Environmental Chemical Corporation for a Border Patrol station at Rio Grande City, Texas, and $137,000,000 to Grunley Construction Co., Inc. for one at Eagle Pass, Texas.

The other awards are for stations at Las Cruces, New Mexico and Brackettville, Texas, and checkpoint facilities at Laredo and Sierra Blanca, Texas, and Nogales, Arizona. The USAspending records give the dollar amounts and descriptions but not the funding source.

Separately, the Social Security Administration placed a $130,434,206 order with International Business Machines Corporation for mainframe hardware and storage, replacing an agreement that expired September 30, 2026.

OMB Apportionments

Fifteen apportionment files flagged for oversight holds, rescissions, and $2.9 billion in litigation reserves

The Office of Management and Budget approved 16 new apportionment files in the window of September 26–October 1, 2026. Fifteen contained conditional apportionments—spending holds pending agency spend plans, documented litigation reserves, or other documentation to track programmatic obligations and large outstanding liabilities.

Department of Agriculture apportioned $1,552,545,319 for food for peace title ii grants (12-x-2278), subject to additional notification. According to the footnote: "Funds are apportioned with the understanding that the Department of Agriculture will provide OMB with monthly written updates on total commitments and obligations, each by population, crisis, or subcategory, as appropriate." Notice.
