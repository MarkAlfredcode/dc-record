Top Story

Secret Service expands its National Computer Forensics Institute in Alabama with a one-source furniture order

The same sole-source batch includes three Transportation Security Administration checkpoint-equipment awards and a $90 million ceiling increase for a Defense Logistics Agency contingency contract.

The U.S. Secret Service awarded a General Services Administration schedule delivery order to Business Interiors, Inc. for furniture, design, freight and installation supporting the expansion of the National Computer Forensics Institute in Hoover, Alabama. The notice says the order covers administrative offices, classrooms, an auditorium, lobby, food-service space and conference rooms, and was placed on an "only one source (except brand name)" basis under Federal Acquisition Regulation 8.4. The posted description does not state a dollar amount.

Separately, the Transportation Security Administration posted three justifications for awards made without full and open competition: Boarding Pass Scanners to Desko GmbH, Enhanced Metal Detectors to CEIA USA Limited, and Advanced Imaging Technology to Rohde & Schwarz USA, Inc. And Defense Logistics Agency Distribution intends to raise the ceiling on its Global Distribution Expeditionary Contract with Olgoonik Federal, LLC by $90,000,000, to $315,500,000.
