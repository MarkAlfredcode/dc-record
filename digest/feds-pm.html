General Services Administration quietly sole-sources four White House complex jobs, from security systems to a historic balcony

Notices posted October 5-6 cite security clearances and undisclosed October start dates to skip competition.

The General Services Administration's White House Branch posted four sole-source justifications. Convergint Technologies LLC, which finished an earlier security-systems job in 2024, gets a follow-on design-build contract to upgrade security systems at the White House Complex, the Eisenhower Executive Office Building, the New Executive Office Building and Joint Base Anacostia-Bolling; the justification cites its Top Secret clearance and says other vendors lack clearance and institutional knowledge. The cost estimate is redacted.

Two further justifications cover urgent repairs to Building 410 at Joint Base Anacostia-Bolling: masonry and power washing by BSA Contractors, LLC and HVAC work by M&M Welding & Fabricators, Inc. Both say the work must begin in October on a date that cannot be disclosed for security reasons. A fourth, for DJB Contracting, Inc., covers replacing the deteriorated historic balcony on the Winder Building. All four values are redacted.

General Services Administration's White House Service Center picks two sole-source contractors for urgent repairs to a Joint Base Anacostia-Bolling building
BSA Contractors, LLC; M&M Welding & Fabricators, Inc.  ·  value redacted  ·  urgent need, no time to compete  ·  General Services Administration
One notice covers exterior masonry repair, power washing and repainting of Building 410 at Joint Base Anacostia-Bolling (BSA Contractors, LLC); the other covers HVAC repair and controls work in the same building (M&M Welding & Fabricators, Inc.). Both justifications say the work must start in October on a date that "cannot be disclosed because of security concerns," and the masonry document says other vendors lack White House security clearance. The masonry document also says the agency plans to market ideas with its customer early next fiscal year "to proactively avoid the excessive number of sole source acquisitions that have resulted from urgency such as this one."

Navy extends its Washington-area security guard contract after a protest froze the replacement award
Vendor redacted  ·  value redacted  ·  urgent need, no time to compete  ·  Department of the Navy
The Naval Facilities Engineering Systems Command, Washington, is issuing a bridge modification to the incumbent small-business contractor on the regional security operations contract covering Naval District Washington sites, including Bethesda, Carderock, the Arlington Service Center and the Chesapeake Bay Detachment. The justification says the bridge arose from a timely Government Accountability Office bid protest filed March 13, 2026, which suspended performance on the newly awarded replacement contract (N4008026D1003). It warns a gap could compromise physical security of Department of Defense assets.

Education Department keeps Accenture on student-aid platforms in a $352 million sole-source follow-on order
Accenture Federal Services LLC  ·  $352,153,142 estimated  ·  skipped the competition among existing contract holders  ·  Department of Education
Federal Student Aid will issue a logical follow-on task order of up to 27 months, through the National Institutes of Health's government-wide contract vehicle, for operation, maintenance and enhancement of the platforms used by borrowers, applicants and schools. The justification says Accenture has run the platforms for more than eight years and estimates $23 million in duplicative costs if a new vendor were brought in during a five-month overlap.
