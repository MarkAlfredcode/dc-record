Top Story

General Services Administration gives the Marine Corps' logistics contractor team a $92.6 million no-bid bridge while its replacement contract is competed

The three-month bridge, with one three-month option, keeps the current supply chain contractors working while a new competition runs.

The General Services Administration posted a sole-source justification for a three-month bridge purchase agreement, with one three-month option, for fourth-party logistics (4PL) supply chain services at U.S. Marine Corps locations in the continental United States, Hawaii and Japan. The justification gives an estimated total value of $92,644,112. The vendor is the contractor team that already holds the current agreements: MSC Industrial Direct Co., Inc., L C Industries, Inc. and OSC Solutions, Inc.

Those current agreements (47QSCC21A0004, 0005 and 0006) were awarded under full and open competition, with a stated dollar value of $950,356,271 and a period of performance of July 26, 2021 to July 25, 2026. A six-month option extension runs to January 25, 2027. According to the justification, the bridge is needed during the acquisition phase of the replacement request for quotation, 47QSCC26Q5001, which the agency issued June 15, 2026.

The agency cites its schedule-ordering rules, which allow a sole-source order "in the interest of economy and efficiency because it is a logical follow-on to an FSS order already issued on a competitive basis." The justification is dated October 20, 2026, was posted today, and is redacted.

Three new extensions or bridges today. The largest, the $92.6 million Marine Corps logistics bridge from the General Services Administration, is today's Top Story and not repeated here.

Navy extends Virtual Computing Technology's squadron admin-support contract eight months without competition while protest corrective action continues
Virtual Computing Technology, Inc.  ·  value redacted  ·  urgent need, no time to compete  ·  Naval Supply Systems Command, Fleet Logistics Center San Diego
The justification (J&A 27-240-0001) adds two option periods totaling eight months, a four-month base plus one four-month option, on a cost-plus-fixed-fee basis for Virtual Computing Technology, Inc. It provides administrative and personnel support to Fleet Replacement Squadrons, Air Test and Evaluation Squadrons and the two Navy air type-command headquarters. The Navy says the bridge maintains continuity while it completes corrective action on a protest filed with the Government Accountability Office on June 26, 2025, and covers time for any later protest. The document describes a "complex, multi-phased corrective action process following two GAO protests," and says the incumbent has 215 personnel already in place across 14 squadrons and two headquarters.
