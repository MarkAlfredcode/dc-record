Top Story

Air Force gives Raytheon a $20.7 billion sole-source award for AMRAAM missile lots 41 through 47, citing a 2017 justification that stops at Lot 43

The award notice lists $20,699,334,581 for production, spares and sustainment, and the attached justification names Raytheon Company as the only possible source.

The Department of the Air Force posted an award notice today for AMRAAM (Advanced Medium Range Air-to-Air Missile) Production Lots 41 through 47 under solicitation FA8556-26-C-B001. The awardee is Raytheon Company, the award is dated September 25, 2026, and the amount is $20,699,334,581. The notice says the work covers missile production, variants, spares and sustainment support through Lot 47 to June 2033. It was awarded sole-source under the "only one responsible source" authority.

The reason in the attached justification is a 1997 merger. It says Raytheon Company and Hughes Missile Systems Company merged into Raytheon Missile Systems before the government recouped its investment in a second source, leaving Raytheon as the one contractor able to develop, produce and sustain the missile. It also says the cost of qualifying another source would not be recovered through competition. The dollar estimate for qualifying a second source is redacted in the posted copy.

The justification is a class approval signed September 13, 2017, and it does not obviously cover the award. It supports follow-on sole-source actions "for up to ten years--Lot 34 (FY2020) to Lot 43 (FY2029)" and says it does not go beyond Lot 43. Today's notice describes Lots 41 through 47. The posted documents do not explain how the 2017 approval reaches the later lots. The justification also says the program office expected U.S. missile production to finish at Lot 39 in fiscal 2025, and that the extra lots were approved to give the Air Force and Navy flexibility to buy more.
