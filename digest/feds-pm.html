Top Story

Space Command's planned command-and-control facility will require a builder who can post a $2.5 billion bond

A qualifications form attached to a Defense Department special notice sets a $2.5 billion payment-and-performance bond as the minimum for the design-build contractor.

The U.S. Space Command is seeking a design-build contractor for its Command and Control Facility under a notice titled "Design-Build Contractor (DBC) for Collaborative Delivery," posted under the Department of Defense as a special notice. An attachment, the Minimum Qualifications Form, says the offeror must have "the capacity to provide a project payment and performance bond of a minimum of $2.5 billion (construction value of non-C4IT Work)."

The form is a certification: by submitting it, an offeror attests that each of its minimum-qualification statements is true. The row quoted above is headed "Financial and Bonding Capacity." The form also refers to a "Challenge Statement" and to documentation tying projects to it. The notice carries no price or award estimate in the material reviewed here.

This comes from a file posted October 15 and read for the first time today. The notice's own text did not surface the bonding requirement.
