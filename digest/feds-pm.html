Navy keeps BAE Systems as sole engineer for its nuclear missile fleet, saying a switch would cost three years and $183 million

28 new sole-source justifications today, 18 of them posted under other notice types; 7 of the 28 are extensions or bridges and appear in the Extensions section. 46 repeats from earlier editions are left out. The day is heavy on Navy Strategic Systems Programs follow-on contracts to decades-long incumbents, which is why three of the five Must read cards come from that one office.

Must read
Navy plans to keep BAE Systems as engineer for its Trident and Columbia nuclear missile programs without competition, saying a switch would cost three years and $183 million
BAE Systems Technology Solutions & Services, Inc.  ·  no value given  ·  only one company can do it  ·  Department of the Navy, Strategic Systems Programs
The justification, dated July 28, 2026, approves a cost-plus contract with BAE Systems Technology Solutions & Services, Inc. for systems engineering and integration across the Trident II missile system, the Columbia-class submarine missile compartment and the nuclear-armed sea-launched cruise missile. It calls the work a direct follow-on to two earlier non-competed BAE efforts, from fiscal 2022 and fiscal 2025. The document says competing it would cause "an estimated delay of a minimum of three years and $183M in duplication of cost during transition." No verified contract value appears in the text we have.
Notice  |  J&A document
Navy sole-sources Trident submarine test-equipment support to Unified Industries, the company that has held the role since 1977
Unified Industries Incorporated  ·  $29.93 million over five years, per the document's table  ·  only one company can do it  ·  Department of the Navy, Strategic Systems Programs
Unified Industries Incorporated would provide calibration and test, measurement and diagnostic equipment support for the Ohio, Vanguard, Columbia and Dreadnought classes of ballistic missile submarines. The document says the company has served as program lead coordinator "since 1977" and holds 1,105 inter-laboratory comparison artifacts at a Springfield, Virginia facility; moving them to another controlled site would risk damage. It estimates 12 to 18 months to transfer the work to another source. The planned performance period starts October 1, 2026, a month before the notice was posted.
