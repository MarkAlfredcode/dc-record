Top Story

Air Force plans to hire a private airshow announcer, with no competition, to run Dover Air Force Base's 2027 open house

The sole-source notice names a single individual vendor with no federal contract on record under his name in USAspending.

The Air Force's 436th Contracting Squadron has posted a notice of intent to award a sole-source contract to William David Schultz to serve as air boss, narrator and sound provider for the 2027 First State Airshow and Open House at Dover Air Force Base, Delaware. The notice was posted as a Special Notice. The attached draft performance work statement sets the show for June 12-13, 2027.

The notice's stated reason for skipping competition is that Schultz has more than 200 hours of warbird airshow experience, has run operations with the Thunderbirds, Blue Angels and Snowbirds, and meets specialized FAA and military requirements. No value is stated. USAspending has no federal contract on record under this vendor's name since fiscal 2008.

The draft statement of work goes well beyond directing aircraft. The contractor would produce the show schedule, liaise with the FAA, run the pilot safety briefing, narrate, record promotional announcements for financial sponsors, and supply audio for at least 50,000 spectators along a 5,000-foot crowd line. That includes a control platform of at least 320 square feet, a sound technician on site Friday through Sunday, and generator power at the contractor's expense.
