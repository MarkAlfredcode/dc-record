Top Story

Customs and Border Protection sole-sources legal staffing from Contact Government Services for litigation over the White House's immigration priorities

The justification says the order supports lawsuits challenging the agency's implementation of the Secure America Act and its ability to carry out "the White House's priorities."

U.S. Customs and Border Protection plans to place an order with Contact Government Services, LLC without competition for legal staffing and electronic-discovery services. The work is document review and redaction for its Office of Chief Counsel. According to the signed justification, dated September 18, 2026, the services "are related to pending and reasonably anticipated litigation, including litigation challenging CBP's operational implementation of the Secure America Act's directives and ability to execute the White House's priorities." The order falls under a General Services Administration schedule contract, and the agency's legal basis is unusual urgency. The value is redacted. (70B04C26F00001107, justification)

The document describes reviews of "hundreds of thousands if not millions of files," including body-worn-camera video, cellphone data and forensic images, across review platforms such as Relativity and the Justice Department's Civil Online Relativity Application. It says court deadlines are "inflexible and often compressed" and that missing them can bring "monetary sanctions against the Agency and adverse decisions." It also says the agency "has experienced an unanticipated and unexpected rise in discovery and litigation demands that have strained OCC's resources."

Contact Government Services is a Washington, D.C. firm. USAspending shows a federal contract under its name since September 15, 2023. The justification says the order will be funded with fiscal year 2026 one-year operations and support money, and that the requirement will be solicited under Request for Proposal 20150566.
