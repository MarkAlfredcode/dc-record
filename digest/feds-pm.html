Top Story

Quiet Sunday in contracting: the Air Force asks for weaponized small drones and plans a navigation-warfare lab

No sole-source justifications, nothing tied to the White House or Washington, D.C., and no new apportionments.

The most notable item in today's contracting data is an Air Force Life Cycle Management Center request for information on domestically produced small uncrewed aircraft carrying kinetic (weaponized) payloads. According to the statement of objectives, the Air Force wants production-ready systems in three weight classes: Group 1 (up to 20 pounds), Group 2 (20 to 55 pounds) and Group 3 (55 to 1,320 pounds). The file was posted October 20 and was read today.

Separately, the Air Force Test Center at Arnold Air Force Base asked industry for whitepapers on an Advanced Navigation Simulation Environment Laboratory, an effort to modernize simulation of position, navigation and timing and navigation warfare, using other transaction authority rather than a standard contract.

Two new solicitations were posted; two repeats from earlier editions were left out. Both new ones are written up below.

Air Force Test Center asks industry for whitepapers on a navigation-warfare simulation laboratory
Solicitation  ·  Other transaction authority  ·  Request for whitepapers  ·  Department of the Air Force
The Air Force Test Center at Arnold Air Force Base seeks engineering, technical and integration support to modernize its position, navigation and timing and navigation warfare simulation capability. The request goes to members of the AEDC Velocity Alliance Consortium and other industry partners and uses 10 U.S.C. § 4022 other transaction authority, which sits outside standard contracting rules. No value is given, and the one attached file has not been read yet.
