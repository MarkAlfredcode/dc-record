Army-supporting intelligence task order gets a $152.8 million ceiling increase without competition

The General Services Administration cites simultaneous conflicts and a new Secretary of War task force in raising the ceiling to $567.6 million.

The General Services Administration, acting for the U.S. Army Combat Capabilities Development Command's C5ISR Center, plans to increase the ceiling on its Global Operational Support, Information Insight, and Threat task order by $152,779,419, from $414,857,176 to $567,636,595, using a sole-source exception to fair opportunity. The task order was competitively awarded in May 2024 and runs through May 2029.

The justification says the work expanded because the United States is engaged in or supporting multiple simultaneous conflicts, including Ukraine and Iran, and because of domestic drone incursions in the northeastern United States. It also says the Secretary of War directed a new Joint Interagency Task Force 401, for which this task order was selected as a heavy contributor. The document states that earlier internal funding realignments are exhausted and that the agency is developing a competitive acquisition strategy for a new procurement.

Also today: Immigration and Customs Enforcement is adding a temporary detention structure in Batavia, New York, to an existing contract with Akima Infrastructure Protection, LLC (value redacted), and the Army ordered armed guard services in Erbil, Iraq, from GardaWorld Federal Services before posting its justification.

Immigration and Customs Enforcement adds a temporary detention structure in Batavia, New York, to an existing contract
Akima Infrastructure Protection, LLC  ·  value redacted  ·  only one company can do it  ·  Department of Homeland Security, U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement
Immigration and Customs Enforcement plans to modify an existing detention services contract with Akima Infrastructure Protection, LLC to add a temporary detention structure, medical and environmental support, and armed guards at its Service Processing Center in Batavia, New York, to house up to 100 detainees. The justification says the agency has an increased need for beds in New York because of changes affecting state and local detention space, and that using another vendor would duplicate costs and add security risk. The modification raises the contract's ceiling; the new figure is redacted.

Army orders armed guards for Erbil, Iraq, and justifies the sole-source choice after the fact
GardaWorld Federal Services  ·  value redacted  ·  urgent need, no time to compete  ·  Department of Defense (Army Contracting Command, Rock Island)
On behalf of U.S. Army Central and the Combined Joint Task Force for Operation Inherent Resolve, the Army issued a notice to proceed on September 10, 2026 and awarded an undefinitized task order to GardaWorld Federal Services for armed guard services near Erbil International Airport. This justification is post-award: it was written October 8, after work was already ordered under the multiple-award Global Protective Services contract. The Army cites an urgent need and a compressed 20-day mobilization window.

Immigration and Customs Enforcement buys 366 parking spaces near its Washington, D.C., headquarters from a single garage
Potomac Center CF, LLC  ·  value redacted  ·  only one company can do it  ·  Department of Homeland Security, U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement
Immigration and Customs Enforcement is awarding Potomac Center CF, LLC, at 550 12th Street SW, a 3.5-month base contract for 366 spaces in the Potomac Center South underground garage. The justification says the garage is the only location with a secure direct connection to the agency's headquarters and security modifications for emergency access, and that the agency faces an elevated threat environment including frequent protests around the building. It notes the services have not been procured before.

Quasars  ·  value redacted  ·  only one company can do it  ·  Department of Homeland Security, Transportation Security Administration
