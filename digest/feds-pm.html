Transportation Security Administration shortens a five-year sole-source award to its incumbent after a protest

The agency says it will now competitively rebid the Online Learning Center support work.

The Transportation Security Administration awarded task order 70T01026F7670N008 to incumbent Quasars on August 20, 2026 as a five-year sole-source contract for help desk, project management and IT support of its Online Learning Center, which serves about 70,000 users.

According to the justification, an agency-level protest followed and the agency took corrective action to run a competitive procurement. A modification now cuts the period of performance to no later than May 19, 2027. No dollar value is stated in the document.

Separately, the General Services Administration is justifying sole-source work at the White House Complex, and U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement is adding a temporary detention structure at Batavia, New York; both values are redacted.

Immigration and Customs Enforcement adds a temporary detention structure and armed guards at Batavia, New York, without competition
Akima Infrastructure Protection, LLC  ·  value redacted  ·  only one company can do it  ·  U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement
Enforcement and Removal Operations wants to modify its existing detention contract with Akima Infrastructure Protection, LLC to add a temporary detention structure, supporting medical infrastructure and armed guard services at the Batavia Service Processing Center. The agency says Akima has provided detention and transportation services there since August 1, 2025 and that multiple vendors would create operational and security risks and duplicate costs. The modification would also raise the contract ceiling.

General Services Administration picks its prior contractor for security-system upgrades across the White House Complex
Convergint Technologies LLC  ·  value redacted  ·  only one company can do it  ·  General Services Administration
The General Services Administration's White House branch is justifying a follow-on design-build contract with Convergint Technologies LLC to upgrade system headends at the White House Complex, the Eisenhower Executive Office Building, the New Executive Office Building and other nearby sites, plus Joint Base Anacostia-Bolling. The agency cites Convergint's prior design-build work there (awarded in 2020, completed 2024), its institutional knowledge and its Top Secret facility clearance.

Education Department keeps 144 contract attorneys on student-loan fraud claims in an $81.4 million sole-source extension
Vendor redacted  ·  $81,382,398.35 estimated  ·  logical follow-on  ·  Department of Education
Federal Student Aid is modifying two task orders for contract attorneys who adjudicate borrower defense claims, justified as a logical follow-on to the existing work. The department says changing contractors would waste the onboarding and training of 144 attorneys, and that the original scope proved larger than anticipated, extending timelines. The notice was posted after the modification was awarded; the document refers to a goal of zero backlog by 2028.

White House-area historic Winder Building balcony to be replaced by a single hand-picked contractor
DJB Contracting, Inc  ·  value redacted  ·  only one company can do it  ·  General Services Administration
The General Services Administration's White House branch plans to hire DJB Contracting, Inc without competition to replace the deteriorated wood balcony on the historic Winder Building, citing the firm's 10-plus years of historic-restoration work and prior work on the building. The justification describes the existing decking and joists as unsafe, warped or missing in places.
