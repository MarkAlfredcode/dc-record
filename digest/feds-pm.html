Drug Enforcement Administration rewrites 'Artificial Intelligence' as 'Super Intelligence' in a technology modernization request, citing Executive Order 14434

An amendment to the agency's request for information swaps the term throughout, according to the document itself.

Amendment 0002 to the Drug Enforcement Administration's request for information on the enterprise modernization of its Diversion Control Division's information technology systems says: "In accordance with Executive Order 14434: Super Intelligence, all references to Artificial Intelligence (AI) have been changed to Super Intelligence (SI)." The amendment is dated October 21, 2026, and also says the question period has closed and that questions and answers are in its Attachment 2.

The finding comes from a file read today on a notice (D-27-DC-91626) posted earlier. The posted amendment cites Executive Order 14434 as the reason for the wording change. This edition has not independently checked what that order says, and the file does not say how the change affects the requirements.

Water commission's operations contract for the South Bay Tijuana sewage plant comes with a pollution order and a 50-million-gallon expansion plan

There are 6 new solicitations, and 170 repeats from earlier editions were left out. The items below come from documents read today on notices posted earlier.

Operations contract for the South Bay wastewater plant attaches a state cleanup order and an expansion plan
Solicitation  ·  International Boundary and Water Commission
The International Boundary and Water Commission is seeking operations and maintenance services for the South Bay International Wastewater Treatment Plant. An attached pollution-permit order says the commission began treating over 25 million gallons a day of Tijuana wastewater in September 2025. It says the San Diego Regional Water Quality Control Board adopted a Cease and Desist Order on August 27, 2025, which "found that the 10-MGD expansion threatened to violate USIBWC's NPDES permit." The same order says a design-build expansion to 50 million gallons a day of full secondary treatment is under way, with a target completion of December 2027 communicated by commission and U.S. Environmental Protection Agency leadership. The file was posted September 25, 2026 and read today.
