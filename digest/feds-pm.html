Pension Benefit Guaranty Corporation sole-sources five investment-management contracts in one day

The federal pension insurer posted other-than-full-and-open-competition justifications for contracts with five Wall Street asset managers, all awarded September 25.

The Pension Benefit Guaranty Corporation posted five justifications for other than full and open competition on SAM.gov for contracts awarded September 25, 2026: Pacific Investment Management Company LLC (16PBGC26P0010), PGIM, Inc. (16PBGC26P0011), Wellington Management Company LLP (16PBGC26P0008), Allspring Global Investments LLC (16PBGC26P0012) and Goldman Sachs Asset Management (16PBGC26P0009).

The postings say only that each is a justification for other than full and open competition and refer readers to an attached document. The dollar values, the services purchased and the specific rationale are not in the posting text we reviewed, so this edition does not characterize them. The contract numbers run consecutively from P0008 to P0012.

Elsewhere in the sole-source notices, the Defense Logistics Agency posted two modifications to its Global Distribution Expeditionary Contract with Olgoonik Federal, LLC that would add $190,000,000 to the contract ceiling in total (reaching $415,500,000 in the later notice), and the Transportation Security Administration posted four justifications for airport screening equipment.
