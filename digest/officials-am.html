Top Story
WPP's Gulf Hill & Knowlton registers to run Abu Dhabi's U.S. tourism campaign
The new FARA registrant is a branch of a WPP plc subsidiary, and its foreign principal is the Abu Dhabi government's culture and tourism department.

Gulf Hill & Knowlton WLL (Creative Zone Branch) registered under the Foreign Agents Registration Act on October 22, 2026 (registration #7784). Its foreign principal is the United Arab Emirates, Department of Culture and Tourism - Abu Dhabi. The Exhibit AB checks the box identifying the principal as a government of a foreign country. Exhibit AB.

In the registration statement, the registrant describes itself as a branch under Gulf Hill and Knowlton CO. W.L.L., an indirect subsidiary of WPP plc. It lists one individual rendering services for the principal, Jessica Emily Margaret Macdonald, who filed a short-form registration. Her services are described as communications support for the principal's tourism campaign in the United States, including management of a content partnership between AFAR Media and the foreign principal.
