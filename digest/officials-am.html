Top Story
Rep. Dan Crenshaw still has no 2025 annual financial disclosure on file, 76 days past his final extension
A House check of every sitting member found one missing CY2025 annual report past every extension.

Rep. Dan Crenshaw (R-TX-2) filed for an extension on 6/4/2026. His final extension date was 8/13/2026, and no CY2025 annual financial disclosure is on file 76 days later, according to the Deadline Watch check against the Clerk of the House index and Senate eFD.

The check is partial. The Senate annual-report check and the Senate extensions check both failed with a parsing error. A filing under an unexpected spelling of a name can also read as missing, so the result rests on the House index search alone.

Separately, the Ethics Agreement Tracker lists 7 confirmed officials with no posted certification of ethics-agreement compliance, the longest of them Michelle White Bowman, Governor and Vice Chairman for Supervision of the Federal Reserve System Board of Governors, 421 days past her 9/2/2025 due date.

Deadlines are counted from the confirmation date on congress.gov plus the agreement's stated window (usually 90 days) -- OGE can grant extensions that aren't public, so treat each flag as a lead to confirm, not a finding. OGE posts certifications with a lag, so nothing inside 30 days of a deadline is flagged. A certification filed but not yet posted by OGE will read as missing: confirm with the agency before publishing.
