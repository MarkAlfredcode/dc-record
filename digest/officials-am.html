Top Story
Lobbying firm with a former Trump White House aide registers for a Greek state-linked client on energy security
Capitol Counsel LLC filed a new registration for PPC North America Corporation that lists Greek government entities as foreign entities.

Capitol Counsel LLC registered to lobby for PPC North America Corporation, an energy company, on U.S. energy security, critical infrastructure, digital infrastructure investment and global competitiveness. It will lobby the executive branch and Congress on Energy/Nuclear, Foreign Relations and Utilities issues. Registration.

The filing lists two foreign entities: the Hellenic Corporation of Assets and Participations S.A. and the Hellenic Republic (Minister of Economy and Finance). Its covered positions include George Sifakis, who is described as Assistant to the President of the United States and Director of the Office of Public Liaison under President Donald Trump in 2017, and Martin Gold, a floor advisor and counsel to Senate Majority Leader Bill Frist.

Nothing else in today's input involves a text, call or meeting with the President, Vice President or a cabinet official on behalf of a foreign government. The one OGE item is six Certificates of Divestiture filed October 29 with no details yet.
