Top Story
Lobbyist listing a White House post as a covered position registers $425,000 of quarterly income from two tech clients on 'trade restrictions'
John McEntee's third-quarter reports show Tencent America and Zhongji Innolight as paying clients.

Two third-quarter reports filed under John McEntee list a combined $425,000 in income. Tencent America LLC, described as a video game and technology company, paid $225,000, and the report gives "No specific issues" under trade. Zhongji Innolight Co. Ltd, described as a maker of optical transceiver modules and interconnects for cloud and AI networks, paid $200,000. Its report says the lobbying concerned "Trade restrictions."

Both filings list McEntee's covered position as "Assistant to the President." The filings do not say which agencies or officials were contacted, and they report no other lobbyists.

Nothing else in today's input is bigger. The two FARA contact-log filings that mention President Donald Trump only reference his policies, not communications with him. The OGE summary describes routine compliance certifications and one ethics agreement with divestitures.
