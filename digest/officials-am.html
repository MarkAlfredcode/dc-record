OGE Ethics Filings
Zisk commits to extensive divestitures for Postal Regulatory Commission appointment

Three new ethics filings were submitted on October 7, including agreements and compliance certifications for presidential appointees. The documents confirm executive-branch ethics commitments including asset divestitures, position resignations, and financial recusals designed to prevent conflicts of interest.

  • Jeffrey Zisk, nominated as Commissioner of the Postal Regulatory Commission, submitted an Ethics Agreement committing to divest from Cart.com, Inc., Surrey Ventures LLC, Capisco LLC, FedEx Corp, Walmart Inc., and two private investment funds (CIC V and Apollo Accord+ II Fund, L.P.) within 90 days of confirmation. The agreement also requires resignation from his position with the Prostate Cancer Foundation and from trustee posts, while maintaining passive-only interests in certain entities during his tenure.
  • Preston W. Griffith, U.S. Representative to the Vienna Office of the United Nations and U.S. Representative to the International Atomic Energy Agency at the Department of State, submitted a Certification of Ethics Agreement Compliance dated September 23, 2026, confirming completion of all required resignations, divestitures, and ethics agreement obligations as of his August 7 confirmation.
  • William Makaneole, Ambassador to Turkmenistan at the Department of State, submitted an Ethics Agreement that includes a recusal from matters involving the University of Kentucky, where his spouse is employed, along with standard ethics commitments regarding financial interests and conflicts of interest.

House members who filed 6 or more periodic transaction reports (STOCK Act trade disclosures) in the year before the last 90 days and none since, per the House Clerk's disclosure index. Silence doesn't say why: the member may have sold off holdings, moved assets into a blind trust or index fund, been covered by a new law, or just not traded -- and trades are reportable up to 45 days late, so the latest ones may not be filed yet.
