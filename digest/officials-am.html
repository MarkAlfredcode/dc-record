Lobbyists logged emails and a July 14 meeting for Thailand with Chief of Staff Susie Wiles, plus requests for Rubio and Lutnick
A single Continental Strategy supplemental statement lists a string of Thai government requests to the White House, State Department and Commerce Department.

Continental Strategy LLC reports in a FARA supplemental statement (stamped Sept. 30) that it worked for the Royal Thai Government (Thailand), through the Office of Commercial Affairs at the Royal Thai Embassy on behalf of the Ministry of Commerce. The log shows a 07/14/2026 meeting between Susie Wiles, White House Chief of Staff, and Deputy Prime Minister Suphajee Suthumpun. On 07/08/2026 it shows an email meeting request for Wiles sent to her special assistant, Sarah Schuringa. It also shows a same-day email request for a meeting between Secretary of State Marco Rubio and the Deputy Prime Minister, sent to Virginia Hinzman, listed as scheduler. Filing.

The same log shows an emailed request on 04/30/2026 for a pull-aside with Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick and an email on 07/16/2026 to Lutnick, logged as a meeting request. Its entries for the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of the Co-operative Republic of Guyana include a 07/13/2026 emailed meeting request for Rubio or Deputy Secretary Landau (as the filing reads). The OCR interleaves the log columns, so each date and recipient is reported as the text reads.
