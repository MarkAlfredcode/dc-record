Squire Patton Boggs Registers 11 New Lobbying Clients in a Single Day
The firm accounts for more than a third of the 29 new LDA registrations posted since October 1.

Squire Patton Boggs filed new registrations for clients ranging from Afodina, LLC, which the filing says represents Pakistani critical mineral mines seeking U.S. government funding, to Terminus Industrials Inc., S2G Investments, the Plastics Industry Association and Hyperliquid Policy Center. Issues span defense, energy, trade and transportation.

Separately, TSG Advocates DC, LLC registered five clients, including firearms maker KelTec.

LDA Registrations & Activity
Squire Patton Boggs Signs 11 New Clients in One Day Among 29 New Lobbying Registrations

The Senate lobbying database shows 29 new registrations posted since October 1, 2026, out of 192 total LDA filings in the window: 128 third-quarter reports, 19 third-quarter no-activity reports, 7 terminations (including 2 second-quarter), plus a handful of amendments. Squire Patton Boggs accounts for 11 of the new registrations and TSG Advocates DC, LLC for 5.

One registration, by Pretty Good Policy for Zcash (self-registered), cites H.R. 3633, the Digital Asset Market Clarity Act, among its issues. Another, Squire Patton Boggs for Afodina, LLC, describes representing Pakistani critical mineral mines seeking U.S. government funding.
