Top Story
Two Texas Republicans are late on 2025 annual financial disclosures
Rep. Michael T. McCaul filed 62 days after his final extension, and Rep. Dan Crenshaw has still filed nothing 65 days after his.

Rep. Michael T. McCaul (R-TX-10) filed his calendar-year 2025 annual disclosure on October 14, 2026, 62 days after his final extension date. That makes it a late disclosure.

Rep. Dan Crenshaw (R-TX-2) filed an extension on June 4, 2026. The Deadline Watch check found no annual report on file for him, 65 days after his final extension date of August 13, 2026. That check compares the Clerk of the House index and Senate eFD, and a filing under an unexpected name spelling can read as missing.
