Top Story
Two sitting House members still have no 2025 annual financial disclosure on file, 64 days past their final extensions
Rep. Michael T. McCaul and Rep. Dan Crenshaw, both of Texas, filed extension requests this spring and have not filed since.

Rep. Michael T. McCaul (R-TX-10) filed an extension request on 4/29/2026. Rep. Dan Crenshaw (R-TX-2) filed one on 6/4/2026. The final extension date for both was 8/13/2026, and neither has a CY2025 annual financial disclosure on file, now 64 days past that date. The finding comes from a check of every sitting member's filings in the Clerk of the House index and Senate eFD.

The script behind the finding warns that a filing under an unexpected spelling of a name can read as missing. Members sworn in after Nov. 1 of the report year owe no annual report and were excluded.

Separately, the Ethics Agreement Tracker lists seven Senate-confirmed officials with no posted certification of ethics-agreement compliance, more than 30 days past deadline. The longest overdue is Michelle White Bowman, Governor and Vice Chairman for Supervision of the Federal Reserve System Board of Governors, confirmed 6/4/2025 with a certification due 9/2/2025, 409 days ago.
