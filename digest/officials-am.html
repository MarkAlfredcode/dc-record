Top Story
Rep. Dan Crenshaw still has no 2025 annual financial disclosure on file, 67 days past his final extension
It is the one sitting member the Deadline Watch flags as missing a CY2025 annual report past every extension.

Rep. Dan Crenshaw (R-TX-2) filed for an extension on 6/4/2026. His final extension date was 8/13/2026, and no calendar-year 2025 annual financial disclosure appears in the Clerk of the House index or Senate eFD. That leaves the report 67 days overdue.

The Deadline Watch notes that a filing under an unexpected spelling of a name can look like a missing one, so the absence reflects the indexes checked and not a confirmed failure to file.

Separately, seven confirmed officials still lack a posted certification of ethics-agreement compliance, the longest outstanding being Michelle White Bowman, Governor and Vice Chairman for Supervision of the Federal Reserve System Board of Governors, at 412 days past due. The details are in the Ethics Agreement Tracker below.
