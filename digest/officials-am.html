OGE Ethics Filings
New appointees file ethics certifications as two officials complete required divestitures

The White House Office and the National Credit Union Administration received ethics documents from senior officials on October 8, while the Departments of Justice and State confirmed divestiture certificates for appointees. The Department of Agriculture's newly confirmed Under Secretary for Trade & Foreign Agricultural Affairs filed both an ethics agreement and a routine financial disclosure request.

  • Leslie Wulff at the Department of Justice filed a Certificate of Divestiture (OGE-2026-160); only the fact of the divestiture is confirmed, not the asset.
  • Eric Wendt at the Department of State filed a Certificate of Divestiture (OGE-2026-161); only the fact of the divestiture is confirmed, not the asset.
  • Donald J Trump, President of the United States at the White House Office, filed a 278 Transaction Report disclosing periodic purchases of municipal bonds and corporate securities throughout August 2026, including positions in infrastructure and utility bonds across multiple states, and equity purchases in financial services and technology firms.
  • John Crews, Member of the National Credit Union Administration Board, filed a Certification of Ethics Agreement Compliance certifying that he completed all required resignations and divestitures within the timeframes specified in his ethics agreement, with a confirmation date of August 7, 2026.
  • Gregory A Ibach, Under Secretary for Trade & Foreign Agricultural Affairs at the Department of Agriculture, filed an Ethics Agreement committing to resign from his position with the Aksarben Foundation upon confirmation, maintain his trustee position in Family Trust #1 without compensation, cease consulting services, retain passive investment income from GTI Inc., maintain fixed-rate farmland leases, and recuse from matters involving his spouse's employer.
