Top Story
Maritime union PAC discloses years-long embezzlement by former treasurer, partial bank restitution
Seafarers Political Activity Donation says ex-Treasurer Chelsea Diab diverted committee disbursements into her own account and falsified reports to the FEC; Capital One clawed back some of the money from her personal bank and returned it to the committee.

Seafarers Political Activity Donation — the political fund of the Seafarers International Union of North America-AGLIW — filed an FEC Form 99 on September 23 responding to four pending Requests for Additional Information, and used it to disclose a years-long embezzlement scheme by its former treasurer. The committee says Chelsea Diab "undertook a years-long embezzlement of Committee funds and falsifications of Committee reports to the Commission," concealing her conduct from other personnel at the union, which employed her.

The committee has now amended every report it filed between February 2025 and May 2026 to correct disbursement entries Diab allegedly falsified — payments the reports had listed as going to Fairwater Tanker or to Fetterman for PA that were instead deposited into Diab's personal bank account. The specific corrected entries span April 14, May 8, May 12, May 16, July 10 and July 11 of 2025.

Separately, the committee's July 2026 Monthly Report discloses a partial recovery: Capital One, the bank that had allowed the fraudulently endorsed or unendorsed checks to clear, worked with the committee to claw back funds from Wells Fargo, which held Diab's personal account, before she had removed the balance entirely. Capital One then remitted the recovered amount to the committee — restitution, the committee notes, that came "albeit not with her participation." The committee says internal and external investigations are continuing and it will keep the FEC apprised of further findings.
