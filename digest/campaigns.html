Top Story
After FEC tells Emmett Lee White to refile on Form 3, two Form 99s arrive carrying Form 3P reports, one headed 'Cornel West for President'
The two Form 99 uploads have identical cycle-to-date dollar figures, and one was filed under a different committee ID than the presidential campaign it covers.

The FEC's Reports Analysis Division sent Emmett Lee White's presidential committee (C00931535) a letter dated October 18. It says the report the letter calls a July Quarterly (08/15/2026 to 9/30/2026, received 10/09/2026) is 'not acceptable as filed' and must be resubmitted on FEC Form 3. The letter adds that a Form 99 is for narrative responses and is not to be used in place of the Form 3. Responses are due 11/23/2026.

Two Form 99s followed on October 18. The first, filed under the White committee's own ID, is a 10-page document headed Form 3P for Cornel West for President (C00843508). It names treasurer Josiah McKinney, covers 07/01 to 09/30/2026, is dated 10/15/2026, and shows zero receipts and disbursements for the period. It lists $72,604.55 in debts to three creditors: Financial Innovations, Inc ($43,424.55, merchandise), Law Office of Aaron S. Foldenauer ($7,000, legal services) and Weinberg Partners LTD ($22,180 across two entries, accounting and compliance).

The second Form 99, filed under a different committee ID, Emmett White (C00880609), is a 15-page Form 3P for Emmett Lee White for President (C00931535) covering 07/31 to 10/18/2026. Its cycle-to-date figures match the Cornel West document line for line: $50,324.59 in contributions, $52,115.61 in total receipts and $64,620.24 in total disbursements. Its debts line shows $7,260.55 instead of $72,604.55, and it lists one $25 itemized receipt dated 09/23/2026. None of the filings explains the overlap, and the FEC has not commented.

Sitting Lawmakers
Two sitting House members running for Senate drew FEC letters

Both letters are dated October 18 and carry a 11/23/2026 response deadline. One concerns apparently excessive contributions and the other concerns cycle-to-date totals that do not add up.

  • FEC tells Rep. Harriet M. Hageman (R-WY) that contributions on her 12-day pre-primary report appear to exceed the limits. The letter to Hageman for Wyoming (treasurer Jason Young) cites Schedule A of the report covering 07/01 to 07/29/2026 and says excessive gifts can be kept only if redesignated or reattributed within 60 days, otherwise refunded. The attached list of contributions was not in the text provided, so no amounts or donors are available. Response due 11/23. (RFAI)
  • FEC asks Rep. Julia Letlow (R-LA) to correct cycle-to-date totals on six lines of her July Quarterly report. The letter to Julia Letlow for Louisiana (treasurer Shea Thomas) says Column B on Lines 6(a), 6(c), 11(a)(i), 11(a)(iii), 11(e) and 16 should equal the prior report's Column B plus this report's Column A. It covers 06/08 to 06/30/2026 and asks for amendments to all later reports affected. Response due 11/23. (RFAI)
Requests for Additional Information
Eleven more FEC letters, led by a $1.94 million self-loan gap and a run of joint-fundraising disclosure problems

Eleven RFAIs from non-lawmakers arrived, all dated October 18 with responses due 11/23/2026. They are grouped by type below. Three letters share the same joint-fundraising flaw, and three involve candidate committees whose Statement of Organization problems are basic. Thomas Datwyler is the treasurer on one of these letters and also signs a Form 99 below.

  • FEC asks Casey for Congress to add the interest rate on three self-loans from candidate Casey Askar totaling $1,940,000. The Florida open-seat House campaign (treasurer Elizabeth Curtis) reported loans of $640,000 on 8/10/2026, $300,000 on 9/22/2026 and $1,000,000 on 9/28/2026 on Schedule C of its October Quarterly report. (Candidate committee) (RFAI)
  • FEC asks the Nebraska Democratic Party about $70,000 in transfers from 'Powell Victory Fund' that appear to be joint fundraising proceeds. The fund is not listed as a joint fundraising representative on the party's Statement of Organization. A second item says 24-hour independent expenditure reports naming 'Winning Connections' as payee do not match the Schedule E entries on the September Monthly report. (Party committee, treasurer Ted Kessler) (RFAI)
  • FEC asks the New York State Democratic Committee about contributions above the $10,000 annual limit and $20,000 in transfers from 'Friends of State Democratic Parties'. The letter concerns the amended July Monthly report (06/01 to 06/30/2026, received 08/31/2026). The transfers look like joint fundraising proceeds, but the committee is not listed as a joint fundraising representative. (Party committee, treasurer David Alpert) (RFAI)
  • FEC asks Ready to Fight PAC about $7,000 in transfers from 'Walkinshaw Victory Fund' that appear to come from joint fundraising. The fund is not disclosed as a joint fundraising representative on the PAC's Statement of Organization. The letter covers the July Quarterly report (04/01 to 06/30/2026). (PAC, treasurer Robert Haley) (RFAI)
  • FEC asks the Missouri Republican State Committee-Federal whether contributions from unregistered organizations used permissible funds. A second item asks whether employees paid under 'Payroll' and 'Payroll Tax' spent 25% or less of their time on federal election activity, since otherwise only federal funds may be used. A further point says 'Booth Rental' and 'Tent Rental' payments appear on the wrong line of the Detailed Summary Page. The letter covers the September Monthly report (08/01 to 08/31/2026). (Party committee, treasurer Jennifer Finch) (RFAI)
  • FEC asks Unrigged PAC to explain what 'Administrative Services' disbursements on its October Quarterly report were for. The letter says the description is too vague for Schedule B Line 21(b). The report covers 07/01 to 09/30/2026. (PAC, treasurer Gracie Freeman) (RFAI)
  • FEC tells Because Real Americans Never Doubt Our Nation (BRANDON PAC) that the 9/30/26 qualification date on its multicandidate status form looks incorrect. The letter says the date must be the last date the committee met all multicandidate requirements, and asks for an amended Form 1M if it qualifies. The same treasurer, Thomas Datwyler, signs the Joshua McKee for Congress filing in the Form 99 section. (PAC) (RFAI)
  • FEC asks Marshall & Blue Origin Investments to rename the committee because it lacks the candidate's name. The Alabama Senate committee of Democratic candidate David McCullough Cash must amend its Statement of Organization to include the candidate's name. (Candidate committee, treasurer David McCullough Cash) (RFAI)
  • FEC tells Party of World Fair that its presidential committee name omits the candidate's name and that it checked multiple committee types. The committee (treasurer Constance Johnson) supports candidate Constance 'Connie' Johnson and must amend its Statement of Organization to name one committee type. (Candidate committee) (RFAI)
  • FEC asks the Warren County Republican Committee to confirm it is independent of the Ohio Republican Party State Central & Executive Committee. The Statement of Organization says the local party committee is not affiliated with the state party, but the letter cites the presumption that contributions from state and subordinate party committees are treated as made by one committee. (Party committee) (RFAI)
  • FEC tells presidential candidate Emmett Lee White to refile his report on Form 3. The letter says the report it calls a July Quarterly (08/15 to 9/30/2026, received 10/09/2026) is not acceptable as filed and that a Form 99 cannot substitute for the Form 3. See the Top Story for the Form 99s filed afterward. (Candidate committee, treasurer Emmett Lee White) (RFAI)
Form 99 Correspondence
Fifteen Form 99s: mostly 'below threshold' notices, plus RFAI responses, a foreign-donor refund explanation and a dissolved campaign

Seven are below-threshold or zero-activity statements around the October Quarterly deadline. Two answer RFAIs, two explain donor problems at one campaign, two are the White filings described in the Top Story, and two announce a campaign slowdown or dissolution.

  • Emmett Lee White's committee files a Form 99 containing a Form 3P headed 'Cornel West for President'. The 10-page document, treasurer Josiah McKinney, covers 07/01 to 09/30/2026, shows no activity for the period and lists $72,604.55 in debts. (Form 99)
  • Emmett White (C00880609) files a Form 99 containing a 15-page Form 3P for Emmett Lee White for President. Its cycle-to-date totals match the Cornel West document, and it lists one $25 itemized receipt dated 09/23/2026. (Form 99)
  • The Republican Party of Iowa tells the FEC that $1,561.52 of David Barker's contribution was reattributed to his spouse and that Vernon Hill was refunded in October. It also says a contribution from ADM PAC went to its state account, as the PAC's 'Nonfederal contribution' purpose indicated. The response covers the August Monthly report (07/01 to 07/31/2026) and says the fixes will show on the pre-general and post-general filings. The letter is signed by treasurer David Barker, the same name as the contributor, and the filing does not say whether they are the same person. (Form 99)
  • Joshua McKee for Congress tells the FEC its negative cash-on-hand balance reflects uncleared checks. The Alabama open-seat campaign says its register balance is negative but the bank account is not overdrawn. It is answering an RFAI on the October Quarterly report (07/23 to 09/30/2026). Treasurer Thomas Datwyler also signs the BRANDON PAC filing. (Form 99)
  • Sam Husseini for Congress says it refunded $250 from three donors with addresses outside the United States. Two donors who said they were US citizens gave $220 total (one gave twice) but sent no passport copy and were refunded 10/3/2026. A UK citizen's $30 gift was refunded 9/29/2026. The Maryland Green Party challenger's committee notes the refunds fell outside the reporting period. (Form 99)
  • Sam Husseini for Congress says it amended its October 15 report to add three omitted donations totaling $255. The gifts of $200, $5 and $50 arrived after 8:30pm on September 30 and its donation software left them out of the export. It also deleted a duplicate contributor entry and updated employer and occupation data for one donor. (Form 99)
  • Presidential candidate Neil G. Leeds tells the FEC he has raised no funds and is slowing his campaign. Leeds for President (Republican, Torrance, California) says he is entering full-time litigation against a bank and will wait for the midterm elections. He says he will report any funds deposited. (Form 99)
  • Sandi Stevens for Congress tells the FEC the campaign 'has been dissolved and no longer exists'. The committee was a Democratic open-seat House campaign in the District of Columbia. The filing is a Form 99, not a termination report. (Form 99)
  • Committee to Elect Derek Hawn for Congress says it has not passed the $5,000 reporting threshold and is not taking donations. The Tennessee Democratic challenger says he was redistricted out of District 3, has received no contributions as of September 30, 2026, and posted the statement for the October Quarterly record. (Form 99)
  • Javi Andrade for Congress says it has not raised or spent more than $5,000 by the October Quarterly coverage end date. The Texas Democratic open-seat House campaign filed the one-line statement. (Form 99)
  • Michael Robinson for Congress says it has not raised or spent more than $5,000 through September 30, 2026. The Pennsylvania Democratic challenger says no October Quarterly report is required and filed the notice for the public record. (Form 99)
  • Committee to Elect Pamela Moses tells the FEC it has '$0 to report'. The Tennessee independent Senate challenger gave no further detail. (Form 99)
  • Amplify Progress PAC says its activity did not exceed the registration threshold from 10/01 to 10/14/2026. It was filed on 10/17 within minutes of two near-identical notices from other PACs below. (Form 99)
  • Copper State Progress says its activity did not exceed the registration threshold from 10/01/2024 to 10/14/2026. It was filed on 10/17, three minutes after the Amplify Progress PAC notice. (Form 99)
  • 365 PAC says its activity did not exceed the registration threshold from 09/09 to 10/14/2026. The statement cites ID C00867242, which is Copper State Progress's ID, not 365 PAC's own C00961201. (Form 99)
