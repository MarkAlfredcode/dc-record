Top Story
Nebraska House hopeful reports $263,006 in personal money on TV ads, correcting a $270,000 48-hour report
Kishla Askins’ committee, which has terminated, told the Federal Election Commission on September 30 that two in-kind contributions were never reported on 48-hour notices.

Kishla Askins for Congress, the committee of Kishla Askins (D-NE-2, an open-seat candidate), filed a Form 99 on 09/30/2026 answering an apparent Request for Additional Information (the visible text begins at item 2). It says the candidate made two in-kind contributions that were not covered by 48-hour reports: $3,525 on 5/2/2026 and $32,525.94 on 5/1/2026.

It also says the 48-Hour Report filed 5/5/2026 for a $270,000 in-kind contribution from the candidate was wrong; the correct figure, reported on the Termination Report, is $263,006. That money paid MLZ / Canal Media of Atlanta for TV ads on 5/5/2026. The response also itemizes personal-funds spending including $32,525.94 and $3,525 to Scale to Win for texting services, $850 to Dan Woodward for an event speaker/headliner, $450 to Mary Broughton for canvassing support, and small LinkedIn advertising charges.

Nothing in the letter suggests misconduct; it reads as a reconciliation of candidate-funded spending. The treasurer on file is Alison Thompson. The filing was received 09/30, within the last two days.
