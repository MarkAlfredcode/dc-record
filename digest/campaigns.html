Top Story
Texas House candidate Shelly DeZevallos forgives $442,799.75 in personal loans to her campaign
The Republican open-seat candidate's letter to the FEC is the largest of three candidate loan-forgiveness filings received this week.

Shelly DeZevallos, the Republican candidate for an open Texas House seat, told the FEC that she has "forgiven all personal loans made to my campaign committee" Shelly for Congress, in the amount of $442,799.75. The letter is dated October 14, 2026 and was received by the FEC on October 26. It is signed by DeZevallos and lists Steven Martin as treasurer. It does not say why the loans were forgiven or what the committee's other debts or cash on hand are.

Two smaller candidate loan-forgiveness letters arrived the same day. Justin Story, a Republican challenger in Florida, forgave $23,885.64 he had loaned Justin Story for Congress. Michael Foley, a Republican challenger in Minnesota, forgave $3,272.03 still owed on a $6,000 loan to Mike Foley for Congress. His letter is dated August 25 and says the amount will be reported in the committee's Termination Report. DeZevallos's figure is roughly 19 times Story's.

Separately, the FEC's PDFs for all 19 new RFAIs, including those to the Senate campaigns of Roy Cooper (North Carolina), Michael Whatley (North Carolina) and John E. Sununu (New Hampshire), could not be downloaded today. Their contents are unknown.

Form 99 Correspondence
Three candidates forgive loans, a Florida Libertarian reports little activity, and five filings are unreadable

Nine Form 99 miscellaneous submissions were received October 26 and 27. Three are candidate loan-forgiveness letters, one is a short letter from a Libertarian candidate saying her campaign has stayed below $5,000, and one appears to be a paper-filed Form 3 whose scan is mostly illegible. The other four could not be downloaded.

  • Shelly for Congress tells the FEC that candidate Shelly DeZevallos has forgiven $442,799.75 in personal loans to the committee. The letter is dated October 14 and was received 10/26. Steven Martin is the treasurer. (Form 99)
  • Justin Story for Congress tells the FEC that Republican candidate Justin Story forgives $23,885.64 in personal loans to his Florida campaign. The letter says he is forgiving all outstanding debts owed to him, and it was received 10/26. (Form 99)
  • Mike Foley for Congress tells the FEC that Michael Foley forgives the remaining $3,272.03 of a $6,000 personal loan. The letter says he made a partial repayment on August 24, 2026 and that the forgiveness will be reported in the committee's Termination Report. It is dated August 25 and was received 10/26. (Form 99)
  • Groves for Congress tells the FEC that its campaign has neither received nor spent more than $5,000. The letter is from Libertarian candidate Ralph Groves of Florida, dated October 15, and is titled a "Financial Disclosure Statement" citing the Ethics in Government Act. It is copied to the House Committee on Ethics and to Joe Hannoush of the Libertarian Party of Florida. Received 10/26. (Form 99)
  • Committee to Elect Heather Shelton submits what appears to be a paper Form 3 report of receipts and disbursements. The 10-page scan is mostly illegible handwriting, so no figures could be verified. An FEC envelope replacement page is attached. Received 10/26. (Form 99)
