Top Story
No Labels PAC reports $7.79 million in member contributions steered to candidates of both parties
A Form 99 filed October 20 says members gave through the Democracy Engine platform after solicitations made under the group's internal communication allowance.

No Labels PAC told the FEC that during the current reporting period it used its "internal communication" allowance to solicit No Labels members to contribute directly to aligned candidates through the Democracy Engine platform. The filing says members gave a total of $7,786,591.11 in the period. It lists the amount going to each of roughly 35 candidates and committees, including $47,775 to Rep. Jared Moskowitz, $20,675 to Rep. Thomas Suozzi and $19,750 to Rep. Hillary Scholten.

The filing also gives cycle-to-date totals for the 2026 election cycle. The largest are $726,028.81 for Sen. Susan Collins, $710,845.38 for Sen. Bill Cassidy, $409,602.90 for Rep. Josh Gottheimer and $358,051.72 for Rep. Mike Lawler. The list also includes the Lawler Victory Fund and LA Victory ($297,000).

The five-page filing is a plain list of figures and does not say how the funds were routed or whether any compliance question prompted it. The amounts are the committee's own statement, not an FEC finding.

The other notable item from today is a May-to-October-cycle compliance request to Club for Growth PAC, whose amended August Monthly report drew questions about apparent corporate contributions, $19,000 in earmarked contributions with no matching receipts, and independent expenditures supporting Derek Merrin and Scott Perry that appear to have been disseminated after their primaries. Its response is due 11/24.

Requests for Additional Information
Ten new RFAIs, led by Club for Growth PAC's three-part inquiry

Ten RFAIs went out on October 20, each with a response due date of 11/24/2026. Four are to candidate committees and six to PACs and party committees. Most are template items: Column B total errors, unclear disbursement purposes, earmarked contributions and excessive contributions. The Club for Growth PAC letter is the most substantive. No treasurer is shared among these ten.

PACs and party committees

  • FEC asks Club for Growth PAC about apparent corporate or LLC contributions, $19,000 in earmarked contributions with no Schedule A entries, and independent expenditures for Derek Merrin and Scott Perry that appear to have been disseminated after their primaries. The letter concerns the amended August Monthly report received 09/20/2026. Treasurer is Adam Rozansky. (RFAI)
  • FEC says Republican Voters of Texas PAC may have failed to file timely 24-hour reports on last-minute independent expenditures. It also asks why the amended April Quarterly report shows receipts down $69,710.97 and disbursements down $45,500.44 from the original. Treasurer is Linda Stuckey. (RFAI)
  • FEC asks Kern County Republican Central Committee (FED) to explain a Schedule H2 ratio for an "IE" activity that it reported as unchanged though the event was not previously reported. Treasurer is Tim Collins. (RFAI)
  • FEC asks Nevada County Republican Party whether receipts labeled "Miscellaneous Small Donations" include any individual's contributions above $200 for the year. If so, they must be itemized. Treasurer is Tammie Siller. (RFAI)
  • FEC says the Column B totals on the July Quarterly report of Do Not Use Teresa Leger Fernandez Victory Fund appear incorrect on a dozen lines. Treasurer is Steven Fitzer. (RFAI)
  • FEC says Column B totals and line categories are wrong on the July Quarterly report of Seventh Congressional District Republican Party of Minnesota. Contributions to federal candidates and committees belong on a separate Schedule B supporting Line 23. Treasurer is Gregg Allan Kulberg. (RFAI)

Candidate committees
