Top Story
Seafarers union PAC reports insurance recovery for 'additional misappropriations' of committee funds
The FEC's October 8 letter says the $13,064.50 receipt appears related to an apparent unauthorized use of committee funds.

The Federal Election Commission sent an RFAI dated October 8, 2026 to Seafarers Political Activity Donation, the political action committee of the Seafarers International Union of North America–AGLIW, about its September Monthly Report. Schedule A shows a $13,064.50 receipt described as "Insurance recovery for additional misappropriations found See memo."

The Commission wrote that the receipt "appears to be related to an apparent unauthorized use of Committee funds" and that it "may take further legal action regarding this apparent improper use." The letter is addressed to the committee's treasurer, Thomas Orzechowski, Jr., and contains a single item.

Two other RFAIs dated the same day raise similar flags: Bish for Congress has debit-card "fraud charge" disbursements of $483.27 and $1,000.00 offset by $11,193.51 in Wells Fargo provisional credits, and the EchoStar DISH Network PAC reported a $4,767.41 "DISPUTED ACH DEBIT." Both letters carry the same legal-action warning.
