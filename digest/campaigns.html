Top Story
Committee tells the FEC it fixed a $5,000 contribution to Rep. Ken Calvert's campaign that was wrongly labeled as a primary gift
Maintaining All Republicans In Office PAC answered an October 13 request for additional information by amending its year-end report.

Maintaining All Republicans In Office PAC told the FEC in a Form 99 received October 17 that it re-designated a $5,000 contribution to Ken Calvert for Congress. The contribution had been attributed to the primary election when it should have been a general election gift for 2026.

The PAC says it amended its January 31, 2026 Year-End report to show the general election designation and filed it with the FEC. The Form 99 is a response to an RFAI dated October 13, 2026.

The filing is a routine correction of an election designation, and it shows no sign of wrongdoing. No new RFAI letters surfaced in this batch, and no red-flag keywords or voided transactions turned up.

Form 99 Correspondence
Eight small committees file Form 99s, mostly saying they fall below filing thresholds

Most of these are zero-activity or below-threshold notices from small candidate and PAC committees. One is a response to an RFAI, and one is a long-shot presidential candidate describing a mailed paper report.

  • Maintaining All Republicans In Office PAC tells the FEC it re-designated a $5,000 contribution to Ken Calvert for Congress from the primary to the 2026 general election. It says it amended its Year-End report in response to an RFAI dated October 13. This is a PAC. (Form 99)

The presidential candidate, Jonathon Mark Rice, says he has had no confirmation of receipt and asks the FEC to confirm that the Form 99 clears a Late File Notification. He gives an updated address in Los Angeles. This is a candidate committee. (Form 99)
