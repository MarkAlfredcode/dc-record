Top Story
Open-Seat Tennessee Campaign Tells FEC Its $157,000 Conduit-Contribution Report Was Accurate — Nearly the Only Compliance Filing on a Recess-Week Day
Johnny Garrett for Congress pushes back on an FEC request for more information, itemizing conduit money passed through a bundler in the second quarter — the lone substantive FEC filing since yesterday as Congress sits in a Labor Day pro forma lull.

In the only piece of substantive FEC correspondence filed since Sunday, Johnny Garrett for Congress — the committee for Johnny Garrett, a Republican running for the open seat in Tennessee's 6th Congressional District — told the FEC's Reports Analysis Division that its July Quarterly Report is accurate as filed, disputing the commission's suggestion that it failed to disclose earmarked contributions.

The response, filed as a Form 99 miscellaneous submission and signed by Assistant Treasurer Les Williamson, answers an FEC letter dated August 16, 2026 that questioned the committee's disclosure of contributions received through a conduit during the April 1–June 30, 2026 reporting period. Williamson's letter lays out the full accounting: $157,496.65 total received through a conduit, split between $92,373.21 reported on Line 11(a)(i) and $65,123.38 on Line 11(a)(ii), alongside $4,529.24 and $5,523.81 in conduit-contribution chargebacks reported on those same two lines, respectively. The FEC's earlier letter is not itself part of today's filing window and was not independently reviewed for this edition.

It's a quiet marker for an otherwise sparse news day: no other Requests for Additional Information (RFAI) were filed, no committees filed notable Form 99 correspondence, no stock trades were reported by lawmakers in either chamber, and no committee meetings fall in the next 24 hours. Congress itself gaveled in and out in pro forma sessions on both sides of the Capitol on September 4, its last legislative day on the record, with no votes and only routine bill introductions — consistent with the Labor Day recess. Details on all of it below.
