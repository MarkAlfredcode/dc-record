Top Story
PAC says unreported independent expenditures were already disclosed, answering FEC inquiry
More Jobs, Less Government replied October 10 to a September 7 Request for Additional Information.

More Jobs, Less Government (treasurer Charles Gantt) — responding to a September 7 Request for Additional Information on its amended July Quarterly Report, the committee says two independent expenditures disseminated May 19 that the FEC thought were not timely reported on 24-Hour Reports were already disclosed in an amended report filed July 31, and that it separately made a self-reported (sua sponte) submission to the FEC Office of General Counsel that day, calling the omission of two small production costs inadvertent.

The letter is addressed to Aaron Carrell, Senior Campaign Finance Analyst in the FEC Reports Analysis Division, and does not state the dollar amount of the two production costs.
