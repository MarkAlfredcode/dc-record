Top Story
Seafarers union PAC says its former treasurer diverted another $13,064.50, including three candidate refunds
The committee's Form 99, received Nov. 4, says an insurer has reimbursed the full amount.

Seafarers Political Activity Donation - Seafarers International Union of NA-AGLIW told the FEC it has found more embezzlement by its former treasurer, Chelsea Diab (formerly Chelsea D. Heindel). The Form 99 answers an FEC letter dated Oct. 8, 2026. It says Diab diverted payroll-deducted member contributions into her personal bank account in 2023 ($1,407), 2024 ($1,211) and 2025 ($2,946.50), a total of $5,564.50. The committee also says she intercepted three refunds from federal candidate committees of contributions the PAC had made, $2,500 each.

The three original contributions went to Sean Patrick Maloney for Congress (Nov. 3, 2022), Brian Fitzpatrick For All Of Us (Oct. 17, 2024) and Pat Ryan for Congress (June 11, 2024). The filing says Diab improperly reported the Maloney contribution as voided on Aug. 29, 2024. The newly found losses total $13,064.50. The committee says they were covered by insurance and reimbursed by Chubb and Son Federal Insurance Company, a receipt dated Aug. 31, 2026 on Line 17 of its September Monthly report.

The filing follows two Form 99 letters the committee dated June 18, 2026 about earlier embezzlements, which it says it disclosed in consultation with the FEC's Reports Analysis Division. This filing does not state a total for the earlier losses. The new diversions are disclosed as unitemized Line 11a memo entries and itemized Line 16 memo entries on the September Monthly report.
