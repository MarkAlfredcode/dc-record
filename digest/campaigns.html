Top Story
Rep. LaMonica McIver's campaign amends two reports to fix contribution limits, designations and a wrong committee ID
In a Form 99 filed Monday, the committee answers an October 8 FEC request about excessive and misdesignated contributions.

LaMonica McIver for Congress, the campaign committee of Rep. LaMonica McIver (D-NJ), filed a Form 99 on October 12 responding to a Request for Additional Information dated October 8, 2026. The request referenced the Amended April Quarterly Report, which the committee originally received on May 18, 2026. The committee says it amended the April Quarterly Report and the 12-Day Pre-Primary Report on October 12. The amendments are FEC-2019869 and FEC-2019878.

The response covers four matters. Contributor James Gee gave $1,000 through ActBlue on March 24, 2026 with no election designation. The committee had reported it as a primary contribution, but says it was meant for the general election and that Gee confirmed this in writing. Gee's totals are now $3,500 for the primary and $2,500 for the general, each within the $3,500 per-election limit. Contributor Donald Henley gave $1,000 and $4,000 through ActBlue on January 25, 2026. The $4,000 was designated for the general election and exceeded that limit by $500. The committee refunded the $500 by check dated May 12, 2026, and the Pre-Primary Report originally labeled the refund as for the primary. It now labels the refund as for the general election.

The notice treated the Air Line Pilots Association PAC and United Pilots PAC/United Airlines Master Executive Council as affiliated, sharing one $5,000 per-election limit. The Air Line Pilots Association PAC gave a total of $5,000 designated for the primary. United Pilots PAC's $4,000 from February 27, 2026 was designated for the general election but had been reported as a primary contribution. The committee amended it, so the affiliated totals are $5,000 for the primary and $4,000 for the general. A $1,000 contribution received February 13, 2026 had been reported under the wrong committee ID (C70002563). The contributor is the National Association of Realtors Political Action Committee, FEC ID C00030718. The response is signed by Treasurer LaShea McIver.

Form 99 Correspondence
Mostly below-threshold statements, plus replies to FEC notices from a state party and a donor-information PAC

Sixteen other Form 99 filings arrived on October 12, all received the day before this edition. No new RFAI letters surfaced in this window. Most are short statements that a committee has not reached the $5,000 reporting threshold or had no activity, filed around the October Quarterly deadline. The substantive ones are a response from the Republican Party of Florida to an FEC letter, a donor-information disavowal response from Americans for Legal Immigration PAC, and a debt correction from John Beccia for Congress. Three filings contained only the cover header with no message text.

  • Republican Party of Florida (party committee): replies to an FEC letter dated September 7, 2026 about its May, June and July Monthly reports. It states that no employee spent more than 25% of compensated time on Federal Election Activity in any month of those periods, or in the August and September Monthly periods filed since. Signed by Nancy H. Watkins, CPA, and addressed to Jack Baisden of the Reports Analysis Division. Filing.
  • Americans for Legal Immigration PAC (PAC): a "disavowal response" addressed to Nicholas Tarone, Senior Campaign Finance Analyst, in the Reports Analysis Division. Treasurer William Gheen says the PAC cannot supply missing "best efforts" donor information, such as occupation and employer, for all donors. He attributes the low response rates to donors fearing for their jobs, and describes its donation-page disclaimer, reply cards, 30-day follow-up emails or calls, and at least three solicitations a year requesting the information. The PAC says it will amend its reports as donors respond. Filing.
  • John Beccia for Congress (candidate committee, Democrat, Massachusetts open seat): tells the FEC it made adjustment entries on its October Quarterly 2026 report to remove debt reported in error to the vendor Authentic Campaigns. The vendor confirmed the debt is not owed. Signed by Treasurer Steven Mele. Filing.
