Top Story
Quiet filing window: seven FEC requests and responses, led by a sitting congressman's cumulative-total errors
Nothing in today's batch involves alleged misuse of funds; the most notable filing is a request to Rep. Scott Fitzgerald's campaign filed Sunday.

The Federal Election Commission asked Scott Fitzgerald for Congress, the campaign committee of Rep. Scott Fitzgerald (R-WI-5), on October 4 to correct year-to-date totals on lines 6(a), 6(c), 11(c), 11(e) and 16 of an amended July Quarterly Report covering April 1 to June 30, 2025. The amendment was received September 27, 2026, and a response is due November 9.

The rest of the window is routine: five other requests for additional information, mostly about registration paperwork and mismatched report totals, and one Form 99 from a former House candidate explaining what his committee will do with leftover cash. None of the filings read mentions personal use of funds or any of the red-flag terms in the keyword scan.

Requests for Additional Information
Five requests, four about registration paperwork

The FEC's Reports Analysis Division sent five requests dated October 4, all due November 9. Four concern committee registration forms; one concerns arithmetic errors in a monthly report. Nothing here involves alleged misconduct.

  • Wolf Pack (PAC, ID C00599092) — August Monthly Report: the opening cash balance doesn't match the closing balance of the prior monthly report, and Column B year-to-date totals on 11 summary lines (6(c), 7, 11(c), 11(d), 19, 20, 21(b), 21(c), 23, 31, 32) look wrong. Amended reports are required. Treasurer: James D. Alexander. Filing.
  • Posh Lion (presidential committee, ID C00962050) — the committee's name doesn't include its candidate's name, which FEC rules require for a principal campaign committee; it must amend its Statement of Organization. Treasurer: Jeff Raysor. Filing.
  • The Presidential Campaign Commitee of Daniel for America 2028 (presidential committee, ID C00961631) — the FEC says the committee appears to have filed its Statement of Organization late (the deadline is 10 days after designation) and asks for an explanation. Treasurer: Daniel Bernard. Filing.
  • Act Now Arkansas (PAC, ID C00950758) — its amended Statement of Organization, received 09/11, checks the box for an authorized committee that isn't a principal campaign committee, yet prior filings suggest it is an independent-expenditure-only committee; it must amend or clarify. Treasurer: Wilbert Jones. Filing.
  • Dantwan for GA (House candidate committee, ID C00893156) — its amended Statement of Organization, received 09/22, lists a different office sought than the candidate's Statement of Candidacy filed 08/10/2026; one must be corrected. FEC candidate records list Dantwan Watkins under a Georgia Senate filing, while the FEC filing index marks the committee as a House committee. Treasurer: Dorothy Thomas. Filing.
