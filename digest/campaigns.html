FEC Flags Justice Democrats PAC Over a $100,000 Contribution It Says May Be Illegal — Plus Two Unreported Attack-Ad Buys
A Request for Additional Information tied to the PAC's July Quarterly report zeroes in on a six-figure gift from a policy nonprofit, plus two independent-expenditure buys in competitive New York and California primaries that never showed up on the required disclosure schedule.

The Federal Election Commission sent Justice Democrats PAC (FEC ID C00630665) a Request for Additional Information on September 9 flagging three problems with its July Quarterly Report, covering April 1 through June 30, 2026. The most serious: Schedule A shows the PAC received $100,000 on June 29, 2026, from an organization called The Institute For Middle East Understanding (IMEU) Policy Project, which the FEC says is not registered as a federal political committee. Federal law bars corporations and labor organizations from contributing to political committees. The FEC's letter notes an exception if the money was deposited into a "Non-Contribution Account" consistent with the settlement in Carey v. FEC — but if that's not the case, the PAC must either amend its report to properly disclose the account or refund the contribution.

The letter also flags two apparent gaps in the PAC's independent-expenditure reporting. A 48-Hour Report disclosed $5,000 payments each to LC Media LLC on May 27, 2026 — one supporting Democratic primary candidate Darializa Avila Chevalier and one opposing incumbent Rep. Adriano Espaillat, both in New York's 2026 primary — but neither was itemized on the Schedule E the law requires. Separately, the FEC says a Schedule E entry for an expenditure backing Rep. Diana DeGette was designated for a "2025 Primary Election," but DeGette did not appear on a ballot in 2025 — a discrepancy the committee needs to clarify and correct.

Justice Democrats PAC has until October 14, 2026 to respond. Failure to adequately respond "could result in an audit or enforcement action," per the FEC's standard RFAI language. The filing is one of 55 Requests for Additional Information the FEC issued yesterday — see the full list below.

Today's NASA Astronomy Picture of the Day: LDN 1295, "The Giraffe Nebula." Many see a giraffe facing right, neck stretched high, mid-stride — though some see a squirrel instead. It's a dark nebula, an interstellar cloud of dust and gas blocking the light of stars behind it, cataloged in 1962 by astronomer Beverly Lynds. The animal shapes are pareidolia — our brains' tendency to find familiar patterns in random shapes.
