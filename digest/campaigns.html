Top Story
ActBlue tells the FEC it now processes contributions from donors with foreign addresses
A Form 99 filed October 22 says a passport-verification update let U.S. citizens living abroad give through the platform starting around October 2.

ActBlue told the FEC in a one-page Form 99 that, beginning on or about 10/02/2026 and following an update to its passport-verification process, it began processing contributions from donors with addresses outside the United States. The filing says these are contributions from U.S. citizens residing abroad and that they will appear on ActBlue's regular reports with the donor's foreign address as provided. (Form 99)

The filing does not say how many foreign-address contributions have been processed, their dollar value, or which committees received them. It was received 10/22, two days before this edition.

Also filed 10/22: the Harris County Democratic Party told the FEC that $102,999.17 in state-designated fundraising receipts was mistakenly deposited into its federal account and is being moved to the state account. (Form 99)

Form 99 Correspondence
ActBlue's foreign-address disclosure and a Texas county party's misdeposit lead 28 Form 99s

Most of the 28 Form 99s are short statements from small committees saying they are under the $5,000 reporting threshold. A handful are substantive: ActBlue on foreign-address donors, the Harris County Democratic Party on state money deposited in its federal account, and the Ohio Democratic Party - Federal answering an FEC letter. Several were received October 21 or 22, one to three days before this edition.

  • ActBlue tells the FEC it began processing contributions from donors with addresses outside the United States around October 2. The filing ties this to an update to its passport-verification process and says the donors are U.S. citizens living abroad. Their foreign addresses will show on its regular reports. Received 10/22. (Form 99)
  • The Harris County Democratic Party tells the FEC that $102,999.17 in state-designated receipts was mistakenly deposited into its federal account. The filing explains its October 1-14 Pre-General Form 3X: 36 receipts are being corrected through transfers to the state account, and the total includes $4,500 in checks deposited October 1, among them a $2,500 contribution from Rodney Ellis. It reports a $14,985 corrective transfer for a $15,000 September 'Auntie Up AAPI' receipt less a $15 bank fee, and says a $14,000 Bayou City Event Center payment was for a party-sponsored event and not an in-kind candidate contribution. Received 10/22. (Form 99)
  • The Ohio Democratic Party - Federal answers an FEC letter dated September 16 about its August Monthly report. It says it amended its July Monthly report to correct ending cash on hand and amended its statement of organization to add the DSCC Victory Fund and Friends of State Parties. It says its Consulting/Communications payments were consulting fees, not public communications, and its printing payments did not refer to a clearly identified federal candidate. Received 10/21. (Form 99)
  • McGinnis for the People tells the FEC it emailed 420 donors on October 22 asking for their profession and employer. The Donorbox-database donors were missing that information, and the committee says responses have started coming in. Treasurer is Frederick Clarkson. Received 10/22. (Form 99)
