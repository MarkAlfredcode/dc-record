Top Story
Nebraska House Candidate Says She Personally Paid More Than $300,000 for Campaign Spending, Including a $263,006 TV Buy
A Form 99 filed Wednesday, September 30 answers an RFAI over unreported in-kind contributions and a $270,000 48-hour report that did not match the final figure.

Kishla Askins for Congress, the Nebraska open-seat House committee of Kishla Askins, answered a Request for Additional Information on September 30. It says two in-kind contributions from the candidate were never put on a 48-hour report: $32,525.94 on 5/1/2026 and $3,525 on 5/2/2026. Both paid Scale to Win for “Texting Services.”

The response also says the 48-hour report filed 5/5/2026 for a $270,000 in-kind contribution from the candidate was wrong. The committee says the correct amount, $263,006, is in its Termination Report. That money paid MLZ/ Canal Media for TV ads on 5/5/2026.

The letter lists the candidate’s other personal-funds spending with vendor names and addresses. Items include Mary Broughton ($450, canvassing support), Dan Woodward ($850, event speaker) and several small LinkedIn ad charges. The listed items total more than $300,000. I read the committee’s response, not the Commission’s underlying RFAI letter, so the questions it answers (starting at item 2) are inferred from the reply.
