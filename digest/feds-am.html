Top Story

Navy award of more than $1 billion to RTX Corporation for aircraft propulsion spares becomes public, three months after signing

The $1.08 billion order for "propulsion initial spares" is the largest contract action in today's data.

The Department of the Navy obligated $1,081,885,601 to RTX Corporation on a new award whose entire description reads "PROPULSION INITIAL SPARES." It was signed July 30 and only now published on USAspending; Defense Department contract data is released with a 90-day delay, so this is not a new signing.

The product category is gas turbines and jet engines for aircraft and their components, and the industry code is aircraft engine and engine parts manufacturing. The record does not say which aircraft or engine program the spares support, or whether the award was competed, so those questions are open.

The amount is the sum obligated so far on the award, and no other contract action in today's USAspending pull comes close to it.

Coast Guard plans no-bid contract with Defense Maritime Solutions for engine salvage inspection on cutter Healy
Combined Synopsis/Solicitation  ·  U.S. Coast Guard
