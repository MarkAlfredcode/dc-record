Top Story

Immigration agency plans no-bid deal with Procentrix to link its A-File records to the immigration court and citizenship systems

The value is redacted, and the justification was posted October 16, after the September 30 start date it lists for the 12-month modification.

U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement intends to modify its existing Records and Information Management Unit task order with Procentrix LLC on a sole-source basis. The 12-month modification buys Alien-File (A-File) records modernization and integration support. According to the justification, the work would connect the Office of the Principal Legal Advisor's PLAnet litigation case management system with the U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services STACKS digital A-File repository through Immigration and Customs Enforcement's data mesh and API gateway. The dollar value is redacted in the posted document.

The agency cites the 'logical follow-on' exception under FAR 16.507-6(b)(3), meaning the order is sole-sourced for economy and efficiency because it follows an order already placed under the contract. The underlying contract is the Scalable Ways to Implement Flexible Tasks (SWIFT) Collaborative Services indefinite-delivery contract, and the task order is 70CTD024FR0000094. The modification covers September 30, 2026 through September 29, 2027, concurrent with Option Period 2, and the document says it does not extend the task order's existing period of performance. The notice was posted October 16, after that start date.

The justification leans on incumbency. It says Procentrix 'has current operational responsibility for the accepted baseline' of the records environment that must be modified, and it warns of 'unmanageable division of accountability between multiple vendors that could compromise system and records integrity.' It also cites Executive Order 14243, which directs agencies to reduce information silos and facilitate authorized sharing of unclassified records. According to the extraction, Procentrix has a federal contract on record in USAspending going back to 2010.
