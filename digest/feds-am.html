Census Bureau sole-sources $950,000 in voter registration data from Labels & Lists for 2027 test and 2030 Census planning

The justification says Labels & Lists Inc. is the only company that can supply raw and enhanced voter registration records for all 50 states and the District of Columbia.

The U.S. Census Bureau, part of the U.S. Department of Commerce, plans a 12-month, firm-fixed-price contract with Labels & Lists Inc. (called L2 in the document) with no competition. The estimated value is $950,000, verified against the document. The Bureau wants four commercial data products: current and historical (from 2014) enhanced voter registration data, and current and historical (from 2012) raw state voter registration data, covering all 50 states and the District of Columbia.

According to the justification, the data would support the Bureau's administrative-records research, including matching people and addresses, the 2027 Census Test and the 2030 Census. It says L2 "is the only source capable of providing the specific data set for this unique requirement." It also says the Bureau "has historically utilized enhanced voter registration data supplied by L2." It cites a commercial-item authority (41 U.S.C. § 1901, as implemented by the Revolutionary FAR Overhaul) rather than a standard only-one-source finding.

USAspending's earliest federal contract on record under the vendor's name is dated October 23, 2026, four days before this notice was posted.

OMB Apportionments

145 agency and program spend plans approved; Title X funds held pending civil rights investigation

The federal government approved 145 new spend plans across dozens of agencies for fiscal years 2025 and 2026, covering routine allocations for benefits programs, law enforcement training, international development, and research initiatives. Three items stand out: a Title X family planning hold affecting 13 grantees, a $285 million apprenticeship expansion, and State Department rescissions.

Title X Family Planning Hold

U.S. Department of Health and Human Services is holding approximately $19.1 million in Title X family planning funding for 13 grant recipients pending an investigation into civil rights violations and active litigation (NPFRHA v. Kennedy). The withheld recipients are Virginia League for Planned Parenthood ($4,802,042), Planned Parenthood of Greater Texas ($2,133,365), Planned Parenthood of Minnesota, North Dakota, and South Dakota ($2,664,525), Planned Parenthood Association of Utah ($2,081,656), Planned Parenthood of Greater Ohio ($1,850,361), Planned Parenthood of Southern New England ($1,748,590), Planned Parenthood Great Northwest, Hawaii, Alaska, Indiana, and Kentucky (Alaska, $925,349; Indiana, $501,813; Kentucky, $438,148; Idaho, $482,976), Planned Parenthood of Northern New England ($464,894), Planned Parenthood South Atlantic (South Carolina, $527,325; North Carolina, $441,967). Spend plan.

Apprenticeship Expansion

U.S. Department of Labor will administer $285 million appropriated under the Full-Year Continuing Appropriations and Extensions Act of 2025 (P.L. 119-4) to expand registered apprenticeships under the National Apprenticeship Act. The funds, available from July 1, 2024 through June 30, 2025, will support grants, cooperative agreements, and contracts with states, equity intermediaries, and business and labor partners to expand opportunities in skilled trades. Spend plan.

State Department Rescissions

U.S. Department of State's Cultural and International Protocol Affairs (CIPA) account is subject to multiple rescissions totaling $753,768: $203,328 from FY 2024–2025 carryover funds, $157,906 from FY 2025 appropriations, and $392,534 proposed for rescission in the President's special message. The account's total appropriated and carryforward funds for the period are $683,704. Spend plan.

Also apportioned (routine, non-flagged): Corps of Engineers—Civil Works, $55,909,000 (FY 2027 Expenses); U.S. Department of Agriculture, Office of the Secretary, $6,253,947,428 (FY 2027) and Rural Development, $512,279,500 (FY 2027 Salaries and Expenses); Executive Office of the President, Unanticipated Needs, $0 (FY 2027 Spectrum Relocation Fund).
