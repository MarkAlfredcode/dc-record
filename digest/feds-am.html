Top Story

Missile Defense Agency posts justification for skipping competition on Sea-Based X-Band Radar task order

A redacted Justification for Exception to Fair Opportunity covers development, operations and sustainment of the radar under the agency's SHIELD contract vehicle.

The Missile Defense Agency posted a redacted Justification for Exception to Fair Opportunity for the Sea-Based X-Band Radar Development, Operations and Sustainment task order. The notice says the order falls under the agency's Scalable Homeland Innovative Enterprise Layered Defense (SHIELD) indefinite-delivery contract, and that the document is marked approved for public release (Distribution Statement A).

An exception to fair opportunity means the agency is steering a task order to one company on a contract that normally has several holders who compete for each order. The raw notice text does not name the company or give a value. The screening pass that normally reads the attached justification failed this run, so the vendor, dollar value and stated reason are unverified here.

OMB Apportionments

Office of Management and Budget places $300 million oversight hold on Department of Veterans Affairs pharmacy pilot pending detailed briefing and spend plan

The Office of Management and Budget flagged one apportionment file during this window, attaching an oversight hold condition to a multi-million dollar program level account at the Department of Veterans Affairs. In addition, routine apportionment files were processed for the Department of Health and Human Services and the Department of Defense--Military Programs.

Out of a $387,318,090 annual program level apportionment for the Department of Veterans Affairs under Public Law 119-37, the Office of Management and Budget withheld $300,000,000 from obligation. This funding remains restricted until the agency provides a detailed briefing and a revised spend plan covering the Department of Defense and Department of Veterans Affairs pharmacy pilot, a unified credentialing and privileging system, and data sharing components under Executive Order 14426, demonstrating alignment with 38 U.S.C. 8111.
