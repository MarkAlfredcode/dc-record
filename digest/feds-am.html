Veterans Affairs' draft contract for disability exams sets a $40 billion ceiling

A draft request for proposals posted Friday would split nationwide medical disability examinations among two to four companies per service group.

The Department of Veterans Affairs has posted a draft request for proposals for "Medical Disability Examinations-Next Generation" (solicitation 36C10X27R0001). According to the draft, "The maximum value for MDE-NG is $40,000,000,000 in the aggregate across all MDE-NG IDIQ contracts and all SGs for the entire contract period, including all options." That is a ceiling, not a spending commitment: the same section says the government does not guarantee orders or dollar value above a $3,500,000 minimum.

The draft says the government "intends two to four contracts for each Service Group." The ordering period would be a 12-month base period plus four 12-month option periods. The exams feed the ratings decisions that determine disability compensation and pension benefits. Claimants include veterans and active-duty service members, who can also go through the joint Department of Defense-Department of Veterans Affairs disability evaluation programs.

This comes from a 105-page attachment posted October 9 and read today, on a presolicitation notice from the Veterans Affairs contracting office in Frederick. It is a draft, so terms may change before a final solicitation.

Air Force Research Laboratory wants a hypersonic weapon at under one-fifth the cost and five times the production rate
Solicitation  ·  Department of the Air Force, Air Force Research Laboratory
Late finding, from a call dated October 9 under the lab's broad research solicitation (FA2391-23-S-2403). It says the call will seek "an operationally relevant, air-launched hypersonic weapon for less than 1/5 the cost and greater than 5x the production rate of current systems." A contractor proposing flight testing would have to build at least two flight test vehicles and support at least two flight test events. A separate March call in the same solicitation, for affordable hypersonic weapon concepts, sets aside 15% of base cost as a schedule incentive.

General Services Administration's Washington-area construction contract lets it add contractors and raise the ceiling unilaterally
Solicitation  ·  General Services Administration
Late finding, from an agreement document posted September 25 for the D.C. Metro Area multiple-award construction contract. It says the government "reserves the unilateral right to increase the Master Contract MOL by up to 50% of the total aggregate amount" if conditions are met. It can also add contractors at any time and add Virginia and Maryland counties to the coverage area. The document lists work in sensitive secure rooms (SCIFs), high-security data centers and high-security buildings.

Coast Guard's next small response boat must carry machine gun mounts fore and aft
Solicitation  ·  U.S. Coast Guard
Late finding, from the Response Boat Small Third Generation specification posted September 30. It requires "forward and aft working decks, each accommodating weapon mounts for M240B light machine guns." The camera system must be able to detect "a 30-foot vessel at a range greater than one nautical mile in total darkness."
