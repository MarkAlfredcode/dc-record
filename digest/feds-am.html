Top Story

Space Command wants a builder for an 800,000-square-foot command-and-control facility, and bidders must show a $2.5 billion bond

A special notice on the Redstone Arsenal project sets a minimum bonding capacity far above most federal construction.

The Air Force Civil Engineer Center and U.S. Space Command are looking for a design-build contractor for the roughly 800,000-gross-square-foot Space Command command-and-control facility at Redstone Arsenal, Alabama. The special notice describes architectural, engineering and secure facility design, construction, furniture and equipment, electronic security, a construction site surveillance system, command-and-control computer and intelligence infrastructure, and integrated commissioning.

According to the attached Minimum Qualifications Form, an offeror must certify that it "has the capacity to provide a project payment and performance bond of a minimum of $2.5 billion (construction value of non-C4IT Work)." The notice gives no contract value.

The government posted Amendment 01 on October 29 with an updated Challenge Statement and a new "RFI to Industry" document. Responses to the request for information are due by the end of the day on November 12, 2026.
