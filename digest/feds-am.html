Customs and Border Protection awards seven border facility construction contracts worth $777.8 million in two days

Design-build awards for Border Patrol stations and checkpoints in Texas, New Mexico and Arizona all posted September 28–29.

U.S. Customs and Border Protection posted seven design-build awards totaling $777,754,316 on September 28 and 29. Environmental Chemical Corporation took the largest, $158,000,000 for a Border Patrol station at Rio Grande City, Texas; Grunley Construction Co., Inc. won both the $137,000,000 Eagle Pass station and the $103,000,000 Brackettville station.

Clark Construction Group LLC won the $123,585,356 Laredo checkpoint facility and a $117,000,000 station at Las Cruces, New Mexico; Southwest Valley Constructors Co ($75,810,908, Sierra Blanca, Texas) and Brasfield & Gorrie LLC ($63,358,052, Nogales, Arizona) completed the set.

The timing matches the end of the federal fiscal year, when 324 of the 362 new awards in this report were dated. Elsewhere, a Federal Highway Administration cybersecurity modification cites a White House threat detection warning, covered in the SAM.gov section.

Notable awards

The Social Security Administration awarded International Business Machines Corporation $130,434,205.69 on September 28 to set up a new mainframe hardware and storage contract. The description says it replaces a blanket purchase agreement that ends September 30, 2026, so this is a replacement for an expiring vehicle, not new capacity. Award record.

The same agency separately gave Carahsoft Technology Corp $60,490,406.19 for a Broadcom mainframe consumption license, a portfolio license agreement and support services. Together, the two September 28 mainframe awards total roughly $190.9 million. Award record.

The Office of the Assistant Secretary for Preparedness and Response awarded Kaleo, Inc. $96,058,494 on September 28 so the Biomedical Advanced Research and Development Authority can use Project BioShield funds for late-stage clinical development and regulatory approval of 2-PAM autoinjectors, a drug-device combination to treat organophosphate poisoning, including nerve agent exposure. Award record.

The same office awarded Seqirus Inc $42,769,479 on September 29 for formulation and filling of an MF59-adjuvanted, cell-based H3N8-like influenza vaccine (strain A/Henan/4-10CNIC/2022) and for implementing a clinical trial. Award record.

The Transportation Security Administration placed a $63,606,870 delivery order with Smiths Detection Inc. on September 28 for medium-speed explosive detection systems, installation services and ancillary equipment. Award record.

The Centers for Disease Control and Prevention awarded Ketchum Inc., an advertising firm, $64,990,000 on September 29 for the National Tobacco Education Campaign; the description gives no further detail. Award record.

Chenega Enterprise Systems & Solutions, LLC received $37,385,494.95 from the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention on September 28 for its Emergency Mass Notification System. The description is prefixed “EO 14398,” and the federal product-and-service code is “construction of office buildings,” an odd fit for a notification system. In all, 15 of the new awards in this batch cite “EO 14398” in their descriptions, across the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention, the Department of Veterans Affairs, and the Food and Drug Administration; the records do not say what the citation means. Award record.
