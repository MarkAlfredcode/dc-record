Customs and Border Protection books $382 million in border facility construction in the last days of the fiscal year

Three awards from September 29-30 cover facilities in Jacksonville, Sierra Vista and Presidio.

U.S. Customs and Border Protection awarded Tutor Perini Corporation $144,000,000 on September 30, 2026 to build an agency facility in Jacksonville, Florida. The same day it awarded Southwest Valley Constructors $129,000,000 for a facility in Sierra Vista, Arizona, and on September 29 gave BL Harbert International LLC a $109,000,000 task order for design-build services for the Presidio Border Patrol Station.

Elsewhere, the Defense Logistics Agency posted two sole-source notices raising the ceiling on its Global Distribution Expeditionary Contract with Olgoonik Federal, LLC by $90,000,000 and then $100,000,000, to $415,500,000, and the U.S. Coast Guard opened bidding on a new light icebreaker class.

Border Patrol and Customs construction awards worth $382 million lead a fiscal-year-end surge of 421 new contracts

USAspending shows 421 new-to-report contract awards of $2 million or more (base awards, not modifications) in the September 8 – October 3, 2026 window, many booked at fiscal year-end. Customs and Border Protection construction led by size, and the Executive Office of the President awarded a $6 million classified-facility contract. Descriptions below are the agencies' own wording, lightly shortened.

White House & Executive Office of the President

BSNB LLC won a $6,000,000 contract from the Executive Office of the President on September 30, 2026, for a modular Sensitive Compartmented Information Facility — a prefabricated, portable secure room built to handle classified intelligence material. USAspending lists the Executive Office of the President as both the awarding agency and sub-agency, and the record carries no further description of where the facility will be installed or which component will use it. Award record.

Border facility construction: three awards totaling $382 million

U.S. Customs and Border Protection, part of the Department of Homeland Security, booked three large construction awards in the last days of the fiscal year. Tutor Perini Corporation received $144,000,000 on September 30 to build a Customs and Border Protection facility in Jacksonville, Florida (Award); Southwest Valley Constructors received $129,000,000 the same day for a facility in Sierra Vista, Arizona (Award); and BL Harbert International LLC received a $109,000,000 task order on September 29 for design-build services for the Presidio Border Patrol Station (Award). The Jacksonville award is notable because it is far from the southwest border.
