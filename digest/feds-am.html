Top Story

Army hands eleven firms a place on a $4.17 billion contract to build and modernize its ammunition plants

The Army Contracting Command at Rock Island, Illinois, made multiple awards on November 5 for design, build and commissioning work at seven government-owned, contractor-operated ammunition plants.

The U.S. Army Contracting Command – Rock Island says it awarded 11 indefinite-delivery, indefinite-quantity multiple-award task order contracts from solicitation W519TC26RA020. The base ordering period is five years (1,826 calendar days). The winners will compete for task orders covering design, build, facility modernization and commissioning at seven government-owned, contractor-operated Army ammunition plants. The notice names Holston (Kingsport, Tennessee), Iowa (Middletown, Iowa), Lake City (Independence, Missouri), Milan (Milan, Tennessee), the Quad City Cartridge Case Facility (Rock Island, Illinois) and Radford among them.

The eleven awardees are Bering-Weston JV, LLC, Hensel Phelps Construction Co, Clark Construction Group LLC, MSM Group North America Inc., Applied Research Associates, Inc., Parsons Government Services Inc., M. C. Dean, Inc., J Kokolakis Contracting Inc, BAE Systems Ordnance Systems Inc., Alberici Constructors Inc and Burns & McDonnell Engineering Company, Inc. Each of the eleven notices lists the same figure, $4,171,491,000, dated November 5, 2026. Because the figure is identical across all eleven, it appears to be a shared program ceiling, not a separate award to each firm.

The one attached file has not been read yet; it is queued for a later edition, so the per-plant scope and any per-firm limits are not yet confirmed.

Marine Corps buys 15 intelligence shelters from Anduril without competition, citing the company's prototype work
Anduril Industries Inc.  ·  value redacted  ·  only one company can do it  ·  Department of the Navy, Marine Corps Systems Command
The Marine Corps Systems Command program office for intelligence and cyberspace operations will place a sole-source delivery order under Anduril's existing Army single-award contract for 15 expeditionary shelters (8 operator, 7 processing) and logistics support for the Maritime Targeting Cell.
