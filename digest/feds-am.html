Nothing major broke in contracting; Veterans Affairs awards carry a new DEI-discrimination clause

No sole-source justifications and no $2 million-plus awards were posted, but dozens of small Veterans Affairs actions reference the clause or an executive order.

No new sole-source justifications were posted and no new awards of $2 million or more appeared. The 146 new solicitations were routine parts requests, mostly from the Defense Logistics Agency.

The notable thread is in smaller Department of Veterans Affairs awards from October 1: five awards to drug, radiopharmacy, lab-supply and test-kit vendors, totaling $3,453,802, add Federal Acquisition Regulation clause 52.222-90, which the descriptions say addresses DEI discrimination by federal contractors. USAspending Contracts

No new $2 million-plus awards; Veterans Affairs contracts add a clause on DEI discrimination

No new awards of $2 million or more appeared in the window. Among 1,142 smaller awards from October 1-4, the notable pattern is Department of Veterans Affairs actions whose descriptions reference a new anti-DEI-discrimination contract clause or Executive Order 14398.

Other notable awards
  • Cardinal Health 414, LLC (two awards), Jubilant DraxImage Radiopharmacies, Inc., Fisher Scientific Company L.L.C. and Cepheid — $3,453,802 combined, Department of Veterans Affairs: drugs, radiopharmaceuticals, lab supplies and COVID test kits; each description says the award or modification adds Federal Acquisition Regulation clause 52.222-90, titled as addressing DEI discrimination by federal contractors (April 2026) (Cardinal $1,059,206, Cardinal $486,147, Jubilant $827,558, Fisher $606,051, Cepheid $474,840).
  • Agiliti Health Inc (two awards, $849,999 and $316,599) and Hill-Rom, Inc. ($699,993) — Department of Veterans Affairs: hospital bed rentals whose descriptions end "and addressing DEI" (Agiliti 578, Agiliti 556, Hill-Rom).
  • Redhawk Group International, LLC — $1,034,292, Department of Veterans Affairs: a PET/CT scanner lease whose description cites Executive Order 14398 (award).
  • Volunteers of America of North Louisiana — $887,680, Department of Veterans Affairs: residential mental-health and substance-abuse services; the description reads "implementation of Executive Order 14398" (award).
  • RGH Enterprises, LLC — $250,000, Department of Veterans Affairs: direct delivery of medical and surgical supplies to the Washington, D.C. VA Medical Center, a modification inserting a standard clause (award).
Screened 1,142 new awards under $2 million (floor $100,000); 12 were flagged, 1 flagged award not listed (a $466,631 Smithsonian Institution contracting-support award for a program in San Francisco, matched on the word "Capitol"). Of the rest, 1,109 were Department of Veterans Affairs. 148 new awards worth $70.3 million, nearly all Department of Veterans Affairs, cite Executive Order 14398 in their descriptions, and one more ($706,874) cites Executive Order 14042.
