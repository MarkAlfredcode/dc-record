Top Story

Immigration and Customs Enforcement commits $278 million to deportation charter flights with CSI Aviation

A single call order dated October 31 is the largest new contract action in today's data.

Immigration and Customs Enforcement obligated $277,971,276 to CSI Aviation, Inc. on October 31, 2026, according to USAspending. The order's description says it 'obtains dedicated and on-demand chartered flight services for enforcement and removal operations under the ICE Air Program.' The same description says the program 'facilitates the safe, secure, and cost-effective transportation and deportation of illegal aliens.' The dollar figure is the amount obligated so far.

USAspending classifies the work as air passenger transportation and the company's business as travel arrangement and reservation services. The record does not say whether the order was competed. CSI Aviation also appears in this edition's Lobbying × Contracts section as a registered federal lobbying client.

Sole-Source Justifications

Commerce Department adds $35.4 million to Accenture Federal Services' 20-year financial-system contract without competition

Five new sole-source justifications came in today, two of them posted as Special Notices. None repeats an earlier edition. One is a contract extension and is covered in Extensions, so four are written up here.

Must read
Commerce Department raises Accenture Federal Services' financial-system contract by $35.4 million, saying only the incumbent can do the work
Accenture Federal Services LLC  ·  $35,440,397 increase  ·  Only one company can do it  ·  Department of Commerce
The Department of Commerce wants to raise the value of its Business Applications Solution contract with Accenture Federal Services LLC. The contract was awarded on April 24, 2020 at $340,914,621.69 after full and open competition, with a base year and 19 option years, and is now in option year six. The justification says the modification covers extra post-launch support for the National Institute of Standards and Technology, added Sunflower, PRISM and Oracle E-Business Suite licenses, and related maintenance and hosting. It says user populations grew by an average of more than 80 percent across the programs. It argues that 'Since this contractor has specifically configured the system, no other contractor has the required knowledge of, or access to, their system.'

Bureau of Prisons keeps Naphcare running at its Brooklyn detention center for November while a contract protest is pending

One new extension today: a one-month bridge for medical services at the Metropolitan Detention Center in Brooklyn.

Bureau of Prisons extends Naphcare's Brooklyn jail medical contract without competition while a protest holds up the replacement
Naphcare  ·  $889,018 estimated  ·  Only one company can do it  ·  Department of Justice, Federal Bureau of Prisons
The Federal Bureau of Prisons wants incumbent Naphcare to keep providing inpatient and outpatient facility and physician services to inmates at the Metropolitan Detention Center in Brooklyn from November 1 to November 30, 2026. The estimated cost is $889,017.86, through monthly purchase orders at its prior Medicare benchmark rates. The justification says 'Award of a CMS contract is pending the resolution of a protest,' and that a requirements contract is anticipated. The performance period began the day before the notice was posted. The pipeline's log shows an earlier Naphcare interim-services justification at the same $889,018 value.
