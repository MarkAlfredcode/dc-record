Top Story
South Korea's Korea Foundation Registers Its Washington Office Under FARA
The Korea Foundation, a statutory body funded by an endowment from Korean passport fees, filed for its D.C. overseas office as Korea Foundation USA.

Korea Foundation USA registered under the Foreign Agents Registration Act on October 8 as registrant #7777, with The Korea Foundation of South Korea as its only foreign principal (registrant listing). The registration statement says the registrant is not separately incorporated. It describes itself as the Washington overseas office of the foundation, operating under headquarters' internal regulations, approved plans, personnel arrangements and budgetary control. It lists a D.C. organization date of December 9, 2005, and names Sung Won Bae as director (registration statement).

The Exhibit AB says the foundation was established under the Korea Foundation Act and is categorized by Korea's Ministry of Finance and Economy as a Non-Classified Public Institution. It describes its funding as an endowment drawn from Korea's passport issuance fees, managed by the foundation (Exhibit AB). Its stated work is Korean studies, cultural and people-to-people exchange, research and dialogue. The OCR text available here is cut off before the answers on government supervision and financing, and shows no fee or contract terms.
