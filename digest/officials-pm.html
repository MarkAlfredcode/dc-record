Top Story
Rep. Michael T. McCaul filed his 2025 annual disclosure 62 days past his final extension
The House Clerk record shows a second McCaul filing, a periodic transaction report, posted the same day.

Deadline Watch flags Rep. Michael T. McCaul (R-TX-10) for filing his calendar-year 2025 annual financial disclosure on October 14, 2026, which is 62 days after his final extension date. That is a late disclosure.

The same day, a periodic transaction report under McCaul's name was posted. The text could not be parsed into trade lines because it is a scanned image or unusual layout, so nothing is reported here about its contents.

The rest of the window was quiet. There were no new Office of Government Ethics filings and no new FARA filings, and no new presidential or vice-presidential 278-Ts. Three lobbying reports name the President or Vice President as an entity lobbied (see the first section).
