Top Story
Rep. Maria Elvira Salazar discloses a wide sell-off of tech and industrial stocks
A single House filing lists dozens of partial sales dated September 24 across retirement and brokerage accounts.

Rep. Maria Elvira Salazar (R-FL27) filed a Periodic Transaction Report on 10/07/2026 listing roughly 50 transactions in a UBS IRA and brokerage account. Most are partial sales of $1,001 - $15,000 dated 09/24/2026, including Microsoft Corporation, Meta Platforms, Inc., UnitedHealth Group Incorporated, Boeing Company and Space Exploration Technologies Corp.; one full sale of Cisco Systems, Inc. was $15,001 - $50,000.

The filing also shows purchases on 09/02, 09/04, 09/08 and 09/22/2026 of Chevron Corporation, Snowflake Inc. (one $15,001 - $50,000), Okta, Inc., Corning Incorporated and Meta Platforms, Inc. The disclosed ranges are broad, and the filing does not state a reason for the trades.
