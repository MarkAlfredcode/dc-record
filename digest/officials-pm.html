Top Story
Rep. Pete Sessions Reports 32 Stock Sales in One Filing, With Three Positions at $250,001 to $500,000
Most of the sales are dated October 2 and carry a spouse ownership code.

Rep. Pete Sessions (R-TX17) filed a periodic transaction report on October 27 listing 32 transactions. Every one is a sale. The largest are Apple Inc., Costco Wholesale Corporation and Microsoft Corporation, each in the $250,001 to $500,000 range and each coded as spouse-owned and dated October 2. Read the filing.

Seventeen sales are dated October 2 and carry a spouse code, and one more spouse-owned sale (McDonald's Corporation) is dated October 27. The other 14 carry no owner code and are dated October 8 through October 28. The report lists an October 28 sale of Walt Disney Company shares even though its filing date is October 27. None of the trades is flagged as past the 45-day deadline.

The most clear-cut disclosure violation in today's batch is a different filing. Rep. Jodey Arrington (R-TX19) reported a July 10, 2025 sale that was disclosed 474 days after the trade, against a 45-day limit. Read the filing.

Seven House filings: one 474-day late disclosure and one 32-sale report

One is a purchase of Black Belt Energy Gas District (Alabama) project revenue bonds due 12/01/2035. The other is a sale of Tennessee Energy Acquisition bonds due 12/01/35. Filing.

Alphabet Class C and Amazon (October 2, spouse) are $100,001 to $250,000, as is a separate Costco sale on October 13.
