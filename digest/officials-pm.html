Top Story
Rep. Gilbert Cisneros discloses 57 trades in a month, 31 of them on two days
The periodic transaction report covers stocks plus three bond lines of $250,001 to $500,000 each.

Rep. Gilbert Cisneros (D-CA31) filed a periodic transaction report on October 12 listing 57 transactions dated September 3 through October 1, 2026: 29 purchases, 10 full sales, 17 partial sales and one exchange. Thirteen trades are dated September 18 and 18 are dated September 29. Filing.

The largest lines are municipal bonds: a purchase and a partial sale of California Community Choice Financing Authority revenue bonds and a purchase of Indiana Finance Authority health system revenue bonds, each $250,001 to $500,000. He also sold El Camino Community College District and Imperial Irrigation District bonds in the $100,001 to $250,000 range. The equity trades are mostly $1,001 to $15,000 and include semiconductor names (NXP Semiconductors, ON Semiconductor, Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Company, MACOM Technology Solutions) and WuXi AppTec. The input flags none of the trades as late.
