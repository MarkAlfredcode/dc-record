Top Story
Nancy Pelosi Reports Up to $1 Million Stake in San Francisco Office-Building LLC
A spouse-owned investment in a private LLC was the largest single transaction in today's disclosures.

Rep. Nancy Pelosi's periodic transaction report, filed October 2, lists a $500,001–$1,000,000 purchase on September 8 in REOF XXX, LLC, which the filing says is acquiring and managing a commercial office property at 225 Bush Street in San Francisco. The filing marks the owner as spouse.

Separately, Rep. Michael Rulli reported three trades of $1,001–$15,000 each in Goldman Sachs Group, Inc., Microsoft Corporation and Wells Fargo & Company. Beyond that, the day's FARA, LDA and Office of Government Ethics activity was routine.
