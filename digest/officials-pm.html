Top Story
Lobbying firm reports contacting the Pardon Office for a client
Mintz Levin's third-quarter report describes July and August communications with the Pardon Office on behalf of Abdeslam Jaidi.

Mintz Levin Cohn Ferris Glovsky and Popeo, P.C. reported $20,000 in lobbying income from Abdeslam Jaidi in its third-quarter Lobbying Disclosure Act report. The issue description says the firm communicated with the Pardon Office in July, submitting information from an application form the office had sent, and in August asked a question about a listing on the office's official website. Peter Chavkin is the named lobbyist.

It was the only new filing this window that mentions pardons or clemency.
