Top Story
SpaceX hires Akin Gump to lobby on space-launch and AI trade policy, with a former White House economic aide on the team
The new registration lists a former deputy director of the National Economic Council among its covered officials.

Akin Gump Strauss Hauer & Feld registered today to lobby for Space Exploration Technologies Corp. (SpaceX) on "issues related to trade policy regarding space launch and AI services." The filing lists manufacturing and trade as the issue areas.

Its covered positions include Kelly Ann Shaw, whose listed past posts are deputy assistant to the President for international economic affairs and deputy director of the National Economic Council, 2018-2019. It also lists Roger Murry, a former legislative assistant to Rep. Ellen Tauscher and Rep. Grace Napolitano.

Nothing else in today's input reaches a communication with the President, Vice President or a cabinet member on behalf of a foreign government. The two House stock-trade reports carry no late disclosures, and the Office of Government Ethics pass flagged nothing.
