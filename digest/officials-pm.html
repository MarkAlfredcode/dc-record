Top Story
FBI Director Kash Patel amends stock-trade report to add a MicroStrategy purchase omitted for about six months
An amended 278-T delivered to the oge@ inbox on Nov. 5 adds a $100,001–$250,000 purchase that was not in the original report.

Kashyap P. Patel, Director of the Federal Bureau of Investigation, amended his periodic transaction report (Form 278-T) to add a purchase of MicroStrategy, Inc (MSTR) stock on Nov. 21, 2025, valued at $100,001–$250,000. A Department of Justice cover letter dated May 28, 2026 forwards his May 26, 2026 amendment request. The original 278-T was signed Dec. 4, 2025 and certified by the U.S. Office of Government Ethics on Dec. 30, 2025. It listed one sale: ON Semiconductor Corporation (ON), Oct. 6, 2025, $50,001–$100,000.

Patel says the purchase was inadvertently left out because of a miscommunication. The MicroStrategy purchase was disclosed about 186 days after the transaction, well past the 45-day STOCK Act window, so it is a late disclosure. The report also notes that Patel received a 30-day filing extension. The document came from the OGE Form 201 requests filed automatically for the oge@markalfred.news inbox.

Also delivered Nov. 5: a certificate of divestiture for Todd Blanche (file 'Blanche, Todd OGE-2026-183-CDIV.pdf'). The summary available for this edition does not state his post or what he divested, so nothing more is reported here.
