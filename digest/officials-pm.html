Top Story
Quarterly lobbying reports flood in, led by a former Transportation Department aide's wide client roster
No new FARA items or financial disclosures today; the Q3 LDA deadline crush dominates the window.

The day's only new material is lobbying paperwork: 705 Lobbying Disclosure Act filings, 614 of them ordinary third-quarter reports. There were no new FARA items, no new House or Senate financial disclosures and no new ethics filings from the Office of Government Ethics in the window.

One pattern stands out in the reports with covered positions. H.A. Cumber & Company, Inc. lists Husein Cumber, a former Assistant to the Secretary and Deputy Chief of Staff at the U.S. Department of Transportation, as a covered former official on filings for clients far beyond transportation. Those include Blackstone Administrative Services Partnership, LP (financial regulation and AI policy, filing), Bausch & Lomb (Food and Drug Administration approvals, filing), Acronis AG (software procurement, filing) and Abra Group Limited (airline regulation and corporate financial disclosure approvals, filing).

Separately, Mercury Public Affairs, LLC reported $330,000 in income from a private citizen, Dr. Kocho Angjushev, for work on trade and commerce, with former Congressman Peter Kucik listed as a covered official (filing).
