Top Story
Senator Jim Justice Reports Partial Sale of Greenbrier Hotel Stake in the "Over $50,000,000" Range
The periodic transaction report was filed 09/28/2026 for an 08/14/2026 sale.

Senator James Conley Justice, II filed a periodic transaction report on 09/28/2026 with a single entry: a partial sale, dated 08/14/2026 and made by the senator himself, of non-public stock in Greenbrier Hotel Corporation and Affiliates of White Sulphur Springs, West Virginia, described in the filing as hospitality, with an amount of "Over $50,000,000." The filing lists no ticker and no buyer, and the filing as read does not say what the proceeds were or who purchased the stake.

Separately, Senator Sheldon Whitehouse reported five partial stock sales on 09/04/2026 in JP Morgan Chase & Co., Visa Inc. and Analog Devices, Inc., each $50,000 or less.
