Top Story
Sen. Tim Scott amends 2025 annual disclosure, reporting $94,563 in book royalties
The amendment was posted 10/06/2026; the filing does not say what changed.

Sen. Tim Scott filed Amendment 1 to his Annual Report for calendar year 2025. It lists $94,563 in royalties from HarperCollins Publishing LLC, retirement income from the South Carolina Retirement System, and spousal wages from MEB LLC of Charleston, South Carolina.

His spouse holds Amazon.com Inc. stock valued at $250,001–$500,000 and several $100,001–$250,000 funds, and the report lists five mortgages, the largest a $500,001–$1,000,000 loan to his spouse from Movement Mortgage (spelled "Movement Morgage" in the filing). The filing text does not identify which items were amended.
