Top Story
Lobbyist reports billing $20,000 for work on a pardon application
A third-quarter lobbying report shows Mintz Levin was paid $20,000 on behalf of Abdeslam Jaidi for communications with the Pardon Office.

Mintz Levin Cohn Ferris Glovsky and Popeo, P.C. reported $20,000 in income from client Abdeslam Jaidi in a third-quarter Lobbying Disclosure Act report. The issue description says that in July the firm communicated with the Pardon Office to submit information from the application form and confirm it was received, and in August asked a question about the listing on the office's official website. The listed lobbyist is Peter Chavkin.

The filing was flagged by the automated pardon-lobbying scan of new lobbying filings; it describes procedural contacts only and does not say whether a pardon was granted or recommended.
