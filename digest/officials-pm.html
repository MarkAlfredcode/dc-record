Kalshi hires Empire Consulting Group to lobby on prediction markets
Empire Consulting Group registered for the prediction-market exchange and, the same day, for the Business Roundtable.

Empire Consulting Group filed a new LDA registration for Kalshi Inc., a regulated prediction market exchange, covering "issues related to prediction markets and finance" under the Commodities and Financial Institutions/Investments/Securities issue areas. The filing lists former congressional staff as covered positions, including a former chief of staff to Rep. Robin Kelly and a former senior advisor to Rep. Gregory Meeks. Its Business Roundtable registration lists Michael Hayes, a former deputy director of the White House Office of Public Engagement and former National Security Council director.

A second new registration, for Innovation Council Action Inc. (super intelligence infrastructure and regulation), lists Nicholas Elliot, whose filed covered positions include senior advisor at the Commodity Futures Trading Commission on a White House detail. Neither filing shows contact with the President, the Vice President or a cabinet official.

No FARA filing or OGE ethics filing in this window involved the President, Vice President or cabinet. The Senate disclosure feed failed to load today, so Senate filings were not checked.
