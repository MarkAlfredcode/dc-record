Lobstermen's Group Hires Kelley Drye to Push Bill Extending Lobster Fishery Moratorium
A new lobbying registration cites H.R. 9436, the Northeast Lobstermens Protection Act of 2026.

Kelley Drye & Warren LLP registered to lobby for the New England Fishermen's Stewardship Association, a trade association representing commercial fishermen. The filing lists the Northeast Lobstermens Protection Act of 2026 (H.R. 9436), described as an extension of a moratorium on Northeast lobster fishery Marine Mammal Protection Act restrictions.

It was among 21 new registrations posted since October 2. The filing's description was truncated in this summary; read the full filing for the complete issue text.
