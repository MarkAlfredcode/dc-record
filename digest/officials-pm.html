House members who filed 6 or more periodic transaction reports (STOCK Act trade disclosures) in the year before the last 90 days and none since, per the House Clerk's disclosure index. Silence doesn't say why: the member may have sold off holdings, moved assets into a blind trust or index fund, been covered by a new law, or just not traded -- and trades are reportable up to 45 days late, so the latest ones may not be filed yet.
