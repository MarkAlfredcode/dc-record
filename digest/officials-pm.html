Top Story
Rep. Laurel Lee's Spouse's Bank of America Sale Disclosed 53 Days After the Trade
The periodic transaction report was filed eight days past the STOCK Act's 45-day window.

Rep. Laurel Lee (FL-15) filed a periodic transaction report on October 16 showing two transactions in accounts marked as owned by her spouse. One was a late disclosure: a sale of Bank of America Corporation common stock worth $50,001 to $100,000 on August 24, reported 53 days later, eight days beyond the 45-day deadline in the STOCK Act.

The same report shows a purchase of Nokia shares (ticker NOKBF) worth $15,001 to $50,000 on September 18, which was within the window. The input shows no other late trades among the three House filings reviewed today, and the Office of Government Ethics summary reports only routine annual disclosures.
