Top Story
Rep. Maria Elvira Salazar discloses 42 stock trades, most a September 24 sell-off
Her October 7 periodic transaction report is dominated by partial sales in a UBS IRA, including a stake in SpaceX.

Rep. Maria Elvira Salazar (R-FL27) filed a periodic transaction report on October 7, 2026 listing 42 stock transactions: 33 sales and 9 purchases. 28 of the sales are dated September 24, 2026, mostly partial sales in her UBS IRA, spanning Boeing Company, Goldman Sachs Group, Inc., Microsoft Corporation, Meta Platforms, Inc., UnitedHealth Group Incorporated and Space Exploration Technologies Corp. (SPCX).

Purchases cluster earlier in September: Okta, Inc. and Snowflake Inc. on September 4, Corning Incorporated on September 8, Meta Platforms, Inc. on September 22 and Chevron Corporation on September 2. Each line is $1,001–$15,000 except a full Cisco Systems, Inc. sale and a Snowflake Inc. purchase, both $15,001–$50,000. Notification dates are October 1, 2026 for every line.
