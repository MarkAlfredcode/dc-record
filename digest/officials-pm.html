Top Story
Quiet Saturday: no disclosures, FARA filings or top-official contacts; lobbying amendments dominate
The only new material today is 62 lobbying filings, led by a former National Security Council aide's defense-industry client list and a string of amended Lyft reports.

No House or Senate financial disclosures, no FARA filings and no filings naming the President, Vice President or a cabinet official were reported in this window. The Office of Government Ethics reported no new filings, and the deterministic extras (deadlines, ethics agreements, presidential trades, pardon lobbying, stopped traders) found nothing.

In lobbying, the most notable pattern is Aubrey Maturin LLC, whose third-quarter reports list Adrielle Churchill Wiese, a former National Security Council senior director for legislative affairs, as a covered former official lobbying for eight defense and manufacturing clients, including Thales Defense & Security, Inc. (report).

Separately, Lyft filed amended reports for all four quarters of a prior year span, with amounts ranging from $170,000 to $480,000 in expenses across the filings listed below.
