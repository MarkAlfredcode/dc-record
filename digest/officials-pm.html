Contacts With the President, VP & Cabinet
No logged contacts with the President, VP or cabinet; two lobbying reports name the President

The two FARA informational-materials filings by Friends of Sinn Fein, Inc. (foreign principal Sinn Fein, Ireland) quote press coverage and statements from Irish-American groups about President Donald Trump's public comments on a united Ireland. They do not log any contact with him, so they are not reported as communications.

Two third-quarter lobbying reports name the President:

  • National Affordable Housing Management Association (NAHMA) (in-house): flagged as naming President Donald Trump. The lobbying text captured covers H.R. 8626, the Workforce Housing Tax Credit Act, and the One Big Beautiful Bill Act (Public Law 119-21). Filing.
  • Air-Conditioning, Heating, and Refrigeration Institute (in-house): lists the President among the entities lobbied. Topics include climate policy, heat pumps, PFAS and the H.R. 8016 / S. 4153 Forever Chemical Regulation and Accountability Act of 2026, refrigerants and HFCs under the AIM Act, and ENERGY STAR. Filing.
 
LDA Registrations & Activity
15 new registrations and 37 terminations, mostly routine

The lobbying feed for October 13 onward lists 750 filings, but the fetch stopped after 30 pages, so the rest are not counted: 607 third-quarter reports, 78 third-quarter no-activity reports, 17 third-quarter no-activity terminations, 16 third-quarter terminations, 15 registrations, 6 third-quarter amendments, 3 second-quarter terminations, 3 second-quarter amendments, and 1 each of first-quarter amendment, fourth-quarter amendment, fourth-quarter termination amendment, second-quarter no-activity report and second-quarter report.

Standouts: Groisman LLC registered for Apollo Power America, Inc., with the foreign entities Solarpaint Ltd. and Apollo Power Ltd. listed, to lobby on Section 232 tariff treatment of Israeli-origin polysilicon products. Cornerstone Government Affairs, Inc. registered for Welltower Inc. with Andrew Flick, a former special assistant to the President and senior adviser in the Office of the Vice President, listed as a covered position. The largest spender seen was the Securities Industry and Financial Markets Association at $1.81 million in expenses.
