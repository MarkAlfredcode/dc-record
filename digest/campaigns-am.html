Top Story
Rep. Scott Perry's campaign says a $7,000 WinRed donation went through 14 times in one hour
Patriots for Perry told the FEC it refunded the duplicates before the donor's card was charged.

Patriots for Perry, the campaign committee of Rep. Scott Perry (R-PA), filed a Form 99 on 10/24 saying that Roy Alexander's $7,000 contribution on 10/22/2026 through WinRed "went through 14 times." The committee says it caught the problem within the same hour and refunded the duplicate transactions.

According to the filing, the donor's card was not charged for the duplicates, and the committee never received the funds or paid processing fees on them. The committee says it reported the one valid $7,000 donation on a Form 6 48-hour report filed 10/24/26. The filing is signed by Treasurer Joel Jukus. These are the committee's own statements, and the input does not include the 48-hour report itself.

The rest of today's batch is small and mostly routine: small committees saying they are under the $5,000 reporting threshold, and one PAC explaining a cash balance discrepancy.

Sitting Lawmakers
Two sitting House members' committees filed explanatory Form 99s

Two committees tied to sitting House members filed Form 99 notes in the last three days. One explains a burst of duplicate card transactions, the other amends a 48-hour report.

  • Patriots for Perry tells the FEC that a $7,000 WinRed contribution from Roy Alexander went through 14 times and the duplicates were refunded. The committee says the donor's card was never charged for the duplicates and that it reported the one valid $7,000 donation on a Form 6 48-hour report filed 10/24/26. The committee belongs to Rep. Scott Perry (R-PA). (Form 99)
  • Self for Congress amends its 10/20/26 48-hour report to say a donation from Working for Ohio, postmarked 10/20/26, was received 10/23/26. The note references FEC filing FEC-2034640 and belongs to Rep. Keith Self (R-TX). It was received by the FEC 10/23. (Form 99)
Form 99 Correspondence
Threshold statements, a termination question and a PAC's balance explanation

Six Form 99s came from non-lawmakers. Four say a committee is under the $5,000 registration or reporting threshold or ask to close, one explains a replacement quarterly file after a treasurer change, and one is a PAC's reply to an FEC request about reported negative balances. No RFAI letters appeared in this batch.

  • EnergySolutions, Inc. Fund for Effective Government tells the FEC that reports of negative balances were data entry errors and that its cash never fell below roughly $100,000. The PAC (EnergySolutions PAC) says it audited its books after an FEC request and that cash stayed between about $100,000 and $130,000. It reports a closing balance of $125,193.95 as of October 14, 2026, and says it made a line 26 entry in its October 20 monthly report reflecting the August 31, 2026 closing balance. The filing spells the name "Energey Solutions." (Form 99)
  • McGinnis for the People tells the FEC it sent a replacement second quarter file on October 21, 2026 to fix a deficit and mismatches. The committee of independent House candidate Brian McGinnis (North Carolina) says a change in treasurer left the original second quarter report unassociated with the first quarter report. The note is signed by Treasurer Frederick Clarkson and was received 10/23. (Form 99)
  • Friends of Cousin Pat asks the FEC whether a $0 raised, under-$200 spent committee can terminate its reporting requirement. The committee of West Virginia House challenger Patrick Joseph Carney says it collected $0, below the $5,000 threshold, and spent less than $200 on mailbox and shipping costs for first quarter filings. It says it has no debts and no longer intends to raise or spend money. (Form 99)
  • Lam4Aloha - Adriel for Congress tells the FEC it had not met the $5,000 reporting threshold as of October 14, 2026. The committee of Hawaii Republican House challenger Adriel C. Lam gave no further detail. It was received 10/23. (Form 99)
  • Shimp for President tells the FEC that its 2028 presidential campaign has not passed $5,000 in contributions or expenditures as of October 24, 2026. Candidate Fogel G. Shimp (independent, Pennsylvania) asks the FEC to associate the statement with the committee's record. (Form 99)
  • Shimp for President files a second statement saying it was under the $5,000 threshold through June 30, 2026. The note concerns the second quarter (April 1 to June 30), whose original reporting deadline was July 15, 2026, and was submitted October 24, 2026, the same day as the other Shimp statement. (Form 99)
