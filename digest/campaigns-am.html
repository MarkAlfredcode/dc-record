Top Story
Two Committees Trace the Same Restitution Debt to One Former Treasurer's Embezzlement
BackPAC and Friends of Don Beyer, professionally managed from the same Arlington, Virginia address, each told the FEC this week that a debt owed to them is restitution from their former shared treasurer's guilty plea to embezzlement.

In near-identical letters filed with the Federal Election Commission on September 22, 2026, BackPAC and Friends of Don Beyer — the campaign committee of Rep. Don Beyer (D-VA-8) — responded to Requests for Additional Information about debts each had disclosed on Schedule D of its 2026 July Quarterly report. Both letters explain that the debt reflects "court-ordered restitution owed to the Committee by the Committee's former treasurer."

That former treasurer was Katherine Buchanan. Both filings state she "has pleaded guilty to embezzlement and a federal court has ordered her to pay restitution to her victims, including the Committee." Both committees say they have already submitted information about the matter to the FEC's Office of General Counsel.

The FEC's original inquiry to each committee went further, telling both that the debt "appears to be related to an unauthorized use of committee funds" — for now noted only as a receivable on Schedule D, not resolved. Both committees are currently managed from the same Arlington address (2503-D N. Harrison St., Box #310) under the same current treasurer, Allison Murray, meaning the fallout from one treasurer's embezzlement is now on the books of two separate federal committees at once.
