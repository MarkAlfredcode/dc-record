No New FEC Compliance Filings to Report Today
Every RFAI, Form 99, and voided/negative transaction in this window was already covered by an earlier edition compiled today or yesterday.

This is the second Campaigns edition compiled today. GitHub Actions remains billing-blocked, so this edition is a manual catch-up run following this morning's own manual run, which already fetched and reported everything that appeared in the FEC filings API's 3-day lookback window (2026-09-26 through 2026-09-29).

Re-querying the FEC filings API just now turned up 31 RFAI (Request for Additional Information) and Form 99 filings across that window — 10 RFAIs and 21 Form 99s, including two termination filings — but every one of them matches a file_number/image-number/submission-ID already recorded in this edition's dedupe manifest as reported on 2026-09-28 or earlier today. None qualify as new.

The Voided & Negative-Amount Transactions sweep (FEC Schedule A/B, $10,000-or-more negative) also came back empty for this window: zero qualifying rows.

The automated red-flag keyword scan flagged one filing — a Request for Additional Information sent to Asian Legal Aid's presidential committee — for the word "fraud." On inspection, the hit is the FEC's own standard penalty-clause boilerplate (citing 52 U.S.C. § 30109's prohibition on "materially false, fictitious, or fraudulent" statements to the government), which appears on RFAI cover language generally, not a substantive allegation specific to this filer. That filing was also already reported in the 2026-09-28 edition, so it isn't new regardless.
