Top Story
Valero Energy PAC Flagged for Maxing Out to Both of Rep. Ashley Hinson's Campaign Committees
An FEC compliance letter flags $5,000 contributions to Hinson's old House account and her new Senate committee in the same primary — more than a multicandidate PAC is normally allowed to give one candidate in one election.

The FEC sent Valero Energy Corporation Political Action Committee a Request for Additional Information on September 20 over its July Monthly Report, flagging two $5,000 contributions in the 2026 primary election that, taken together, appear to exceed the $5,000-per-candidate-per-election cap a multicandidate committee is allowed to give. According to the letter's attachment, Valero PAC gave $5,000 to Ashley Hinson for Congress on April 30, 2025, and another $5,000 to Ashley for Iowa on June 1, 2026 — both designated to the 2026 primary.

FEC committee records confirm the connection: Ashley for Iowa is registered as a U.S. Senate principal campaign committee under candidate ID H0IA01174 — the same ID originally assigned to Rep. Ashley Hinson (R-IA-2) as a House candidate. Hinson's old House committee, Ashley Hinson for Congress, remains open even as she runs for Senate in 2026, meaning a PAC that maxes out to both is effectively giving $10,000 to the same candidate in what the FEC treats as one election.

The letter tells Valero PAC's treasurer, Carl Crawford, that the committee must either obtain a refund of the excess or secure written authorization to redesignate one of the contributions within 60 days of receipt, and must report back to the Commission on which corrective step it took. A response is due by October 26, 2026.
