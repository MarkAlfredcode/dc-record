Top Story
A negative $83,210.54 receipt from UBS Financial Services appears on Darrell Issa for Congress's books
The FEC record carries no description for the reversal, and the filing itself doesn't say why it was made.

An itemized receipts entry (Schedule A) for Darrell Issa for Congress shows -$83,210.54 with counterparty UBS Financial Services Inc, dated March 31, 2026. FEC's convention is that a large negative amount marks a voided, reversed or refunded transaction. The description field on the record is blank, so the filing doesn't say what was reversed or why.

That is all the record shows. Whether the entry reflects a returned contribution, a corrected earlier entry or a movement of committee funds can't be determined from it. Transaction record.

Separately, today's batch includes a Form 99 that tripped the 'fraud' keyword scan. On a full read it is a rambling statement from Jason Changkyu Kim about his own election-fraud claims, not a compliance disclosure (see Form 99 Correspondence).

Form 99 Correspondence
Eight Form 99s: mostly 'below the $5,000 threshold' notices ahead of the October Quarterly, plus one rambling political statement

No new RFAI letters surfaced in this edition's window, and no filings carry a sitting-lawmaker tag. Most of the eight Form 99s are small campaigns telling the FEC they haven't reached the $5,000 raising/spending threshold that triggers regular reporting, ahead of the October 15 quarterly deadline. One is a free-form political statement that hit the 'fraud' keyword scan, and one has no text at all.
