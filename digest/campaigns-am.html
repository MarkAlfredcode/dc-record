Top Story
Tiny super PAC admits late 48-hour reports and $23,870 donor refund tangle
Vote Courage 4 Democracy Fund, formed in August, explained itself in a Form 99 filed October 5.

Vote Courage 4 Democracy Fund, an independent-expenditure-only committee formed in August 2026, filed a Form 99 on October 5 after notices from the FEC's Reports Analysis Division. Secretary and assistant treasurer James A. Barnett Jr. says the committee filed a paper Form 3X on August 14 without realizing that filing its Form 1 electronically had opted it into electronic filing, and that it will refile the 3X electronically with new transactions.

He also says the committee mistook its paper 3X for a 48-hour independent-expenditure report and is now filing the 48-hour report late for expenditures of $13,470 and $10,000.

The letter further says Barnett asked George Jordan to give personally so the fund could refund $23,870 in earlier donations from Jordan's business, Ansercall LLC. The fund's bank attributed the gifts to Jordan personally, which Barnett says was correct under the single-member-LLC rules, but they had already been refunded. The committee asks for leniency on late reports and says it expects no further spending above $10,000 this cycle.
