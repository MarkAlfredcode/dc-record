Top Story
Seafarers union PAC says its former treasurer diverted another $13,064.50, including three candidate refunds, all repaid by its insurer
A November 4 Form 99 expands the embezzlement the committee first described in June.

Seafarers Political Activity Donation - Seafarers International Union of NA-AGLIW tells the FEC it has found more diversions of committee money by its former treasurer, Chelsea Diab (formerly Chelsea D. Heindel). It says she intercepted payroll-deducted member contributions of $1,407 in 2023, $1,211 in 2024 and $2,946.50 in 2025, and sent them to her personal bank account. The committee reported these amounts as unitemized Line 11a memo entries on the September Monthly Report.

The filing also says Diab intercepted three refunds of $2,500 each from federal candidate committees: Sean Patrick Maloney for Congress (original contribution November 3, 2022, which the filing says she improperly reported as voided on August 29, 2024), Brian Fitzpatrick For All Of Us (October 17, 2024) and Pat Ryan for Congress (June 11, 2024). The committee says all of the newly found losses, $13,064.50 in total, were covered by insurance and reimbursed by Chubb and Son Federal Insurance Company. It reported the August 31, 2026 reimbursement on Line 17. The filing answers an FEC letter dated October 8, 2026 and builds on two Form 99 letters dated June 18, 2026.

Also filed this week, United for Progress says it amended and added independent-expenditure reports on June 2026 spending for Adriano Espaillat on November 2, more than four months after the primary. The committee also said that it is unsure whether two of the added reports were required. Separately, the Cory Steele for President committee filed six Form 99s on November 4 that carry hourly election-night vote tallies.

Requests for Additional Information
A quarterly-deadline cycle brings 1,446 more RFAIs, almost all October Quarterly non-filer notices

Separate passes write one-liners for a bulk batch of 1,439 RFAIs, which a script appends below my items. Of those, 542 went to candidate committees and 897 to PACs and party committees. 1,419 cite the October Quarterly 2026 report, and the rest cite pre-general, July Quarterly, September Monthly and other reports. 1,433 were received on 11/03 and 6 on 11/04. 1,413 list no numbered items, which matches the failure-to-file pattern of the letters shown here.
