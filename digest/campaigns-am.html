Top Story
FEC flags $7,438.74 'repayment of unauthorized disbursements' at The Existentialist Republic PAC
The Miami-based PAC was also sent a second FEC letter on its termination report the same day.

The FEC sent The Existentialist Republic PAC a request for additional information on its July Quarterly Report (04/01/2026 to 06/30/2026). The letter says Schedule A supporting Line 17 discloses receipts described as 'Repayment of unauthorized disbursements' totaling $7,438.74. The FEC says the receipts 'appear to be related to an apparent unauthorized use of Committee funds.' It adds that it may take further legal action, but that any clarifying information the committee provides will be taken into consideration. The letter is addressed to treasurer Chris Armitage, and the response is due 11/30/2026.

The PAC also received a separate RFAI the same day on its termination report. That PDF has no text layer, so what it asks could not be read. The filing record shows only that it is an RFAI on a termination report, received 10/25. The first letter does not say whose funds were used or who repaid them. A keyword hit is a lead, not a finding.

Sitting Lawmakers
Three Virginia House members draw October Quarterly RFAIs

Three Virginia Republicans and Democrats who sit in the House received RFAIs on their October Quarterly reports, all received 10/25 with responses due 11/30/2026. The letters center on missing 48-hour notices for last-minute contributions. Rep. Robert J. Wittman (R-VA) faces the longest list: five items on prohibited and excessive contributions.

  • FEC asks Rep. John J. McGuire III (R-VA) why McGuire for Virginia may have missed 48-hour notices for last-minute contributions, and why only part of a contribution from Elaine E. Beck was reported. The committee filed a 48-hour notice for a 'last minute' Beck contribution on 7/20/2026, but only a partial amount appears on Schedule A. Treasurer is Jason D. Boles. (RFAI)
  • FEC questions Rep. Robert J. Wittman (R-VA) on five items at Rob Wittman for Congress, from apparent corporate and labor contributions to excessive contributions and LLC gifts. The letter also asks about contributions from unregistered organizations and a 7/30/2026 'last minute' contribution from Fidelity Brokerage Services LLC that is missing from Schedule A. It flags an incorrect committee ID (C90015249) for Independent Insurance Agents & Brokers of America Inc. Treasurer is Chris Marston. (RFAI)
  • FEC asks Rep. Robert C. 'Bobby' Scott (D-VA) about apparent excessive contributions at Scott for Congress and Schedule B memo entries that inflate cash on hand. It also asks about possibly missed 48-hour notices and itemized disbursements to other candidate committees that lack state, office sought and district. Treasurer is Sean M. Williamson. (RFAI)
Requests for Additional Information
Eighteen more RFAIs, mostly cash-balance mismatches, undisclosed joint fundraising partners and loan questions

Eighteen RFAIs cover challengers, open-seat and presidential candidates and one PAC. Most are routine FEC template letters, all with responses due 11/30/2026. The standouts are the PAC letter in the Top Story and two Virginia and Florida campaigns whose transfers came from joint fundraising committees they never disclosed. Three of the 18 are RFAIs on termination reports; their PDFs have no text layer. All 18 are candidate committees except The Existentialist Republic PAC. Latz4Kansas received three RFAIs sharing treasurer Richard Korentager.

  • FEC flags $7,438.74 in 'repayment of unauthorized disbursements' reported by The Existentialist Republic PAC as an apparent unauthorized use of committee funds. PAC. July Quarterly Report. See the Top Story. (RFAI)
  • FEC asks Beth Macy for Congress why it reports transfers from Virginia Congressional Victory Fund 2026 as joint fundraising proceeds when that fund is not on its Statement of Organization. Candidate committee of Democratic challenger Beth Macy (VA). Treasurer is Andrew Madras. (RFAI)
  • FEC asks Tom Perriello for Congress why transfers from Perriello Victory Fund and Virginia Congressional Victory Fund 2026 are not backed by joint fundraising representatives on its Statement of Organization. Candidate committee of Democratic challenger Tom Perriello (VA). Treasurer is Lauren Decot Lee. (RFAI)
  • FEC asks Driscoll for Congress why transfers from LaLota Victory Fund are not tied to a joint fundraising representative on its Statement of Organization. Candidate committee of Republican challenger Jeanine Driscoll (NY). July Quarterly Report. Treasurer is Thomas Datwyler. (RFAI)
  • FEC asks Casey for Congress to disclose the interest rate on five loans from Casey Askar totaling $4.19 million, and why transfers from 'Grow the Majority' lack a joint fundraising representative. The loans are $250,000 on 10/8/2026, $2,000,000 on 10/14/2026, $640,000 on 8/10/2026, $300,000 on 9/22/2026 and $1,000,000 on 9/28/2026. Form 99 Correspondence
Two disavowal items, two termination requests and a below-threshold statement

Five non-termination Form 99s were filed or received over the weekend. Two are termination requests sent as free-text Form 99s, and one is an FEC disavowal notice to a New York candidate. All were received 10/25.

  • FEC notifies Dennis Hannon, a Republican challenger in New York, that his campaign appears to have received or spent more than $5,000 and gives him 35 days to disavow it or file a Statement of Candidacy. The letter says Hannon for Congress has filed reports. Candidate ID H6NY26131. Response due 11/30/2026. (Form 99)
  • Celina Wilson for Congress Committee asks the FEC to terminate, saying it never received contributions, made expenditures or carried debt. The filing, signed George Wilson, says all activity is being done in another committee, C00954578. (Form 99)
  • Kathy McKinstry Campaign tells the FEC it has not met the $5,000 reporting threshold. Ohio challenger. The one-line filing gives no further detail. (Form 99)
  • Kirkland for Congress asks the FEC to terminate 'ASAP', reporting no cash on hand and no debts as of 7/31/26. The filing from Walter Kirkland, a Maryland open-seat Democrat, says no matters are pending and asks for a call back. (Form 99)
  • NATO files a disavowal response with the FEC. The committee is tagged to presidential candidates Bendu Brown (Independent) and Dave Sunday (Green). The one-page form carries no statement text beyond the committee name and Philadelphia address. (Form 99)
