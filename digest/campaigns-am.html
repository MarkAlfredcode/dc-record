Top Story
Union PAC tells FEC it received insurance money for "additional misappropriations" of committee funds
The FEC's October 8 letter to the Seafarers International Union's PAC calls the $13,064.50 receipt related to an apparent unauthorized use of committee funds.

The Seafarers Political Activity Donation - Seafarers International Union of North America (AGLIW) reported a receipt described as "Insurance recovery for additional misappropriations found See memo" totaling $13,064.50 on Schedule A of its September Monthly report (covering August 1-31, 2026). In an RFAI dated October 8 to treasurer Thomas Orzechowski, Jr., the FEC said the receipt "appears to be related to an apparent unauthorized use of Committee funds," and that the Commission may take further legal action while it will consider any clarifying information. The word "additional" suggests earlier misappropriations were found. The response is due November 12, 2026.

Two other RFAIs the same day also cite apparent unauthorized disbursements. The FEC flagged Bish for Congress disbursements described as fraud charges ($483.27 and $1,000.00) and Wells Fargo provisional credits of $5,001.21 and $6,192.30, and asked which expenditures the credits offset. It flagged a $4,767.41 "DISPUTED ACH DEBIT" by the EchoStar Corporation and DISH Network Corporation PAC.
