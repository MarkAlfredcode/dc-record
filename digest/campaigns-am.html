Top Story
FEC Presses Daines Campaign on Refunds Tied to His March Retirement Announcement
A compliance letter filed Sept. 16 requires Steve Daines' campaign to refund or return contributions collected after his March 2026 decision not to seek re-election, and flags a separate excessive contribution refunded well past the FEC's 60-day deadline.

On September 16, 2026, the FEC sent a Request for Additional Information to Steve Daines For Montana, the campaign committee of Sen. Steve Daines (R-MT), regarding its April Quarterly Report. The letter notes that Daines “announc[ed] on 3/4/2026 not to seek office/re-election,” ending his status as an active 2026 candidate, and instructs the committee that primary-election contributions accepted after that announcement must be refunded — except to the extent the committee had net debts outstanding for the primary — and that any general-election contributions collected must be returned outright, since Daines will not compete in a general election.

The letter separately flags $3,500 contributions from Michael Gearon in March 2025 and again in March 2026 that together exceeded the $3,500-per-election limit; the committee's $3,500 refund in June 2026 came after the 60-day window the FEC allows for a no-penalty correction, prompting the Commission to instruct the campaign to refund the remaining excessive amount “immediately.” The committee has until October 21, 2026 to respond in full.

The filing is one of six RFAI and Form 99 filings in today's edition tied to sitting members of Congress — see Sitting Lawmakers below for the rest.
