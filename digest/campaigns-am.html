Top Story
FEC sends 23 RFAIs in three days, including three to sitting House members, but none of the letters could be read
No filing in this batch could be described beyond its filing record, so nothing in it can be called a finding.

Of the 32 new filings, 23 are requests for additional information (RFAIs), one is a Form 99 and eight are termination approvals. The FEC's PDFs for the RFAIs and the Form 99 could not be read, so what each letter asks is unknown. The filing records show the committee, the report in question and the date received, and little else.

Three RFAIs went to committees of sitting House members: Rep. Donald S. Beyer, Jr. (D-VA), Rep. Mario Diaz-Balart (R-FL) and Rep. Virginia Foxx (R-NC). All three concern October Quarterly 2026 reports. Several committees received two letters at once, including Dr. Annie Andrews for Senate, the South Carolina Democratic Senate challenger, and Catholic Engagement PAC.

The batch is dated close to Election Day, which is Tuesday, November 3. Nothing in the available material points to personal use, embezzlement or another red-flag issue.
