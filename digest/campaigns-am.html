Top Story
Court orders former treasurer to repay $29,413.77 to Forward Together PAC
The committee told the FEC on Tuesday, in answer to an RFAI, that restitution is owed to it.

Forward Together PAC filed a Form 99 on October 6 answering a September 1 Request for Additional Information on its 2026 July Quarterly Report. The FEC's letter had noted a Schedule D debt described as "Court-ordered restitution payment owed to the Committee" and said it appeared tied to an unauthorized use of committee funds.

The committee says a federal court ordered its former treasurer, Katherine Buchanan, to pay $29,413.77, that it first explained this in a Form 99 on July 20, 2026, and that it is still working with the FEC. It cites FEC advisory opinions 1991-38 and 2005-04 for carrying the amount as a debt owed to it.

Form 99 Correspondence
Committees answer RFAIs; a former treasurer's restitution is the standout

Most are replies to September RFAIs. Forward Together PAC reports court-ordered restitution from its former treasurer; New Era Leadership admits a late independent-expenditure report.

  • Forward Together PAC — answers a September 1 RFAI on its July Quarterly Report. The committee reports a federal court ordered its former treasurer, Katherine Buchanan, to pay $29,413.77 in restitution, carried as a debt owed to the committee on Schedule D; it says it disclosed this on a July 20 Form 99 and continues to work with the FEC. Response.
  • New Era Leadership — admits a $25,000 supplemental advertising buy with AL Media LLC (part of a $150,000 buy) was disclosed 48 days late, on July 20, because a separate 24-hour independent-expenditure report was never filed by June 2. It says it has amended the reports, filed the late report, and notes the supported candidate lost. Response.
  • States Win — amended its July Quarterly: a 05/12/26 contribution from Vote Save America was reported as $5,000 but was $10,000 (refund pending), and a $5,000 transfer to its non-federal account, States Win, Inc., had been omitted. Response.

  • Ola Victory Fund — says a $24,000 contribution of 12/31/25 from candidate Ola Nesheswat Hawatmeh's personal funds went through its link by mistake and was allocated entirely to People for Ola, so it was not excessive; it also asks to complete a pending termination review. Response.
  • Johnson Leadership Fund — says a contribution from Yum Brands Inc Good Government Fund (issued 02/12/26, deposited 05/07/26) was reported accurately and the event expenses were catering, site rental and travel. Response.
