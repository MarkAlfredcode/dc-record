Top Story
Small PAC tells the FEC its former treasurer repaid money he misappropriated
The Existentialist Republic PAC says former treasurer Clifford Michael Pickens repaid all $7,438.74 he moved into an unauthorized account, and it asks the FEC to approve its termination.

In a Form 99 filed October 31, The Existentialist Republic PAC says three receipts on Schedule A, Line 17 of its July Quarterly Report are repayments by former treasurer Clifford Michael Pickens. They cover the $7,438.74 in committee funds he deposited into an unauthorized account, plus $61.26 reported as reimbursement of filing fees. The committee says it first reported the misappropriation to the FEC on a Form 99 on June 2, 2026. Pickens repaid $3,500.00 on June 9 and $3,938.74 plus the $61.26 on June 15.

The filing says eight unauthorized disbursements to Pickens are itemized on Schedule B, Line 29. They total $4,593.82 on the amended April Quarterly Report and $2,844.92 on the July Quarterly Report, which sum to the same $7,438.74. The committee says it has refunded all $8,648.00 in contributions through ActBlue, as reported on its Termination Report. It says it holds no cash and no debts, and that it did not pursue the complaints or civil action described in its June 2 Form 99 after Pickens repaid in full.

The account comes from the committee's own filing, and the PDF does not say whether the FEC has reviewed it. The committee is represented by treasurer Christopher Armitage. A second copy of the same document was also received October 31.

Requests for Additional Information
Ten PAC and party RFAIs, all dated November 1 with December 7 response deadlines

All ten new RFAIs went to PACs or party committees. No candidate committees are among them. All were received November 1 and carry a December 7, 2026 response due date. They are mostly routine template letters: negative cash balances, contributions to candidates for elections that had already happened, and incomplete donor or debt disclosures. No treasurer appears on more than one letter.

  • FEC asks Debt Relief PAC why Schedule B shows contributions to candidates for the 2026 primary that were paid after the election date. The letter covers the amended June Monthly Report received 10/09. Treasurer is Marjorie Sinclair. The committee must obtain a refund or a redesignation, or amend to mark the money as debt retirement. (RFAI)
  • FEC raises the same late-contribution problem with Zoetis Inc. PAC (Zoetis PAC). The October Monthly Report letter uses the same template as the Debt Relief PAC letter, asking for the date and method of any refund or redesignation. Treasurer is Mahlet H. Makonnen. (RFAI)
  • Decouple China PAC is asked to explain a negative ending cash balance of $2,254.01. The July Quarterly Report suggests an overdrawn account, a math error or an unreported debt. The letter also notes for future filings that Schedule H4 activity paid entirely with federal funds belongs on Schedule B, Line 21(b). Treasurer is Paul Boardman. (RFAI)
  • Pakistani American Public Affairs Committee PAK-PAC is asked about a negative ending cash balance of $30,042.13. The figure is on its 12 Day Pre-General Report, which covers 10/01 to 10/14. Treasurer is Sobia Shakeel. (RFAI)
  • FEC asks Growing Our Own Dynamic Economy Now about contributions that appear to exceed the $5,000 per-year limit. The October Quarterly Report letter says the committee may have to refund the excess or reattribute it within 60 days. Treasurer is Robert Phillips III. (RFAI)
  • Good Growth is asked whether it failed to file 24-hour reports on last-minute independent expenditures. The October Quarterly Report letter also says other federal operating expenses were put on the wrong lines of the Detailed Summary Page. Treasurer is Steven Mele. (RFAI)
  • La Bamba PAC is told its April Quarterly beginning cash does not match the ending balance of its amended Year-End Report. The amended reports were both received 10/12. Treasurer is Andrew Madras. (RFAI)
