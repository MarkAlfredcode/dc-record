A quiet weekend: no new lobbying registrations, contributions, or FARA filings
The only Senate LDA activity posted overnight was two routine quarterly activity reports; DOJ's FARA system logged zero new or terminated registrants.

Today's edition covers a genuinely slow stretch for federal lobbying and foreign-agent disclosure. The Senate Office of Public Records' Lobbying Disclosure Act database shows zero new lobbying registrations (Form LD-1) and zero LD-203 lobbyist campaign-contribution reports posted since yesterday. The only filings that came in were two routine quarterly activity reports (Form LD-2) from existing registrants.

DOJ's FARA e-file system was similarly quiet: no new foreign-agent registrants and no terminations were posted in the same window.

Weekend and holiday-adjacent stretches routinely produce little to no filing activity in both systems, since most registrants and their compliance staff file on business days.

K Street
No new registrations or lobbyist contributions; two quarterly reports on file

No new lobbying registrations (Form LD-1) and no LD-203 lobbyist campaign-contribution filings were posted to the Senate LDA database in the coverage window. Two existing registrants filed routine quarterly activity reports (Form LD-2).

  • Hollinger Group Consulting filed a 1st Quarter 2026 (Jan. 1–Mar. 31) activity report for client Agile Patriot, described in the filing as a military-construction-focused (MILCON) client, covering military construction issues. View filing.
  • Viking Navigation LLC filed a 2nd Quarter 2026 (Apr. 1–June 30) activity report for the Kansas Department of Commerce, the state's lead agency for promoting Kansas. The report lists activity across a broad range of issue areas — agriculture, budget/appropriations, disaster planning, Medicare/Medicaid, transportation, veterans and others — most of it described as monitoring or coordinating with federal agencies and the state's congressional delegation on funding, disaster declarations and rural broadband/healthcare programs, rather than lobbying on specific bill numbers. View filing.
