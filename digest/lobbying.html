A quiet weekend on K Street and at DOJ's FARA unit: nothing new posted since Saturday
Senate lobbying disclosure filings, LD-203 contribution reports, and DOJ's foreign-agent registry all show zero new activity in the window this edition covers.

This edition's coverage window runs from Saturday, September 5, 2026 through the early hours of Sunday, September 6 — a weekend stretch when registrants, lobbying firms and DOJ's National Security Division typically file little or nothing. A direct check of all three source systems this morning confirms that's exactly what happened: no new lobbying registrations, no quarterly activity or termination filings, no LD-203 lobbyist campaign-contribution reports, and no new or terminated Foreign Agents Registration Act registrants have posted since Saturday.

The Senate Office of Public Records' LDA API shows the most recent filings were posted Friday, September 4, 2026, before the window this edition covers began. The DOJ FARA e-file system's own report pages independently confirm zero new registrants and zero terminated registrants for the September 5–6 date range — the system's built-in record counts read "0" for both reports, not merely an empty page render.

Regular K Street and FARA coverage resumes in the next edition, once Monday filing activity begins posting.
