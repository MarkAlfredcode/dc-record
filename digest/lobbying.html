Top Story
K Street logs its quietest day in weeks — one plastics client closes its lobbying account, and nothing else moves
No new federal lobbying registrations, no reportable LD-203 campaign contributions, and no FARA registrant activity turned up anywhere in Monday's filings.

It was a nearly empty morning across all three of the disclosure systems this newsletter tracks. The Senate Office of Public Records logged zero new lobbying registrations, the LD-203 semiannual contribution reports that came in all checked the "no contributions" box, and the Justice Department's FARA e-file system recorded neither a new nor a terminated foreign-agent registrant.

The only lobbying-disclosure activity of note came from Government Relations Group, LLC, which filed both a second-quarter activity report and a third-quarter termination for its client Culture Organics, LLC within two minutes of each other. Culture Organics, which describes itself as a manufacturer of alternative plastic materials and products, had retained the firm to lobby on "economic incentives" tied to manufacturing and economic-development policy. Lobbyist Michael Kesti was named on both filings. The pairing — a catch-up quarterly report immediately followed by termination — suggests the firm closed out a lapsed account rather than an active engagement ending on schedule.

Six lobbyists and firms filed LD-203 semiannual contribution reports for the mid-year period, including Albanian American Civic League, Two Rivers LLC, Barral M Jorge Consultores Associados Ltda, and three separate filings from lobbyists at Williams Consulting Group, LLC. Every one of the six reported no contributions to disclose.
