Top Story
Ferring Pharmaceuticals discloses $165,000 in charity-event payments naming nine sitting lawmakers as honorees
Two LD-203 lobbyist contribution reports filed overnight by the drugmaker's registrant show payments to March of Dimes and The Congressional Award Foundation for events honoring members of both parties — the largest disclosed sums in today's LD-203 batch.
Ferring Pharmaceuticals, Inc. filed two LD-203 lobbyist contribution reports on September 7, 2026, disclosing a combined $165,000 in "Honorary Expenses" — payments to nonprofit organizations hosting events that named sitting members of Congress as honorees. Neither filing lists a registered lobbyist as the discloser; both are filed under the registrant itself.
The company's 2026 mid-year report (covering January 1 – June 30, 2026) discloses a $65,000 payment to the March of Dimes on April 8, 2026, for an event honoring Rep. Madeleine Dean, Rep. Sanford Bishop, Rep. Robert Aderholt, Rep. Bobby Scott, and Sen. Angus King Jr.
A second filing — a belatedly submitted 2025 mid-year report, also posted September 7 — discloses two more such payments from last year: $100,000 to The Congressional Award Foundation on May 6, 2025, honoring Rep. Debbie Dingell, Rep. Richard Hudson, Sen. Cynthia Lummis, Rep. Ritchie Torres, and Sen. Ben Ray Luján; and $65,000 to March of Dimes on April 15, 2025, honoring Sen. Rick Scott, Sen. Amy Klobuchar, Rep. Drew Ferguson, and Rep. Ruben Gallego. This newsletter did not independently verify why the 2025 report was filed late.

No new lobbying registrations overnight; a defense-consulting shop catches up on a stack of late quarterly reports
No new LD-1 registrations posted in this window.
