NextNav Registers Lobbying Team — Including a Former Member of Congress — to Work FCC on GPS Backup Rules
A new Senate lobbying registration shows the location-technology firm retaining a Nashville shop whose roster includes a former U.S. representative to lobby on GPS vulnerabilities and spectrum auction authority.

NextNav, LLC, a Virginia-based technology services company, registered Venture Government Strategies, LLC (filing under its former name, Hobart Hallaway & Quayle Ventures, LLC) to lobby on its behalf effective August 1, 2026, according to a new registration posted to the Senate's Lobbying Disclosure Act database.

The filing lists the lobbying activity as "issues and policymaking related to PNT [positioning, navigation and timing] systems, GPS vulnerabilities, and spectrum auction authority; FCC Rulemaking impacting GPS back-up and complement system." All three lobbyists assigned to the account disclose past government service: Ryann Kinney lists prior work as Director of Member Services for the U.S. House Committee on Appropriations and as an executive assistant to Congresswoman Rosa DeLauro; Benjamin E. Quayle discloses his covered position as a former Member of the U.S. House of Representatives; and Rashid Hallaway discloses prior service as a legislative assistant to then-U.S. Senator Evan Bayh.

The single issue area coded on the filing is Telecommunications. No lobbying income or expense figures were reported on the registration itself.

K Street
Five new registrations, a busy quarterly-report day, and a Speaker among the LD-203 recipients

Five new lobbying registrations posted overnight, spanning clinical-trial policy, pharmaceutical supply chains, higher-education appropriations, GPS/spectrum rulemaking and physician reimbursement. Separately, 23 additional quarterly activity reports, amendments and no-activity filings posted in the same window — mostly routine, dominated by two firms (Larkin Hoffman and Perry, White, Ross & Jacobson, LLC) working through their client rosters.

On the money side, five lobbyists/registrants filed LD-203 contribution reports disclosing 34 individual campaign contributions totaling $24,000, ranging from $50 to $3,500. The recipients include Speaker of the House Mike Johnson and Senate Minority Leader Chuck Schumer, among many others; nine other registrants and lobbyists filed LD-203s certifying no contributions to report.
