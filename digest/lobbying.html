A Quiet Day: No New K Street Registrations, No New Foreign Agents — But Lobbyist Cash Keeps Flowing to Senate Races
Neither the Senate's lobbying database nor DOJ's FARA system logged a single new registration overnight; the closest thing to news is a batch of lobbyist campaign-contribution disclosures touching two open Senate races.

The Senate Office of Public Records logged zero new lobbying registrations (LD-1 filings) with a posting date on or after yesterday, and DOJ's FARA e-file system logged zero new foreign-agent registrants and zero terminations in the same window — a rare doubly quiet night across both disclosure systems.

The activity that did post was routine: 19 quarterly activity reports, amendments and one termination from existing registrants (see K Street below), plus a batch of LD-203 lobbyist campaign-contribution filings. Among the latter, Federal City Strategies disclosed contributions to the Senate campaigns of Sherrod Brown in Ohio and Scott Colom in Mississippi, while Triangle Associates, Inc. disclosed a contribution to freshman Rep. Eugene Vindman of Virginia.

Full detail on both is in the K Street section below.
