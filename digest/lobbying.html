Top Story
Hospital association's PAC spreads $60,500 across both parties in overnight LD-203 disclosure
America's Essential Hospitals Political Action Committee reported 34 separate contributions, the only lobbyist campaign-finance filing with actual money behind it in today's batch.

America's Essential Hospitals, a national association representing safety-net hospitals, disclosed that its political action committee, the America's Essential Hospitals Political Action Committee, made 34 contributions totaling $60,500 to federal candidates, party caucuses and leadership PACs between January and June 2026. The semiannual LD-203 report was posted September 18, 2026.

The two largest checks, $5,000 each, went to Rep. Lori Trahan and Rep. David Valadao, both dated March 25, 2026. Other $2,500 contributions went to a bipartisan mix of sitting senators and representatives, including Sen. Ron Wyden, Sen. Shelley Moore Capito, Rep. Brett Guthrie, Rep. Juan Ciscomani and Rep. Yvette Clarke, along with $1,000-$2,000 contributions to members such as Sen. Josh Hawley, Sen. Susan Collins, Sen. Edward Markey and Sen. John Hickenlooper. The PAC also gave to party and caucus vehicles, including the Congressional Black Caucus, Future Forum, the New Democrat Coalition Action Fund, the Republican Governance Group, Aspire PAC and CHC BOLD PAC.

It was the only filing among 16 LD-203 contribution reports posted since yesterday to actually report money; the other 15 registrants and lobbyists checked the "no contributions" box for the period.

K Street
Eight new registrations, mostly trade and health policy

Eight lobbying registrations were posted overnight, clustered around trade/tariff policy and Medicare/Medicaid matters, plus a dozen more quarterly activity reports, terminations and amendments with nothing individually notable.

VantageKnight LLC registered to lobby for two sugar and agribusiness companies, Florida Crystals Corporation and Central Romana Corporation, both on U.S. trade and agriculture policy. Monument Advocacy picked up three new clients in one day, including ChartSpan Medical Technologies, Inc. on the CMS Physician Fee Schedule proposed rule for CY 2027, and PetFive Brands Global Holding Inc. on Brazil tariff relief. The Matador Group registered to represent law firm Roybal-Mack & Cordova, PC on matters tied to the Hermit's Peak/Calf Canyon fires.
