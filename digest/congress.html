Top Story

Rep. Jamie Raskin bill would put Trump-era "authoritarianism" in a permanent Smithsonian exhibit

The House met for two minutes with no votes, but 22 measures were introduced.

On a day when the House met for two minutes and held no votes, Rep. Jamie Raskin (D-MD-8) introduced H.R. 10765, which would direct the Secretary of the Smithsonian Institution to collect, preserve and display, in a permanent exhibition at the National Museum of American History, artifacts reflecting "the rise of governmental propaganda, corruption, and authoritarianism" during the two presidencies of Donald J. Trump.

The bill text defines the periods as January 20, 2017 through January 20, 2021, and January 20, 2025 through January 20, 2029. It was referred to the Committee on House Administration and, for a period to be determined, the Committee on Natural Resources. The record lists no cosponsors at introduction.

Also introduced: H.Res. 1618 by Rep. Josh Gottheimer (D-NJ-5), marking the third anniversary of the October 7, 2023, attacks on Israel, with more than 40 bipartisan cosponsors listed.
