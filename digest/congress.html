Top Story

Senate rejects bid to advance Affordable Care Act rule disapproval, 48-51

The Senate also voted down a push to force a Foreign Relations Committee discharge on Israel human-rights reporting, 47-51, on a day the House was out.

On Tuesday, September 29, the Senate voted 48 yeas to 51 nays (Vote No. 251) against the motion to proceed to S.J.Res. 197, a resolution of disapproval under the Congressional Review Act of the Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services rule on the 2027 Notice of Benefit and Payment Parameters and the Basic Health Program, according to the Congressional Record Daily Digest.

In a second record vote (Vote No. 252), the Senate voted 47 to 51 against discharging the Committee on Foreign Relations from further consideration of S.Res. 852, which requests information on Israel's human rights practices under section 502B(c) of the Foreign Assistance Act of 1961.

The Senate also continued on the motion to proceed to H.R. 9340, the Ratepayer Protection Act. A unanimous-consent agreement sets the cloture votes filed Monday to ripen at 11:30 a.m. on Wednesday, September 30. The House was not in session and next meets in a pro forma session at 11:30 a.m. on Thursday, October 1.

Senate-only day: two failed votes, seven House bills cleared, 45 measures introduced

The Senate convened at 10 a.m. and adjourned at 8:16 p.m. Two record votes were taken. Senators introduced 31 bills (S. 5573 through S. 5603), one joint resolution and 12 simple resolutions (S.Res. 917 through S.Res. 928). The House did not meet.

Among the more newsworthy: Sen. Brian Schatz proposed a constitutional amendment to abolish the electoral college, Sen. Ruben Gallego proposed a Select Committee on Artificial Intelligence, and Sen.
