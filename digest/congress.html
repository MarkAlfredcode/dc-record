Top Story

A discharge petition on immigration relief sits one signature short of 218

Discharge Petition No. 25 stands at 217 of 218 signatures while the House holds no votes.

Discharge Petition No. 25, which would bring H.R. 1589 to the House floor, has 217 of the 218 signatures it needs, one to go. The petition seeks consideration of the bill to authorize the cancellation of removal and adjustment of status of certain aliens, and for other purposes. The petition is tied to H.Res. 1414, sponsored by Sylvia R. Garcia.

No open discharge petition gained signers, was filed, or reached 218 since October 13, 2026. A second petition, No. 27, is two signatures short at 216 of 218. It seeks consideration of H.R. 185 to advance responsible policies (H.Res. 1430, sponsor Thomas Massie).

The House met on Thursday, October 15, from 10 a.m. to 11:04 a.m. and held no recorded votes. Its next scheduled meeting is a pro forma session at 10 a.m. on Monday, October 19.
