Top Story

Khanna and Massie file bipartisan war powers resolution on Houthi hostilities in Yemen

The concurrent resolution would direct the President to remove U.S. armed forces from unauthorized hostilities against the Houthis.

On Friday, October 9, Rep. Ro Khanna (D-CA-17) introduced H. Con. Res. 124, which invokes section 5(c) of the War Powers Resolution to direct the President to remove United States armed forces from hostilities against the Houthis in Yemen that Congress has not authorized. The Congressional Record lists Rep. Thomas Massie as the co-lead alongside Khanna, making it a cross-party effort.

The resolution was referred to the Committee on Foreign Affairs. It is one of 31 bills and resolutions introduced in the House on a day when both chambers met only briefly: the Senate held a pro forma session lasting 38 seconds, and the House adjourned at 12:32 p.m. until Tuesday.
