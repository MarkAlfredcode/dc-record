Top Story

House Democrats file West Bank sanctions bill as both chambers hold brief pro forma sessions

No votes were taken on Thursday, October 1, but 98 measures were introduced in the House of Representatives.

Rep. Joaquin Castro (D-TX-20) introduced H.R. 10642, a bill to impose sanctions with respect to settlements in the West Bank and Gaza. It lists more than 40 cosponsors and was referred to the Committee on Foreign Affairs and, in addition, to the Committees on the Judiciary, Financial Services, and Ways and Means.

The Senate met in pro forma session for about 35 seconds and the House of Representatives for about three minutes; the Daily Digest records no roll-call votes and no quorum calls in the House. Both chambers next meet in pro forma session on Monday, October 5, 2026.

Also notable: Rep. Jason Crow (D-CO-6) filed H. Res. 1596, a resolution providing for House consideration of H.R. 9899 on temporary protected status for Afghanistan, and Rep. Steve Cohen (D-TN-9) filed H.J. Res. 218, a constitutional amendment to abolish the electoral college. Neither has seen further action.
