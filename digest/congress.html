Top Story

House bill would bar candidates from betting on their own elections

Rep. Donald Davis introduced the measure during a brief Monday House session that produced no votes.

H.R. 10732, introduced by Rep. Donald G. Davis (D-NC-1), would amend title 52 of the United States Code to prohibit candidates for federal office from trading in contracts on the outcome of their own elections. It was referred to the Committee on House Administration.

The Congressional Record lists no cosponsors on the bill. The bill targets candidates trading in contracts on their own election outcomes, an ethics and money-in-politics question.

The House met Monday, October 5, 2026, approved the Journal, received a message from the Senate, and adjourned at 4:33 p.m. until 4 p.m. Tuesday. The Congressional Record shows no recorded votes in the House that day.

On the Record

Short House session, no votes; 41 bills and resolutions introduced

The House convened Monday, October 5, 2026, and adjourned at 4:33 p.m. until 4 p.m. Tuesday. The Congressional Record shows 30 House bills (H.R. 10721–10750) and 11 House resolutions (H.Res. 1606–1616) introduced. The Senate's only recorded action in the Congressional Record was adjournment until 1:30 p.m. Tuesday, October 6.

A message from the Senate read in the House announced Senate adoption of S. Res. 913, mourning the death of former Kansas Senator Nancy Kassebaum, and Senate passage of 21 bills awaiting House concurrence, among them S. 528 (reauthorizing the Great Lakes Restoration Initiative), S. 4395 (reauthorizing the Terrorism Risk Insurance Act of 2002) and S. 4668 (name, image and likeness protections for student athletes). The Senate also appointed Adam Klein of Texas to the Foreign Intelligence Surveillance Act Reform Commission. The House Clerk, Kevin F. McCumber, reported that H.R. 5345, on a single point of contact at the Social Security Administration for identity-theft victims, was presented to the President on September 29, 2026.
