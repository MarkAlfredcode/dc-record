Top Story

House Democrat Introduces Bill to Give Congress Power to End Military Exceptions to Posse Comitatus Act

Rep. Patrick Ryan (D-NY-18) is the lead sponsor, with Democratic colleagues, of a bill on domestic use of the armed forces; it was one of 97 measures filed in a pro forma House session on Thursday, October 1, 2026.

H.R. 10697 would amend titles 18 and 10 of the United States Code to give Congress authority to terminate exceptions to the prohibition on using the Army, Navy, Marine Corps, Air Force and Space Force as a posse comitatus (a force for domestic law enforcement), according to its official title on Congress.gov..

Also filed that day was H.Res. 1596, a rule resolution from Rep. Jason Crow (D-CO-6) that would provide for House consideration of H.R. 9899, the Afghanistan TPS Act of 2026, which would designate Afghanistan for temporary protected status. That underlying bill was referred to the House Committee on the Judiciary on July 23, 2026; the rule resolution was referred to the House Committee on the Rules on October 1.
