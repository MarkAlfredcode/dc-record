Top Story

Bill would bar federal candidates from betting on their own elections

Rep. Donald G. Davis introduced the measure during a House session that lasted three minutes.

Rep. Donald G. Davis (D-NC-1) introduced H.R. 10732, which would amend title 52 of the United States Code to prohibit candidates for federal office from trading in contracts on the outcome of their own elections. It was referred to the Committee on House Administration.

The Congressional Record text gives only the short title and referral, so the bill's penalties and enforcement mechanism are not described there. No cosponsors are listed for it.

Also among the 41 measures: Rep. William R. Keating's H.R. 10740 on temporary protected status for Ukrainians, which lists dozens of cosponsors from both parties, and Rep. Thomas Massie's H.R. 10744 exempting small-scale meat processors, with Representatives Pingree and Burlison listed as cosponsors in the Congressional Record.

Pro forma sessions in both chambers; 41 measures introduced in the House

Monday, October 5, 2026, was a pro forma day. The Senate met at 4:00 p.m. and adjourned within a minute until 1:30 p.m. Tuesday, October 6. The House met at 4:30 p.m. and adjourned at 4:33 p.m., with no roll call votes and no quorum calls.
