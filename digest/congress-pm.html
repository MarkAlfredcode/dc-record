Top Story

Sanctions on West Bank and Gaza settlements bill draws more than 40 House cosponsors

On a day with no votes, the House received 98 new measures, led by a sanctions bill from Rep. Joaquin Castro.

H.R. 10642, introduced by Rep. Joaquin Castro (D-TX-20), would provide for the imposition of sanctions with respect to settlements in the West Bank and Gaza. The Congressional Record lists more than 40 cosponsors on the measure.

It was referred to the Committee on Foreign Affairs, and in addition to the Committees on the Judiciary, Financial Services, and Ways and Means. No committee action or floor scheduling was recorded.

Also notable among the day's introductions: H.R. 10719 from Rep. George Whitesides (D-CA-27) would set age-appropriate design standards for artificial intelligence chatbots accessed by minors, and H.Res. 1596 from Rep. Jason Crow (D-CO-6) would provide for House consideration of H.R. 9899, a bill to designate Afghanistan for temporary protected status.

Read the bill: H.R. 10642 on Congress.gov
