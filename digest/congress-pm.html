Top Story

Khanna and Massie file war powers resolution to end U.S. hostilities against Houthis in Yemen

The House met for two minutes of business on Friday, but a bipartisan war powers measure was among 31 items introduced.

Rep. Ro Khanna (D-CA-17), joined by Rep. Thomas Massie, introduced H.Con.Res. 124, a concurrent resolution directing the President, pursuant to section 5(c) of the War Powers Resolution, to remove United States Armed Forces from hostilities against the Houthis in Yemen that have not been authorized by Congress. It was referred to the Committee on Foreign Affairs.

The resolution invokes the War Powers Resolution’s provision on removing forces from unauthorized hostilities. The Congressional Record text shows only the referral; no floor vote or scheduling was recorded on Friday, when the House adjourned until 1:30 p.m. on Tuesday, October 13, 2026.

No new Congressional Record issue has been published since this one (first covered 2026-10-10); the summary below is carried over.

Pro forma sessions in both chambers; 31 House measures introduced

Both chambers held brief sessions on Friday, October 9, 2026, with no votes. The Senate met in pro forma session at 1:30 p.m. and adjourned 38 seconds later until 3:30 p.m. on Tuesday, October 13. The House met at 12:30 p.m. and adjourned at 12:32 p.m. until 1:30 p.m. on Tuesday, October 13. No committee hearings were held, and the Record lists no Senate bills.

The House received 27 bills, one concurrent resolution and three simple resolutions. Beyond the war powers measure, items drawing attention include a bipartisan 340B rebate limit, a Federal Trade Commission whistleblower bill, a ban on holding certain whales and dolphins for public display, and a resolution on Columbus monuments.
