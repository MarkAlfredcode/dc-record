Top Story
Dentons lobbyist writes DCCC a $10,000 check on his LD-203
A single lobbyist's mid-year contribution report shows $15,000 flowing to Democratic committees and candidates, anchored by a five-figure gift to the party's House campaign arm.

Joe Crowley, a lobbyist registered with Dentons US LLP, disclosed $15,000 in personal political contributions on an LD-203 lobbyist contribution report posted overnight, covering the January-through-June 2026 reporting period. The single largest item is a $10,000 contribution to the Democratic Congressional Campaign Committee (DCCC) made on March 27, 2026.

The same filing lists four more contributions of $1,000 to $2,000 apiece: $2,000 to Dorothy McAuliffe for Congress, and $1,000 each to Stand with Sanchez (the campaign committee for Rep. Linda Sánchez), Robin Kelly for Senate (Rep. Robin Kelly's Illinois Senate campaign) and Joyce Beatty for Congress. Dentons US LLP's House registrant ID is 33854 and its Senate registrant ID is 36105.

LD-203s only require lobbyists to disclose contributions above certain thresholds, not to explain the reasoning behind them, so the filing itself doesn't say why these particular committees. But a $10,000 single-line contribution to a national party committee is unusually large for one of these reports, which is why it's the standout of today's disclosures.
