Top Story
Firm registers to lobby for repatriation of Venezuelan-seized U.S. property
Thorn Run Partners filed a new registration on behalf of a Caribbean hospitality company seeking the return of American property seized in Venezuela.

Thorn Run Partners registered on September 11 to lobby on behalf of Vientos del Caribe, a hotel and resort company, on "repatriation of seized American property in Venezuela." The new-client registration lists the general issue area as Real Estate/Land Use/Conservation, though the underlying subject matter — recovering U.S.-owned assets seized by the Venezuelan government — sits at the intersection of property rights and U.S.-Venezuela foreign policy.

The filing does not name the specific property or provide further detail beyond the one-line description of lobbying activity, and no other client or issue area is listed on the registration.

It was otherwise a light day for new registrations: TAE Technologies, Inc. registered to lobby in-house on fusion energy policy, and Brownstein Hyatt Farber Schreck, LLP registered on behalf of Empower Mental Health, a telehealth psychiatric care provider, on veteran healthcare eligibility issues.
