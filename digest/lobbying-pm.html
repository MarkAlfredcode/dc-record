Hospital Lobby's PAC Spread $60,500 Across Both Parties in a Late-Filed Mid-Year Disclosure
America's Essential Hospitals' political arm gave to Senate incumbents in both parties and a string of caucus PACs, in a report covering the first half of the year that didn't post until mid-September.

America's Essential Hospitals, a trade association representing safety-net hospitals, filed a Mid-Year LD-203 report on September 18 disclosing that its political action committee, the America's Essential Hospitals Political Action Committee, made 34 separate contributions totaling $60,500 to federal candidates and party committees. The report covers activity from January through June 2026 — meaning the earliest contributions listed were disclosed roughly eight months after the fact.

The PAC's giving crossed party lines and both chambers. On the Senate side it backed Republicans Susan Collins of Maine, Shelley Moore Capito of West Virginia and Jon Husted of Ohio alongside Democrats Ron Wyden of Oregon, John Hickenlooper of Colorado and Ed Markey of Massachusetts. House recipients included Brett Guthrie, the Republican chair of the Energy and Commerce Committee, along with Lori Trahan, David Valadao, Yvette Clarke and Angie Craig, among others.

The PAC also gave to a mix of ideological and caucus-aligned committees, including the Congressional Black Caucus, the New Democrat Coalition Action Fund, the Republican Governance Group, CHC BOLD PAC and Aspire PAC — a bipartisan spread typical of a hospital-sector PAC rather than one tied to a single live legislative fight.

Eight New Registrations Lead With Trade and Health Policy

Eight new lobbying registrations posted to the Senate's database in the past day, alongside 14 additional quarterly activity reports, terminations and amendments. Trade and agriculture issues led the new registrations, with two sugar-industry filings tied to ongoing trade policy, plus health care, housing and disaster-recovery work.

Separately, 16 LD-203 contribution reports posted. Fifteen reported no contributions for the period; the only one with actual contributions came from America's Essential Hospitals Political Action Committee, detailed in today's Top Story above and broken out in full below.
