No New K Street Registrations Today, But a Lobbyist's LD-203 Discloses Checks to Three House Members
In an otherwise slow 24 hours for lobbying paperwork, the only filing with real news is a campaign-contribution disclosure.

The Senate Office of Public Records logged zero new lobbying registrations (LD-1s) between September 12 and September 13, 2026, and only six other Lobbying Disclosure Act filings posted in that window — all routine quarterly activity reports, amendments, or terminations from existing registrants.

The most substantive document to post was a semiannual LD-203 contributions report from Philip Andrew Blalock, a registered lobbyist with Triangle Associates, Inc. The filing discloses five personal ("self") campaign contributions totaling $6,500 made between January and June 2026: $1,000 and $500 to Rep. Josh Riley of New York, $3,500 and $1,000 to Rep. David Rouzer of North Carolina, and $500 to a payee the filing itself lists as "Eugene Vindma" — an apparent truncation in the underlying record, most likely referring to Rep. Eugene Vindman of Virginia, though the filing does not spell the full name.

The other six LD-203 filings that posted in the window — from Frontline Public Strategies, Inc., Center Hill Solutions, and Triangle Associates itself — all checked "no contributions" for the reporting period.
