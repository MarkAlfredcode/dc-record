Top Story
A quiet weekend on K Street: no new registrations, no lobbyist contributions
The only substantive disclosure was a Kansas state agency's sweeping federal lobbying agenda, filed a quarter late.

The Senate Office of Public Records logged no new Lobbying Disclosure Act registrations and no LD-203 lobbyist campaign contribution reports in the past day. DOJ's FARA e-file system likewise recorded zero new or terminated foreign-agent registrants. In all, just three Lobbying Disclosure Act filings posted.

The most substantive of the three came from Viking Navigation LLC, which filed a second-quarter activity report on behalf of the Kansas Department of Commerce. The filing discloses federal lobbying across 16 separate issue areas, including opposition to Medicaid block grants and rural hospital cuts, support for a Rural Health Transformation Program grant award, implementation of broadband provisions in the Bipartisan Infrastructure Law, Farm Bill provisions on rural broadband and crop insurance, and coordination with FEMA on disaster declarations affecting Kansas counties.

The other two filings were routine: Hollinger Group Consulting posted a first-quarter report for client Agile Patriot on military construction issues, six months after the quarter it covers, and Turquoise Policy Group filed a third-quarter termination-with-no-activity notice for former client Coterra.
