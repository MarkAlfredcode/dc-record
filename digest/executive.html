CBP's border build-out: $432 million in new construction and recruitment contracts this week, plus a new drone solicitation

Three new Border Patrol facilities, an $86 million recruiting-ad task order, and a call for a new unmanned aerial system all surfaced in the same window.

U.S. Customs and Border Protection posted the single largest concentration of new contract activity in this window outside of one VA task order: three design-build construction contracts for new Border Patrol facilities, all awarded the same day, September 25, 2026. Hensel Phelps Construction Co. won $150,000,000 to design and build a new Border Patrol Station in Calexico, California. Southwest Valley Constructors Co. won two more — $107,066,852 for a new checkpoint facility in Las Cruces, New Mexico, and $89,139,948 for a new checkpoint facility in Wellton, Arizona. Together the three total $346,206,800 in new border-facility construction in a single day.

CBP also awarded an $86,100,000 task order to MARCOM Group, Inc. that same day for marketing and advertising services aimed at recruiting CBP law enforcement officers, bringing the agency's combined new-contract total for the window — construction plus recruitment — to $432,306,800.

Separately, in this window's SAM.gov solicitation data, CBP's Mission Support Contracting Division posted a "Controlled Solicitation Availability for UAS Platform Requirement," signaling the agency is in the market for a new unmanned aerial system for border operations. The notice does not specify a dollar value or technical requirements beyond that title.

Taken together, the three procurement channels — physical infrastructure, staff recruitment, and new enforcement technology — all point toward continued expansion of CBP's border enforcement footprint this fiscal year.
