Executive Branch Edition
Top Story

White House issues five proclamations escalating the tariff and import fight with Canada

Two documents widen existing duties on Canadian motor vehicles and alcoholic beverages while three others impose outright import exclusions on motor vehicles, dairy, and alcoholic beverages.

The Executive Office of the President published five separate Presidential Documents on September 14, 2026, all citing "continued discrimination against the commerce of the United States" by Canada. Two modify the scope of products already subject to added duties — one covering motor vehicles, one covering alcoholic beverages. The other three go further, excluding certain Canadian motor vehicles, dairy products, and alcoholic beverages from importation into the United States outright. The Federal Register's public feed carries only the titles of these documents; no abstracts were published alongside them, so the specific product lists and effective dates are only available in the full proclamation text linked below.

Taken together, the five actions mark a broadening of the administration's trade posture toward Canada across three separate product categories in a single day, rather than an isolated tariff adjustment. A sixth Presidential Document, published September 15, proclaims Patriot Day 2026 marking the 25th anniversary of the September 11 terrorist attacks and is unrelated to the trade actions.

Elsewhere in today's Federal Register, the Office of Personnel Management corrected a reduction-in-force rule that took effect September 2 after an August 3 amendment inadvertently deleted two paragraphs from the regulatory definition of "displaced," and separately issued an interim rule expanding agencies' ability to share competitive and excepted-service hiring certificates across agencies.

USAspending Contracts

Two new VA elevator-modernization awards worth $20.1 million go to the same small contractor

The 25-day trailing sweep of USAspending.gov turned up just two new contract actions meeting our $2 million reporting threshold, both awarded to the same mechanical-systems contractor for elevator overhauls at VA medical centers on opposite coasts on the same day. A mid-run timeout on one results page (see footer) means today's total may understate the full sweep's award volume.

VA Elevator Modernization: West Haven and Houston Medical Centers

The Department of Veterans Affairs awarded Elevated Technologies Services, LLC $15,183,170 on September 13, 2026 for elevator modernization (Project 689-26-760) at the West Haven, Connecticut VA Medical Center, and a separate $4,893,200 the same day for elevator modernization (Project 580-26-760) at the Houston, Texas VA medical center. Both awards fall under the same product/service classification for maintenance, repair and rebuild of service and trade equipment, and both went to the same recipient on the same action date, pointing to a coordinated VA-wide elevator-replacement effort rather than two unrelated procurements. Combined, the two awards total just over $20.1 million; neither underlying award record lists a distinct competed-solicitation number beyond the internal project code.

During the preceding 12 months, Blanche served as Acting Librarian of Congress (May–December 2025) and Acting Attorney General (April 2026–present); he has made his law firm inactive.
