Top Story
All three sitting Election Assistance Commission commissioners filed termination notices within a two-day span
Christy A. McCormick, Benjamin W. Hovland and Thomas Hicks each filed an OGE termination filing Sept. 8-9; the agency's ethics record does not say why.

The Office of Government Ethics's public filing database shows termination notices for Christy A. McCormick, Benjamin W. Hovland and Thomas Hicks — all three commissioners of the U.S. Election Assistance Commission tracked as active in OGE's system — logged within a 24-hour span on Sept. 8 and 9, 2026. The Election Assistance Commission is a small independent agency, created after the 2000 election, that certifies voting systems and distributes federal election-security funding to states.

OGE's database records only the fact and date of each termination filing, not a reason; the entries do not indicate whether the departures reflect resignations, expired terms, or removals. Hicks's filing additionally carries OGE's "Pending Final OGE Disposition" tag, meaning the agency has not yet finalized that record. Each filing is request-gated rather than directly viewable — DC Record has requested the underlying documents.

Elsewhere today: a Department of Homeland Security sole-source software award, a Consumer Financial Protection Bureau contractor bridge order, and a supplemental EPA/Army proposal on the scope of the Clean Water Act. Full rundown below.

The Federal Register
EPA and the Army Corps reopen comment on redefining "waters of the United States"; no executive orders published

No presidential documents (executive orders, proclamations or memoranda) published Sept. 8-9. Twelve final rules published, mostly routine Coast Guard safety zones, EPA state-implementation-plan approvals and a Treasury/IRS rule finalizing the new passenger-vehicle-loan interest deduction. Of 10 proposed rules published in the window, three stand out for policy significance (below); the other seven — airworthiness directives, a Postal Service label discontinuation, a watermelon-marketing-plan withdrawal and a routine North Dakota air-plan reconsideration — are omitted as routine.

Notable proposed rules
  • Updated Definition of “Waters of the United States” — Environmental Protection Agency / Department of the Army (Engineers Corps). Supplemental notice seeking additional comment on the agencies' November 2025 proposal to redefine which waters are federally regulated under the Clean Water Act, following the Supreme Court's 2023 Sackett decision.
  • National Petroleum Reserve in Alaska Production Site Development — Bureau of Land Management. Proposes a streamlined, pre-defined-criteria permitting pathway for qualifying oil and gas production sites and rights-of-way in the National Petroleum Reserve in Alaska.
  • Unleashing Unlicensed Spectrum for Direct-to-Device — Federal Communications Commission. Proposes to let certain unlicensed devices communicate directly with satellites on a non-interference basis, adding satellite allocations in specific unlicensed bands.
