Top Story
OGE Filings Show Three of Four Election Assistance Commission Commissioners Terminated Within 48 Hours
Termination records for Christy A. McCormick, Benjamin W. Hovland, and Thomas Hicks all posted to the Office of Government Ethics's filing index in a single two-day window, leaving the independent, statutorily bipartisan agency with only one commissioner of record in this data.

Filings posted to the U.S. Office of Government Ethics's (OGE) public index show termination records for three of the four sitting commissioners of the U.S. Election Assistance Commission (EAC), the small independent agency that certifies voting systems, accredits testing labs, and disburses federal election-security grants to states. Christy A. McCormick and Benjamin W. Hovland both have terminations logged with a document date of September 9, 2026; Thomas Hicks's termination, logged a day earlier on September 8, still carries OGE's "(Pending Final OGE Disposition)" tag — meaning that record has not yet been finalized by the agency.

OGE's index records only the fact and date of a termination filing, not the reason for it. The underlying filings are request-gated rather than directly viewable, so it was not possible to confirm from this data whether the departures reflect resignations, expired terms, or removals from office.

By statute, the EAC is structured to have four commissioners, with no more than two from the same political party, specifically to insulate election administration from single-party control. Three departures logged within a single two-day window is a significant reduction in the commission's voting membership regardless of cause, and worth tracking as OGE finalizes the pending record and as any nominations to refill the seats surface in future editions.
